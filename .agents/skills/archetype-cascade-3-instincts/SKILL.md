# Archetype Cascade — Step 3: Actionable Instincts

This is **Step 3** in the 6-step cascading pipeline. It takes the tensions, vices, and virtues established in the **Pitch** (Step 1) and **Origins** (Step 2) and crystallizes them into **4 to 6 active behavioral instincts**.

```
  Step 1: Pitch & Community Stake
                   │
                   ▼
  Step 2: Triad of Backgrounds
                   │ (Passes Seed & Origins)
                   ▼
  Step 3: Actionable Instincts ◄── [ YOU ARE HERE ]
                   │ (Outputs Instinct Menu)
                   ▼
  Step 4: Special Possessions & Trade
                   │
                   ▼
  Step 5: Worldbuilding & Connections
                   │
                   ▼
  Step 6: Playbook Assembly
```

---

## 1. Input Requirements (From Steps 1 & 2)

Inspect the outputs of Steps 1 and 2 to identify:
1. **The Core Moral Temptation:** What flaw repeatedly threatens the character’s survival or standing? (e.g., greed, anger, curiosity, isolation).
2. **The Community Bond:** What virtue keeps them sacrificing for their neighbors? (e.g., honor, conscience, peace, guardianship).

---

## 2. Transformation Procedure & Design Rules

Instincts in *Stonetop* are **not alignments** and **not passive traits**. They are **actionable behavioral impulses** tied to the End-of-Session move:
> *"Did I demonstrate my instinct in play, or struggle against it? If yes, mark 1 XP."*

### The 3 Rules of Instinct Crafting:
1. **Infinitive Action Formula:** Always write *To [verb] [consequence/object]*. Never write an adjective like *Angry* or *Brave*.
2. **The Trouble Test:** The impulse must tempt the character into suboptimal, dramatic, or complicating actions.
3. **The Struggle Test:** The character must be able to struggle *against* it. A hero with *Violence* struggles to stay peaceful; a hero with *Peace* struggles against bloodthirsty urges.

---

## 3. The 5 Archetypal Instinct Categories

Select and adapt 4 to 6 instincts covering these dynamics:

1. **The Moral Drive:**
   * *CONSCIENCE:* To feel guilty, to try to do right.
   * *PEACE:* To avoid (further) bloodshed or violence.
   * *DUTY:* To put the village's needs above personal desire.
2. **The Status / Ego Drive:**
   * *PRIDE:* To maintain your dignity, to demand respect.
   * *PRESTIGE:* To impress others, to build a name for yourself.
   * *AUTHORITY:* To command obedience and maintain order.
3. **The Self-Serving Drive:**
   * *COMFORT:* To enjoy yourself and avoid hardship.
   * *FREEDOM:* To chafe against rules, expectations, obligations.
   * *SURVIVAL:* To keep yourself safe, no matter the cost.
4. **The Aggressive / Action Drive:**
   * *VIOLENCE:* To solve problems by force.
   * *RECKLESSNESS:* To act without thought to the consequences.
   * *VENGEANCE:* To repay every slight with interest.
5. **The Playbook-Specific Drive:**
   * *TRICKERY:* To deceive, misdirect, outthink (Fox).
   * *SECRETS:* To uncover that which was buried or forbidden (Seeker).
   * *FERALITY:* To cast off human manners and embrace the beast (Ranger).
