# Archetype Cascade — Step 6: Playbook Assembly & Final Polish

This is **Step 6 (The Final Step)** in the 6-step cascading pipeline. It gathers the outputs from Steps 1 through 5, assigns the combat chassis and moves, and compiles the entire archetype into a standardized, ready-to-play **4-Page Markdown Playbook**.

```
  Step 1: Pitch & Community Stake
                   │
  Step 2: Triad of Backgrounds
                   │
  Step 3: Actionable Instincts
                   │
  Step 4: Special Possessions & Trade
                   │
  Step 5: Worldbuilding & Connections
                   │ (Passes Full Inflow Bundle)
                   ▼
  Step 6: Playbook Assembly ◄── [ YOU ARE HERE: FINAL OUTPUT ]
```

---

## 1. Input Requirements (From Steps 1–5)

Ensure all previous cascade outputs are in hand:
* **Step 1:** Title, Mythic Role, Central Tension, Front-Page Pitch.
* **Step 2:** The 3 Backgrounds with Mechanical Rule Perks and Round 1 Questions.
* **Step 3:** The 5 Instinct Choices with active infinitive verbs.
* **Step 4:** The 5–6 Special Possessions with trade tools and tags.
* **Step 5:** The Worldbuilding move, 4 NPC Connections, and 4 PC Bonds.

---

## 2. Transformation Procedure: The Mechanical Chassis

Assign the mechanical values matching the archetype's combat role:

| Archetype Role | Damage Die | Base HP | Starting Armor | Base Load |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Frontline / Heavy** | `1d10` | `20 + CON` | 1 | 9 + STR |
| **Skirmisher / Hunter** | `1d8` | `16 + CON` | 0–1 | 8 + STR |
| **Specialist / Mystic** | `1d6` | `14 + CON` | 0 | 7 + STR |
| **Scholar / Fated** | `1d4` | `12 + CON` | 0 | 6 + STR |

### Design Starting & Advanced Moves:
* **Starting Moves (2–3):** Proactive, signature abilities that make this class immediately feel unique in combat or exploration.
* **Advanced Moves (4–6 for Levels 2–5):** Upgrades, tactical enhancements, new options.
* **Master Moves (2–4 for Levels 6–10):** High-tier legendary moves requiring specific level 2–5 prerequisites.
