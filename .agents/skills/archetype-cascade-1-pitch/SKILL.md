# Archetype Cascade — Step 1: The Pitch & Community Stake

This is **Step 1** in the 6-step cascading archetype pipeline. It establishes the archetype’s core voice, folkloric premise, and the raw tension of why an isolated village tolerates, needs, or fears them.

## 1. Input Requirements

Before running this step, ensure you have:
1. **The Core Concept:** A 1-sentence premise or character inspiration (e.g., *"A swamp-dwelling herbalist who brews strange draughts"*, *"A scarred battle-skald from the icy north"*).
2. **The Setting Baseline:** The tone of *Stonetop* or *ASH*—Iron Age survival, hearth fantasy, low-magic grit, communal inter-dependence.

---

## 2. Transformation Procedure

Follow this 3-part cadence to write the pitch:

### Part A: The Parable or Harsh Truism (2–3 sentences)
State an elder saying, folk tale, or brutal law of nature that ordinary villagers believe.
* *Example (Fox):* The elders tell a story about Fox who knows many tricks and Hedgehog who curls up...
* *Example (Heavy):* These are good people. Hard-working, honest. They look out for each other...

### Part B: The "You" Turn (1–2 sentences)
Break the cozy folk illusion and pivot directly to the player character in the second person ("you").
* *Example (Fox):* *But you’re not that Fox, and this is no story. You want that Hedgehog? Go get a knife.*
* *Example (Heavy):* *But sometimes, looking out for each other ain’t enough. Sometimes, good people need someone to stick up for them. Someone who’s not afraid to get a little bloody.*

### Part C: The Community Stake (1 sentence)
Define the inescapable burden that tethers this dangerous or strange person to the common hearth.
* *Example:* *Yeah, someone like you.*

---

## 3. Mandatory Output Format (The Inflow for Step 2)

```markdown
### Archetype Seed
* **Title:** The [Archetype Name]
* **Core Mythic Role:** [2–3 words defining their community niche]
* **The Central Tension:** [Their blessing vs. their social burden]
* **Front-Page Pitch:**
> *[Insert Parable]*
> *[Insert The "You" Turn]*
> *[Insert Community Stake]*
```

---

## 4. Cascading Hand-Off

Once this output is generated, **immediately pass the Archetype Seed to `archetype-cascade-2-backgrounds`** to branch this concept into its three contrasting origin paths.
