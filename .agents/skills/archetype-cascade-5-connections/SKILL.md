# Archetype Cascade — Step 5: Worldbuilding & Communal Connections

This is **Step 5** in the 6-step cascading pipeline. It creates the **back-page prompts** that bind the character to the residents of Stonetop and to their fellow adventurers during the Introduction Loop of Session 1.

```
  Step 1: Pitch & Community Stake
                   │
                   ▼
  Step 2: Triad of Backgrounds
                   │
                   ▼
  Step 3: Actionable Instincts
                   │
                   ▼
  Step 4: Special Possessions & Trade
                   │ (Passes Steps 1–4)
                   ▼
  Step 5: Worldbuilding & Connections ◄── [ YOU ARE HERE ]
                   │ (Outputs Back-Page Engine)
                   ▼
  Step 6: Playbook Assembly
```

---

## 1. Input Requirements (From Steps 1–4)

Examine the established:
1. **Mythic Identity & Sins (Steps 1 & 2):** What legendary deeds or terrible mistakes did this character make in the region?
2. **Possessions & Livelihood (Step 4):** Who in the village relies on them? Who resents their trade?
3. **Instincts (Step 3):** What interpersonal friction naturally sparks between this PC and the other heroes?

---

## 2. Transformation Procedure

Generate the three back-page relational engines:

### 1. The Worldbuilding Move (Introductions Round 3)
A modular prompt generator on the left column of Page 4 that lets the player author local history or regional landmarks:
* *Fox Pattern (Tall Tales):* *"There was that time you... [poked in barrows/stole from a wizard/got lost in the Great Wood]... and ended up... [face to face with a ghost/with a sack of silver]... but all you've got left to show for it is... [a nasty scar/this strange key]."*
* *Heavy Pattern (A History of Violence):* *"Everyone talks about the time you... [killed that hagr/drove off a drake]... but folks are less keen to discuss... [the look in your eye/those hard cases who came looking for you]... and what keeps you up at night is... [the worry they'll learn the truth]."*
* *Ranger Pattern (Something Wicked):* Local signs of corruption or beast migrations in the wild.
* *Judge Pattern (The Lawkeeper & The Chronicle):* The most recent dispute ruled on; the nature of the holy records.

### 2. NPC Connection Questions (Introductions Rounds 4 & 5)
Draft **4 tailored questions** that the player answers by naming specific villagers in Stonetop:
* 1 Kinship / Family Question (*"Who is your closest kin?"*)
* 1 Romantic / Heart Question (*"Who holds the reins to your heart?"* / *"Who is your betrothed?"*)
* 1 Duty / Protection Question (*"Who most needs your protection?"* / *"Whose respect means the world to you?"*)
* 1 Debt / Friction Question (*"To whom do you owe a debt that cannot be repaid?"* / *"Whose forgiveness do you strive to earn?"*)

### 3. PC Connection Questions (Introductions Rounds 6 & 7)
Draft **4 "Which one of you...?" questions** that the player poses aloud to the table for another player character to volunteer for:
* 1 Shared Misadventure Question (*"Which one of you joined me on my latest reckless scheme?"*)
* 1 Lifesaving / Debt Question (*"Which one of you dragged me home bleeding and unconscious?"*)
* 1 Moral Anchor / Friction Question (*"Which one of you has stayed my hand when I was seeing red?"*)
* 1 Skepticism / Distrust Question (*"Which one of you trusts me not one bit?"*)

---

## 3. Mandatory Output Format (The Inflow for Step 6)

```markdown
### Back-Page Lore & Connections

#### Worldbuilding Prompt: [Title of Move]
* *Format:* [Sentence completion choices establishing past deeds, consequences, and lasting mementos]

#### NPC Connections (Rounds 4 & 5 — Choose 1 to 2)
1. [Kinship Question]
2. [Romantic / Heart Question]
3. [Duty / Protection Question]
4. [Debt / Friction Question]

#### PC Connections (Rounds 6 & 7 — Ask the Table)
1. Which one of you [shared misadventure / scheme]?
2. Which one of you [saved my life / dragged me back]?
3. Which one of you [knows my secret / stayed my hand]?
4. Which one of you [distrusts me / challenges my word]?
```
