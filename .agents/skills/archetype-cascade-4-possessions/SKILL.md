# Archetype Cascade — Step 4: Special Possessions & The Hearth Economy

This is **Step 4** in the 6-step cascading pipeline. It anchors the archetype directly into the subsistence economy of Stonetop by designing **5 to 6 distinct Special Possessions** (from which the player picks 2) that establish their trade, craft, and value to the community.

## 1. Input Requirements (From Steps 1–3)

Review the character’s:
1. **Community Role (Step 1):** What mundane or sacred services does the village expect from this person?
2. **Background Contexts (Step 2):** Did they learn a craft, leave behind contraband, or wield ancient relics?
3. **Behavioral Impulses (Step 3):** Do their possessions support or collide with their instincts?

---

## 2. Transformation Procedure: The "Earn Your Keep" Test

Every possession must pass this test:
* *When winter sets in and no one leaves the palisade for three months, what does the character do with these possessions to earn their daily bowl of barley from the granary?*

### The 5 Essential Possession Types:
Design a diverse menu of 5 to 6 items incorporating:
1. **The Production Workshop:** A stationary village fixture (e.g. *Smithy, Tannery, Distillery, Glassworks, Pottery*).
2. **The Field / Craft Kit:** Portable tools carried on expeditions (e.g. *Burglar's kit, Carpenter's tools, Husbandry tools, Chirurgeon's tools*).
3. **The Agricultural Asset:** Animals or domestic plots (e.g. *Goat herd, Apiary/beehives, Herb garden, Hunting hounds*).
4. **The Martial Arsenal:** Distinctive weapons or armor with tags (e.g. *Weapons of war, Boiled leather cuirass, Tower shield, Crossbow*).
5. **The Wildcard / Trade Curio:** An oddity, contraband cache, or external trade contact (e.g. *Hidden stash, Foreign trade goods, Scribe's inks, Mummer's kit*).

---

## 3. Formatting Rules & Tag System

Every item must include its concrete contents and appropriate tags:
* **Load / Weight:** Mark whether it takes `1 weight` (small pack), `2 weight` (heavy gear), or is unburdened / stationary.
* **Range Tags:** *hand* < *close* < *reach* < *near* < *far*.
* **Sensory / Fictional Tags:** *messy, forceful, piercing, crude, reload, warm, area*.
* **Mechanical Hook:** Consumable uses (e.g. *3 uses*), advantage on specific rolls, or armor bonuses.

---

## 4. Mandatory Output Format (The Inflow for Step 5)

```markdown
### Special Possessions (Choose 2)

* **[Workshop / Trade] (or access to it):** [Concrete tools, ingots/materials, barrels/containers]. *[Mechanical effect or trade value].*
* **[Field Kit]:** [Picks, files, lanterns, consumables, load tag]. *[Expedition utility effect].*
* **[Agricultural / Animal Asset]:** [Livestock, feed, bridles, plots]. *[Grants advantage on specific animal or survival moves].*
* **[Martial Arsenal]:** Choose up to 2:
  * *[Weapon 1] (range, tags, damage bonus)*
  * *[Weapon 2] (range, tags, piercing)*
* **[Social / Barter Asset]:** [Consumable uses, value, leverage]. *[Grants advantage on Persuade or trading].*
```
