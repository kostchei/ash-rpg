# Archetype Cascade — Step 2: The Triad of Origins

This is **Step 2** in the 6-step cascading pipeline. It takes the **Archetype Seed** from Step 1 and branches it into three distinct origin trajectories that determine how the character entered the playbook, what mechanical rule modifications they receive, and what questions the GM will ask them in Round 1 of play.

```
  Step 1: Pitch & Community Stake
                   │ (Passes Archetype Seed)
                   ▼
  Step 2: Triad of Backgrounds ◄── [ YOU ARE HERE ]
                   │ (Outputs Origins & Round 1 Questions)
                   ▼
  Step 3: Actionable Instincts
                   │
                   ▼
  Step 4: Special Possessions & Trade
                   │
                   ▼
  Step 5: Worldbuilding & Connections
                   │
                   ▼
  Step 6: Playbook Assembly
```

---

## 1. Input Requirements (From Step 1)

Requires the **Archetype Seed** containing:
* **Title:** The archetype name.
* **Core Mythic Role:** The community niche.
* **Central Tension:** The blessing vs. burden.

---

## 2. Transformation Procedure

Generate exactly **three contrasting paths**:

### Path 1: The Sanctioned Origin (High Legitimacy)
* Trained formally, chosen by elders, hereditary, or appointed by local authority.
* *Examples:* *Initiate* (Blessed), *Sheriff* (Heavy), *Legacy* (Judge), *Scion* (Marshal).

### Path 2: The Scarred Origin (High Friction)
* Violent past, criminal enterprise, broken oaths, debt, or dark reputation.
* *Examples:* *A Life of Crime* (Fox), *Blood-soaked Past* (Heavy), *Penitent* (Marshal).

### Path 3: The Touched Origin (The Anomaly)
* Strange calling, wild wanderer, god-struck, raised in the deep woods, prodigal return.
* *Examples:* *Vessel* (Blessed), *Storm-marked* (Heavy), *Prodigal Returned* (Fox).

---

## 3. Mandatory Components per Origin

For each of the three origins, specify:

1. **Narrative Paragraph:** 2–3 sentences defining how they arrived in this station.
2. **Mechanical Rule Modification (Choose 1):**
   * *Stat Substitution:* Swap the rolled stat on a core move (e.g. roll +INT to Seek Insight, or +STR to Persuade with threats).
   * *Conditional Bonus:* Grant +1d4 damage, or add a custom question to an investigation move.
   * *Bonus Asset:* Grant an extra starting move, specialized tool, or contact move.
   * *Arcanum Access:* Grant a specific minor or major arcanum.
3. **3 to 5 Loaded Round 1 Questions:**
   * Must assume drama and trouble already exist.
   * Pull from the 5 Narrative Levers: *Community Anchor, Source of Authority, Social Friction, Lore Injection, Asymmetric Relationships*.
