# Equipment, Encumbrance & Services

> **"A warrior’s sword is only as reliable as the rations in their pack, the oil in their lantern, and the hireling holding the torch."**

---

## 🎒 Slot-Based Inventory System

ASH eliminates tedious pound-by-pound weight math with a clean, tactical **Gear Slot System**:

* **Base Capacity:** You can carry a number of gear items equal to **10 + STR modifier** (or higher with class traits like Fighter's *Hauler* or Half-Ogre *Colossal Brawn*).
* **Slots per Item:**
  * **Standard Item (1 Slot):** Weapon, shield, rations (3 days), torch bundle (3), grappling hook & rope, potion vial, quiver of 20 arrows.
  * **Bulky Item (2 Slots):** Two-handed weapons, heavy armor (chain/plate), portable battering ram, tent/bedroll bundle.
  * **Small / Negligible (0 Slots):** Up to 100 gold coins, single keys, rings, lucky talismans (up to 3 small items fit in 1 standard slot).
* **Encumbrance Penalty:** If you carry items exceeding your total gear slots, you are **Encumbered**:
  * You have **Disadvantage on all physical checks and attack rolls**.
  * Movement speed is halved.
  * You cannot run or flee from combat encounters.
