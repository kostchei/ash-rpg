# Arcane & Occult Classes

> **"Reality is a woven tapestry; arcane scholars pluck its threads, warlocks strike bargains with its horrors, and witches whisper ancient names into the boiling cauldron."**

---

=== "Bard"

    ## 🎵 Bard

    Welcome wanderers, skalds, and wise advisors whose melodies preserve ancient lore, inspire heroic valor, and fascinate the minds of beasts and men.

    * **Weapons:** Crossbow, dagger, mace, shortbow, shortsword, spear, staff
    * **Armor:** Leather armor, chainmail, shields
    * **Hit Points:** 1d6 per level
    * **Languages:** You know **4 additional common languages** and **1 rare language**.
    * **Bardic Arts:** You have Advantage on checks related to oration, performing arts, ancient history, and diplomatic negotiation.
    * **Fascinate (Focus):** Make a **DC 12 Charisma check**. On a success, transfix all targets in Near whose level is $\le 1 + \lfloor \text{Level} / 2 \rfloor$. If you fail (excluding focus loss), you cannot use this again until you rest.
    * **Inspire:** Each day, grant a number of **Luck Tokens equal to your Charisma modifier (min. 1)** to allies.
    * **Magical Dabbler:** You can activate any spell scroll or wand using your **Charisma modifier**. On a critical failure (natural 1), roll a Wizard Mishap.
    * *Wand Acquisition:* In place of making a 2d6 talent roll, you may choose to automatically find a random Priest or Wizard wand.

    ### Bard Talents (2d6)
    | 2d6 | Effect (2 duplicate = reroll) |
    | :---: | :--- |
    | **2** | You have **Advantage on all Downtime checks** (excluding carousing). |
    | **3–6** | **+1 to melee and ranged attacks** OR +1 to Fascinate checks. |
    | **7–9** | **+2 points to distribute among any stats.** |
    | **10–11** | Add **+2 to your group's Carousing event rolls**. |
    | **12** | Choose any talent or gain +2 points to distribute among your stats. |

=== "Necromancer"

    ## 💀 Necromancer

    Feared mystics who gaze beyond the shores of the living, guiding spirits to eternal rest or raising shambling thralls from consecrated soil.

    * **Weapons:** Crossbow, dagger, longsword, scimitar, staff, stave
    * **Armor:** Leather armor, chainmail
    * **Hit Points:** 1d6 per level
    * **Death Sense:** Sense the location and state of undead and dying creatures within Near range.
    * **River of Death:** Your spirit has walked the eternal river. You **do not die at 0 Constitution**, and you roll a **1d6 for your death timer** instead of a 1d4.
    * **Necromancer Spellcasting:** You cast spells from the **Necromancer Spell List** using **Charisma** (DC = 10 + Spell Tier).
        * *Failure:* Cannot cast that spell until you complete a rest.
        * *Critical Failure (Natural 1):* Roll on the **Necromancer Mishap Table** for that spell's tier.

=== "Warlock"

    ## 👁️ Warlock

    Occult conduits bound to cosmic entities, fiends, and ancient eldritch powers that bestow devastating Patron Boons.

    * **Weapons:** Club, crossbow, dagger, mace, longsword
    * **Armor:** Leather armor, chainmail, shields
    * **Hit Points:** 1d6 per level
    * **Languages:** You know one of: **Celestial, Diabolic, Draconic, Primordial, or Sylvan**.
    * **Patron Bond:** Choose an otherworldly patron to serve (e.g. *Shune the Vile, Ramlaat, The Lost, Memnon*).
    * **Patron Boons:** At 1st level, you gain 1 random **Patron Boon** from your patron. You gain an additional Patron Boon at every even level (2, 4, 6, 8, 10).
    * *Alternative Talents:* When leveling up, you may choose to roll on your **Patron Boon table** rather than the Warlock Talents table.

    ### Warlock Talents (2d6)
    | 2d6 | Effect |
    | :---: | :--- |
    | **2** | Roll a **Patron Boon from ANY patron** (an unexplained eldritch gift). |
    | **3–6** | **+1 to any two different stats.** |
    | **7–9** | **+1 to melee or ranged attacks.** |
    | **10–11** | Roll two Patron Boons and **choose one to keep**. |
    | **12** | Choose any talent or gain +2 points to distribute among your stats. |

=== "Witch"

    ## 🦉 Witch

    Cackling crones, hedge herbalists, and outcast maidens who channel natural curses, bind loyal familiars, and cast potent hexes through cauldrons and bones.

    * **Weapons:** Dagger, staff
    * **Armor:** Leather armor
    * **Hit Points:** 1d4 per level
    * **Languages:** You know **Diabolic, Primordial, and Sylvan**.
    * **Familiar:** You have a small loyal animal (raven, toad, black cat, serpent, rat) that speaks Common.
        * Your familiar can be the origin point for spells you cast (calculating range from its location).
        * *Familiar Death:* If it dies, restore it to life by permanently sacrificing **1d4 Hit Points**.
    * **Witch Spellcasting:** You cast spells from the **Witch Spell List** using **Charisma** (DC = 10 + Spell Tier).
        * *Failure:* Cannot cast that spell until you complete a rest.
        * *Critical Failure (Natural 1):* Roll on the **Diabolical Mishap Table** for that tier.

=== "Wyrdling"

    ## 🐙 Wyrdling

    Those called down into the sunless abyss who have heard the gibbering abominations and felt the presence of The Lost walking within their mutating flesh.

    * **Weapons:** Club, crossbow, dagger, pseudopod, shortbow, shortsword, spear
    * **Armor:** Leather armor, chainmail, shields
    * **Hit Points:** 1d6 per level
    * **Languages:** You know **Primordial**.
    * **Hideous Biology:** You can stretch your flesh and bones to **squeeze through inch-wide cracks** (takes 3 rounds).
    * **Pseudopod:** You can sprout a clawed, barbed fleshy tentacle:
        * *Range:* Near | *Damage:* 1d6 (Finesse — use STR or DEX).
    * **Corruption:** At 1st level, roll one talent on the **Corruption Table (d10)**.

    #### Corruption Mutations Table (d10)
    | d10 | Mutation | Effect |
    | :---: | :--- | :--- |
    | **1** | **Mad Certainty** | Immune to mind control, psychic horror, and fear. |
    | **2** | **Vicious Pseudopod** | Pseudopod sprouts barbs; damage die increases by one step (1d6 $\rightarrow$ 1d8 $\rightarrow$ 1d10). |
    | **3** | **Segmented Pseudopod** | Pseudopod attack range increases to **Double Near**. |
    | **4** | **Fish Gills** | You can breathe underwater indefinitely. |
    | **5** | **Dorsal Hump** | Permanently gain **+1d6 maximum Hit Points**. |
    | **6** | **Thickened Skin** | Gain **+1 bonus to Armor Class**. |
    | **7** | **Fish Eyes** | You **cannot be surprised**. |
    | **8** | **Abominable Hunger** | 1/day, regain **2d6 HP** by devouring raw humanoid flesh. |
    | **9** | **Creepy Stillness** | Gain **Advantage on Dexterity checks** to hide and sneak. |
    | **10** | **Evolution** | Distribute **+3 bonus points** among your stats and max HP. |

    ### Wyrdling Talents (2d6)
    | 2d6 | Effect |
    | :---: | :--- |
    | **2** | Gain **two new Corruption talents** immediately. |
    | **3–6** | **+2 to Strength, Dexterity, or Charisma stat.** |
    | **7–9** | Gain **one new Corruption talent**. |
    | **10–11** | **+1 to attack and damage rolls with your Pseudopod.** |
    | **12** | Choose any talent or gain +2 points to distribute among your stats. |

=== "Enchanter"

    ## ✨ Enchanter (Regional Arcane)

    Mesmerists of the Red Sands and Midnight Sun who bend light, temperature mirages, and mortal minds through heat and frost glamours.

    * **Weapons:** Dagger, dart, staff, shortbow
    * **Armor:** None
    * **Hit Points:** 1d4 per level
    * **Mirage Weaver:** Can project heat mirages or shimmering frost phantasms in a Near radius at will (confounding ranged attacks with Disadvantage).
    * **Glamour Spellcasting:** You cast spells from the **Wizard Spell List (Enchantment & Glamour focus)** using **Intelligence or Charisma** (DC = 10 + Spell Tier).
    * **Captivate Mind:** 2/day, force a humanoid of your level or lower to pass a DC 12 CHA check or be charmed, treating you as a trusted friend for 10 rounds.

    ### Enchanter Talents (2d6)
    | 2d6 | Effect |
    | :---: | :--- |
    | **2** | 1/day, cast *Hold Person* or *Charm* without expending a spell slot. |
    | **3–7** | **+1 to glamour spellcasting checks.** |
    | **8–9** | **+2 to Charisma or Intelligence stat.** |
    | **10–11** | Charmed targets roll saves against your magic with **Disadvantage**. |
    | **12** | Choose any talent or gain +2 points to distribute among your stats. |

=== "Fire-Mage"

    ## 🔥 Fire-Mage (Regional Arcane)

    Sun-priests and pyromancers of the Red Sands sun-temples who command searing solar rays, flame bursts, and molten wards.

    * **Weapons:** Dagger, staff, sling
    * **Armor:** Leather armor
    * **Hit Points:** 1d6 per level
    * **Flame Spark:** Can produce magical fire from your fingertips at will (Near range, 1d4 fire damage or ignites oil/flammables).
    * **Heat Immunity:** Complete resistance to natural heat, desert dehydration, and take half damage from magical flames.
    * **Pyromancy Spellcasting:** You cast fire and evocation spells using **Intelligence** (DC = 10 + Spell Tier). Critical hits with fire spells cause targets to catch fire (1d6 damage/round until extinguished).

    ### Fire-Mage Talents (2d6)
    | 2d6 | Effect |
    | :---: | :--- |
    | **2** | All fire spell damage dice **explode** (reroll maximum die values). |
    | **3–7** | **+1 to arcane fire spellcasting checks.** |
    | **8–9** | **+2 to Intelligence or Constitution stat.** |
    | **10–11** | 1/day, erupt in a **Ring of Fire** dealing 3d6 damage to all adjacent foes. |
    | **12** | Choose any talent or gain +2 points to distribute among your stats. |

=== "Illusionist"

    ## 🎭 Illusionist (Regional Arcane)

    Grand illusionists and masquerade masters of the City of Masks who bend shadows, trick mirrors, and false reality into lethal art.

    * **Weapons:** Crossbow, dagger, rapier, staff
    * **Armor:** None
    * **Hit Points:** 1d4 per level
    * **Mirror Trick:** 3/day when attacked, summon a spectral duplicate; the incoming attack automatically hits the duplicate instead.
    * **Master of Deception:** Advantage on all checks to disguise, forge documents, cast optical illusions, and deceive guards.
    * **Phantasm Spellcasting:** You cast illusion spells using **Intelligence** (DC = 10 + Spell Tier). Illusions cast can produce sound, smell, and tactile resistance.

    ### Illusionist Talents (2d6)
    | 2d6 | Effect |
    | :---: | :--- |
    | **2** | 1/day, turn **invisible for 1d6 rounds** upon taking damage. |
    | **3–7** | **+1 to illusion spellcasting checks.** |
    | **8–9** | **+2 to Intelligence or Dexterity stat.** |
    | **10–11** | Gain **+2 uses per day of Mirror Trick**. |
    | **12** | Choose any talent or gain +2 points to distribute among your stats. |
