## 🌿 Detailed Ancestry Profiles

=== "Common Peoples"

    ### 1. Human
    * **Description:** Ambitious, versatile, and enduring. Humans dominate expanding frontiers through perseverance and sheer will.
    * **Traits:**
        * **Adaptability:** Gain **+1 additional Gear Slot** and **+1 extra Starting Talent** rolled or selected from your Class list.
        * **Unyielding Grit:** Once per session, you may re-roll any failed d20 test.
    * **Cultural Seed Question:** *What ancestral empire or kingdom did your family flee from, and what crest do you carry?*
    * **World Impact:** Seeds a bustling **Trading Post** or **Frontier Garrison** dealing in standard arms, hirelings, and supply lines.

    ---

    ### 2. Dwarf
    * **Description:** Forged from deep granite and ancient grudges. Dwarves are sturdy artisans, subterranean miners, and heavily armored vanguard fighters.
    * **Traits:**
        * **Stout Vitality:** You start with **+1 bonus Hit Point at 1st level**. When gaining levels, you roll your Hit Die for maximum HP increases with **Advantage** (roll twice and take the higher result).
        * **Armor Heritage:** You choose to add proficiency in either **Chainmail** or **Plate Mail** at character creation regardless of class (you can wear and fight in this armor type without penalty; note that starting equipment is still basic/leather).
        * **Passive Stone & Trap Sense (No Enunciation Needed):** In procedural / GM-less play, you do not need to declare that you are searching. Whenever you enter an area with hidden stonework, sliding walls, sloping tunnels, deadfalls, or pit traps, make an automatic check against **DC 12**. On a success, you spot the hazard or architectural feature before triggering it.
    * **Restrictions:**
        * **Stature Limit:** Due to your compact ~4-foot frame, you cannot wield **Greatswords**, **Longbows**, or **Lances/Pikes** *(Battleaxes, Warhammers, Mauls, and Heavy Crossbows remain fully usable)*.
        * **Arcane Void:** Dwarves cannot cast arcane spells or take the **Wizard** or **Illusionist** class *(Priest/Cleric, Fighter, Thief/Delver, Sage, and Alchemist/Runesmith remain open)*.
    * **Senses:** Normal Vision (relies on torches and lanterns).
    * **Cultural Seed Question:** *Which ancestral hall of your clan was lost to the deeps, and what oath must you fulfill to reclaim it?*
    * **World Impact:** Seeds a **Dwarven Smithy Hold** producing Masterwork weapons, heavy plate armor, and mining explosives.

    ---

    ### 3. Elf (High)
    * **Description:** Long-lived beings of graceful antiquity, attuned to ancient leylines and arcane scholarship.
    * **Traits:**
        * **Arcane Affinity:** If a non-caster, you learn 1 utility cantrip or minor ritual. If a spellcaster, you gain +1 spell slot or +1 to casting checks.
        * **Fey Trance:** Immune to magical sleep; need only 4 hours of meditative trance for a full rest.
        * **Senses:** Low-Light Vision (60 feet).
    * **Cultural Seed Question:** *What ancient magical catastrophe does your family memorialize, and what taboo star must you never look upon?*
    * **World Impact:** Seeds a **High Arcane Archive** offering rare spell scroll translation, enchanted glass, and astrological readings.

    ---

    ### 4. Halfling
    * **Description:** Small of stature but immense in bravery and stealth. Halflings value good food, hidden burrows, and quiet cunning.
    * **Traits:**
        * **Nimble & Elusive:** When unarmored or wearing light armor, you gain **+1 AC** and can pass through spaces occupied by creatures larger than you.
        * **Stout-Hearted:** Advantage on all saving throws against Fear, Despair, and Horror.
    * **Cultural Seed Question:** *What cozy trade recipe or secret family brew is your hometown famous for across the entire continent?*
    * **World Impact:** Seeds a **Bucolic River Hamlet** offering long-lasting rations, brewing ingredients, swift riverboats, and hospitality.

=== "Wild & Primal Kin"

    ### 5. Wood Elf
    * **Description:** Solitary hunters and canopy wardens who revere the primeval, untamed wilderness.
    * **Traits:**
        * **Mask of the Wild:** Advantage on stealth checks when hiding in foliage, fog, heavy rain, or natural terrain.
        * **Canopy Stride:** Movement speed is not slowed by rough wilderness terrain, brambles, or canopy branches.
        * **Senses:** Low-Light Vision (60 feet).
    * **Cultural Seed Question:** *What beast god does your tribe revere, and which sacred tree marks your border?*
    * **World Impact:** Seeds a **Tree-Top Stalker Enclave** offering poisoned arrows, hunting beasts, and guided wilderness passage.

    ---

    ### 6. Forest Gnome
    * **Description:** Whimsical, sharp-witted burrowers deeply connected with small woodland beasts and woodland illusions.
    * **Traits:**
        * **Beast Tongue:** Can communicate basic emotions, warnings, and queries with natural woodland animals.
        * **Innate Phantasm:** Can cast a minor optical illusion once per rest without a spell roll.
        * **Senses:** Low-Light Vision (60 feet).
    * **Cultural Seed Question:** *What trick or prank did your clan play on a haughty noble that is still celebrated in song?*
    * **World Impact:** Seeds a **Gnome Illusion Workshop** offering trick gear, smoke bombs, lock-picks, and alchemical toys.

    ---

    ### 7. Lizardman
    * **Description:** Stoic, cold-blooded hunters of marsh and delta whose traditions date back to the primordial age.
    * **Traits:**
        * **Scaly Hide:** Natural **AC 12 + DEX modifier** when unarmored or in light hides.
        * **Amphibious Hunter:** Can hold breath underwater for up to 15 minutes; gains a swimming speed equal to land speed.
        * **Bite Attack:** Natural weapon attack (1d6 piercing damage).
    * **Cultural Seed Question:** *What great swamp leviathan did your ancestors slay, and what bone relic did they craft from its skull?*
    * **World Impact:** Seeds a **Lizardfolk Stilt Settlement** offering venom extractions, swamp skiffs, and waterborne tracking.

    ---

    ### 8. Orc
    * **Description:** Strong, disciplined, and battle-tested warriors bound by blood pacts and fierce warrior codes.
    * **Traits:**
        * **Blood Surge:** When reduced to 0 HP, make a DC 12 Constitution check. On a success, drop to 1 HP instead (usable once per expedition).
        * **Relentless Ferocity:** Deal +1d4 extra damage on critical hits in melee combat.
        * **Senses:** Infravision (60 feet).
    * **Cultural Seed Question:** *What great war banner or clan totem do you carry, and what oath of vengeance is stitched upon it?*
    * **World Impact:** Seeds an **Orcish Iron Foundry** specializing in heavy siege gear, spiked armor, and hardened iron weapons.

    ---

    ### 9. Half-Ogre
    * **Description:** Towering behemoths possessing incredible physical might, often serving as frontier heavy lifters or shock vanguards.
    * **Traits:**
        * **Colossal Brawn:** Gain **+4 Gear Slots** for carrying encumbrance. You can wield two-handed heavy weapons in one hand.
        * **Thick Hide:** +1 bonus to maximum Hit Points per level.
        * **Senses:** Infravision (30 feet).
    * **Cultural Seed Question:** *Who was your giant/ogre parent, and what giant-kin heirloom were you gifted upon reaching adulthood?*
    * **World Impact:** Seeds a **Crag Stronghold** capable of moving heavy boulders, clearing mountain passages, and offering brute labor.

=== "The Underdark & Deeps"

    ### 10. Deep Gnome (Svirfneblin)
    * **Description:** Master gem-miners and silent subterranean survivors living amidst the dark stone of the Underdark.
    * **Traits:**
        * **Stone Camouflage:** Advantage on stealth checks in rocky or subterranean terrain.
        * **Innate Earth Magic:** Can cast *Blindness* or *Blur* once per rest.
        * **Senses:** Infravision (90 feet).
    * **Cultural Seed Question:** *What luminous gemstone does your clan protect from the horrors of the deepest chasms?*
    * **World Impact:** Seeds a **Subterranean Gem Mine** offering rare minerals, darkvision lenses, and pickaxe equipment.

    ---

    ### 11. Drow
    * **Description:** Elegant, cunning, and lethal subterranean elves steeped in shadow politics, poisoncraft, and dark arcana.
    * **Traits:**
        * **Venom Craft:** Advantage on saving throws against poison; starts with 2 doses of Drow Sleep Poison.
        * **Shadow Weaving:** Can cast *Darkness* or *Faerie Fire* once per rest.
        * **Light Sensitivity:** Disadvantage on attack rolls when fighting in direct, unshaded sunlight.
        * **Senses:** Superior Infravision (120 feet).
    * **Cultural Seed Question:** *Which matriarchal noble house or shadow syndicate banished you to the upper frontiers?*
    * **World Impact:** Seeds a **Shadow Outpost** offering sleep venoms, hand crossbows, and Underdark tunnel maps.

    ---

    ### 12. Kuo-Toa
    * **Description:** Bug-eyed, fanatical amphibious folk who dwell near underground seas and possess the uncanny ability to manifest gods through sheer shared faith.
    * **Traits:**
        * **Amphibious & Slippery:** Can breathe both air and water. Advantage on checks to escape grapples, bonds, or netting.
        * **God-Shaper Fervor:** Once per expedition, can channel faith to reroll a failed spellcasting check or grant an ally a +2 miracle bonus.
        * **Senses:** Superior Infravision (90 feet), detects invisible/ethereal creatures within 30 feet if submerged.
    * **Cultural Seed Question:** *What bizarre, newly-dreamed deity did your people craft from driftwood, seashells, and strange belief?*
    * **World Impact:** Seeds a **Submerged Shoreline Shrine** trading bioluminescent pearls, harpoons, and waterproof leather gear.

    ---

    ### 13. Derro
    * **Description:** Erratic, pale-skinned dwarven kin touched by eldritch madness and strange subterranean radiation.
    * **Traits:**
        * **Madness Insight:** Immune to magical confusion and madness. Once per rest, can roll 1d6 and add it to any INT or WIS check as a spark of erratic genius.
        * **Sunlight Vulnerability:** Takes 1 point of damage per hour when exposed to direct sunlight without heavy cloaks.
        * **Senses:** Superior Infravision (120 feet).
    * **Cultural Seed Question:** *What strange mechanical riddle or forbidden alien blueprint has driven your mind into restless mania?*
    * **World Impact:** Seeds a **Derro Soul-Forge** dealing in repeating light crossbows, strange clockwork bombs, and alchemical batteries.

    ---

    ### 14. Quaggoth
    * **Description:** Hulking, shaggy, beast-like humanoids with razor-sharp claws, adapted to the freezing, savage caverns of the deeps.
    * **Traits:**
        * **Savage Frenzy:** When below 50% HP, gain +2 to melee damage rolls.
        * **Natural Climber & Claws:** Can climb stone cavern walls at normal movement speed; unarmed claw attacks deal 1d6 slashing damage.
        * **Poison Immunity:** Complete immunity to non-magical poisons.
        * **Senses:** Infravision (60 feet).
    * **Cultural Seed Question:** *What ancient spirit of the deep cavern-winds speaks to you when the hunting is bloodiest?*
    * **World Impact:** Seeds a **Cavern Beast Den** providing thick beast pelts, climbing pitons, and trained cavern hunting dogs.

    ---

    ### 15. Myconid
    * **Description:** Gentle, contemplative fungal humanoids that communicate through harmonic telepathic spores and seek collective harmony.
    * **Traits:**
        * **Rapport Spores:** Can emit spores (30-foot radius) allowing all creatures within to communicate telepathically for 1 hour.
        * **Pacifying Dust:** Once per rest, can emit a cone of spores that forces a target to make a CON check or be incapacitated/calmed for 1d4 rounds.
        * **Sun Sickness:** In direct sunlight, takes Disadvantage on all ability checks.
        * **Senses:** Infravision (90 feet).
    * **Cultural Seed Question:** *What communal memory or fungal dream did the grand spore-circle impart to you before you departed?*
    * **World Impact:** Seeds a **Fungal Grove Haven** providing medicinal mushrooms, healing salves, and bioluminescent spore-torches.

=== "Planar & Otherworldly"

    ### 16. Tiefling
    * **Description:** Bearers of an ancient infernal lineage, marked by horns, sharp teeth, sulfurous scents, and fiery spirits.
    * **Traits:**
        * **Hellfire Spark:** Once per rest, can wreath a weapon in hellfire (dealing +1d6 fire damage on hit) or ignite flammable objects with a touch.
        * **Infernal Resistance:** Resistance to fire damage (take half damage from non-magical and magical flames).
        * **Senses:** Infravision (60 feet).
    * **Cultural Seed Question:** *What devil or demon holds the original binding parchment signed by your great ancestor?*
    * **World Impact:** Seeds an **Ashen Sanctorum** providing soul-contract services, demonology texts, and fire-resistant cloaks.

    ---

    ### 17. Deva (Celestial-Kin)
    * **Description:** Mortals touched by radiant higher planes, featuring luminous eyes, metallic hair, or subtle golden halos.
    * **Traits:**
        * **Halo of Radiance:** Can shed holy light in a 20-foot radius at will.
        * **Healing Touch:** Once per day, touch a creature to restore 1d8 + Level Hit Points or cure one disease/poison.
        * **Senses:** Low-Light Vision (60 feet).
    * **Cultural Seed Question:** *Which fallen angel or martyr saint is your guardian watcher, and what sign confirms their presence?*
    * **World Impact:** Seeds a **Solar Observatory Abbey** offering holy water, curse-cleansing rituals, and ancient celestial prophecies.
