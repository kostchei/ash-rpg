# Ability Archetypes (Playtest)

These six classes adapt the ability-focused archetypes to ASH. None requires technology: a Clever character may be a scholar or craftsperson, a Quick character a scout or rider, and a Charming character a caravan leader. Where a campaign has technology, tools and equipment appropriate to the setting can fill the same roles; these features do not grant free equipment or automatic success.

Choose one archetype at character creation. Its named ability is one of its two prime abilities; choose the second at creation. Roll its hit die plus CON modifier for starting HP and when gaining a level, using the usual HP rules. Use its [saving throws](class_roster.md#class-saving-throws). At every odd level, including 1st and 21st, roll once on its talent table below. Each bonus stacks when rolled again; an ability increase follows the normal limits. Armor and weapon names refer to the [equipment rules](07_equipment_and_services.md).

## Strong

**Prime:** STR. **Hit die:** d8. **Weapons:** all melee weapons, shortbow, sling, and crossbow. **Armor:** all armor and shields.

- **Mighty Blow:** Once on each of your turns, add +1 damage to a melee weapon hit. This becomes +2 at level 5 and +3 at level 9.
- **Feats of Strength:** You have advantage on checks to force, lift, push, or break an object when brute strength can plausibly accomplish it.

## Quick

**Prime:** DEX. **Hit die:** d6. **Weapons:** all except heavy weapons. **Armor:** leather armor, no shields.

- **Quick Step:** Once on each of your turns, move up to Close without provoking a free attack (if applicable) from one creature you can see.
- **Skirmisher:** You have advantage on checks to sneak, balance, or operate a vehicle or mount at speed when speed and reflexes matter.

## Tough

**Prime:** CON. **Hit die:** d8. **Weapons:** all melee weapons, shortbow, sling, and crossbow. **Armor:** all armor and shields.

- **Hard to Kill:** Your maximum HP increases by 2 at level 1 and by 1 each time you gain a level thereafter.
- **Stand Guard:** Once per round, when a creature within Close takes damage from an attack you can see, you may take up to half of that damage, rounded down, instead. This damage cannot be reduced or transferred again.

## Clever

**Prime:** INT. **Hit die:** d4. **Weapons:** dagger, club, sling, and staff. **Armor:** none.

- **Expertise:** Choose two fields such as mechanisms, medicine, lore, alchemy, or technology if the setting has it. You have advantage on INT checks in those fields.
- **Tactical Insight:** Once per round, when an ally within Near can hear you, you may point out a weakness. The ally gains +1 on their next attack roll before the start of your next turn.

## Wise

**Prime:** WIS. **Hit die:** d6 (an ASH playtest choice). **Weapons:** club, dagger, staff, and sling. **Armor:** leather armor, no shields.

- **Intuition:** You have advantage on checks to sense deception, notice danger, or read a creature's emotional state.
- **Mystic Focus:** Once per rest, choose yourself or a creature within Close. It gains advantage on its next saving throw within the next hour. Describe this as spiritual, psionic, or another setting-appropriate practice; it does not require equipment.

## Charming

**Prime:** CHA. **Hit die:** d6. **Weapons:** dagger, club, mace, spear, sling, shortbow, and shortsword. **Armor:** leather armor, no shields.

- **Silver Tongue:** You have advantage on checks to negotiate, persuade, or establish rapport when the other party can understand you and is willing to listen.
- **Rally:** Once per rest, as an action, choose up to three allies within Near who can hear you. Each gains temporary HP equal to 1d4 + your CHA modifier (minimum 1); damage removes these before ordinary HP; they last until the next rest and do not stack with other temporary HP.

## Archetype Talents

Roll 2d6 at the class talent levels above. Use the entry for your archetype's prime ability; no talent turns a failed check into an automatic success.

| 2d6 | Talent |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Gain +1 maximum HP per level, including levels already gained. |
| 3–6 | Gain +1 to checks using your named ability. This does not add to attacks, saves, or spellcasting checks. |
| 7–9 | Increase your two prime abilities by a total of 2 points, distributed as you choose. |
| 10–11 | Gain +1 to one of your strong saving throws of your choice. |
| 12 | Choose one result above, or distribute 2 points among any abilities. |
