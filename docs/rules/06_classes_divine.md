=== "Green Knight"

    ## 🍃 Green Knight

    Green-clad champions of the primeval deepwood wearing armor of antler, ironwood, and bone. Protectors of Gede's sacred realm from demonic blight and corruption.

    * **Weapons:** Bastard sword, crossbow, dagger, greataxe, greatsword, handaxe, spear, staff
    * **Armor:** All armor and shields
    * **Hit Points:** 1d6 per level
    * **Deity & Languages:** Your deity is **Gede**. You know **Sylvan**.
    * **Rooted:** 3/day, activate on your turn as a free action: **Negate all damage you take until your next turn begins**.
    * **Primal Spellcasting:** You cast spells from the **Druid Spell List** using **Wisdom** (DC = 10 + Spell Tier).
        * *Failure:* Cannot cast that spell until you complete a rest.
        * *Critical Failure (Natural 1):* Must perform holy penance to Gede or the Old Spirits.

=== "Knight of St. Ydris"

    ## 💀 Knight of St. Ydris

    Cursed knights who walk the dark path of St. Ydris the Unholy, the Possessed. They embrace darkness to purge evil with steel and forbidden witchery.

    * **Weapons:** All melee weapons, crossbow
    * **Armor:** All armor and shields
    * **Hit Points:** 1d6 per level
    * **Languages:** You know **Diabolic**.
    * **Demonic Possession:** 3/day, enter a frenzied trance gaining a **+1 bonus to attack and damage rolls** for 3 rounds. Add **half your level (round down)** to these bonuses.
    * **Witch Spellcasting:** You cast spells from the **Witch Spell List** using **Charisma** (DC = 10 + Spell Tier).
        * *Failure:* Cannot cast that spell until you complete a rest.
        * *Critical Failure (Natural 1):* Roll on the **Diabolical Mishap Table** for that spell's tier.

=== "Paladin"

    ## ⚔️ Paladin

    Steel-clad paragons of knighthood who exemplify honor, duty, and mercy, riding consecrated warhorses and wielding an evolving Named Blade.

    * **Weapons:** Bastard sword, dagger, greatsword, javelin, lance, longsword, shortsword
    * **Armor:** All armor and shields
    * **Hit Points:** 1d8 per level
    * **Chivalric Oath:** You swear to act with integrity, humility, and chivalry. If you break this oath, you lose *Inspiring Presence* and *Named Blade* until completing a perilous holy quest.
    * **Inspiring Presence:** Allies within Near range rise from dying on a **d20 roll of 18–20** and regain HP equal to your CHA modifier (min. 1). Enemies within Near **lose immunity to morale checks**.
    * **Consecrated Mount:** You start with a warhorse of noble demeanor.
        * Your mount gains **additional levels equal to half your level (round down)**.
        * If lost, pass a DC 15 CHA check during downtime to train another (DC lowers 1 step each attempt).
    * **Named Blade:** At 1st level, you select a sword to name. It is a **+0 magic weapon**.
        * *Level 2:* Becomes **+1 weapon**.
        * *Level 3:* Gains **1 random magic weapon benefit** (roll on Named Blade Magic Benefits below).
        * *Level 5:* Becomes **+2 weapon**.
        * *Level 8:* Becomes **+3 weapon**.

    #### Named Blade Magic Benefits (d8)
    | d8 | Magic Property | Effect |
    | :---: | :--- | :--- |
    | **1** | **Radiant Smite** | Deals +1d6 radiant damage against undead and fiends. |
    | **2** | **Bane-Forged** | Deals +1d8 damage against one enemy creature type (Aberrations, Dragons, Demons, etc.). |
    | **3** | **Guiding Light** | Sheds warm sunlight in Near radius at will; dispels magical darkness. |
    | **4** | **Defender** | Grants +1 bonus to your Armor Class while drawn. |
    | **5** | **Spellcleaver** | Advantage on saving throws against spells while wielding your Named Blade. |
    | **6** | **Avenger's Fury** | Deal double weapon damage on the first attack after an ally drops to 0 HP. |
    | **7** | **Vampiric Rebuke** | Once per rest, regain HP equal to damage dealt on a critical hit. |
    | **8** | **Sun-Forged Edge** | Critical hits occur on a natural **19–20** with your Named Blade. |

    ### Paladin Talents (2d6)

=== "Seer"

    ## 🔮 Seer

    Baleful diviners who reek of smoke and blood. They untangle the whispers of the gods by tossing runes, bone charms, and reading celestial portents.

    * **Weapons:** Dagger, stave, spear
    * **Armor:** Leather armor
    * **Hit Points:** 1d6 per level
    * **Destined:** Whenever you use a Luck Token, **add +1d6 to the roll**.
    * **Omen:** 3/day, make a **DC 9 Wisdom check**. On a success, gain a Luck Token (you cannot possess more than 1 Luck Token at a time).
    * **Seer Spellcasting:** You cast spells from the **Seer Spell List** using **Wisdom** (DC = 10 + Spell Tier).
        * *Failure:* Cannot cast that spell until you complete a rest.
        * *Critical Failure (Natural 1):* Cannot cast that spell again until you complete **Seer Penance**.

=== "Monk of Yag-Kesh"

    ## 🥋 Monk of Yag-Kesh

    Still, quiet ascetics in light linen garb who undergo intense mental and physical conditioning in the desert monastery of Yag-Kesh.

    * **Weapons:** Staff, strikes (unarmed)
    * **Armor:** None
    * **Hit Points:** 1d8 per level
    * **Eye of Yag-Kesh:** While unarmored, add your **Wisdom modifier (if positive)** to your Armor Class.
    * **Fist of the Moon God:** Your unarmed strikes are magical weapons that deal **1d8 damage**. They become **+1 at 2nd level**, **+2 at 4th level**, and **+3 at 8th level**.
    * **Still the Heart:** You can stop your metabolism for a total number of rounds per day equal to your level:
        * You do not need to breathe.
        * Delay the onset of all poisons until the effect ends.
        * If you drop to 0 HP, you **do not fall unconscious or begin dying** until this effect ends.
    * **Sun on the Water:** Once per day, when a melee or ranged attack misses you, reflect the attack back at the attacker using their own attack roll result against their AC.
