# Martial & Frontier Classes

> **"When words fail and magic is exhausted, the edge of cold iron and the unyielding grit of warriors decide who survives the frontier."**

This section details ten martial archetypes from the Western Reaches and frontier borderlands, each featuring unique combat styles, mounts, survival talents, and progression tables.

---

=== "Barbarian"

    ## 🪓 Barbarian

    Clan warriors of the northern reaches — fjord raiders in dragon-headed longboats, tundra hunters, and steppe riders alike — who invoke the Old Gods before battle and settle matters with the axe.

    * **Weapons:** All melee weapons, javelin, shortbow
    * **Armor:** All armor and shields
    * **Hit Points:** 1d8 per level
    * **Shield Wall:** If wielding a shield, you can use your action to take a locked defensive stance. Your **Armor Class becomes 20** until your next turn.
    * **The Old Gods:** Each day at dawn, choose one Old God blessing to guide you until the next dawn:
        * **Odin:** You gain a +1 bonus to all spellcasting checks and lore checks.
        * **Thor:** Deal **+1d4 lightning damage** on melee weapon attacks the first time you kill an enemy each combat.
        * **Freya:** Once per day, gain a Luck Token if you lack one. Whenever you spend a Luck Token, **add +1d6 to the roll**.
        * **Loki:** You have **Advantage on checks to lie, sneak, and hide**.

    ### Barbarian Talents (2d6)
    | 2d6 | Effect (2 duplicate = +1 use/day, 10–11 duplicate = reroll) |
    | :---: | :--- |
    | **2** | 1/day, **Go Berserk:** Become completely immune to damage for 3 rounds. |
    | **3–6** | Your weapon attacks deal **+1 damage**. |
    | **7–9** | **Gain a prime-ability increase as described in [advancement](01_system_overview.md#ability-increases-from-class-talents).**, OR +1 to attack rolls. |
    | **10–11** | **Duality:** Choose **two different Old Gods blessings** each day instead of one. |
    | **12** | Choose another talent, or gain a flexible ability increase as described in [advancement](01_system_overview.md#ability-increases-from-class-talents). |
=== "Basilisk Warrior"

    ## 🦎 Basilisk Warrior

    Blazing-eyed warriors who coat their skin in mud and stone. Their ancient combat style mimics the basilisk's regal poise and ferocious strikes.

    * **Weapons:** Boomerang, club, dagger, spear, spear-thrower
    * **Armor:** None
    * **Hit Points:** 1d8 per level
    * **Basilisk Blood:** You have **Advantage on Constitution checks** to avoid harmful maladies, poisons, or afflictions.
    * **Petrifying Gaze:** One creature of your level or less that meets your gaze must pass a **DC 15 Constitution check or be petrified for 1d4 rounds**. It still takes damage as normal while petrified. You can use this talent a number of times per day equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum 1).
    * **Stone Skin:** Add **2 + half your level (round down)** to your Armor Class if you are otherwise unarmored. You have Advantage on checks to hide in natural environments.

    ### Basilisk Warrior Talents (2d6)
    | 2d6 | Effect (Reroll 2 if you currently have a basilisk) |
    | :---: | :--- |
    | **2** | You find a basilisk egg; a loyal hatchling emerges in 1d4 days. |
    | **3–6** | **+1 to weapon attacks and damage.** |
    | **7–9** | **Gain a prime-ability increase as described in [advancement](01_system_overview.md#ability-increases-from-class-talents).** |
    | **10–11** | **+1 use per day of Petrifying Gaze.** |
    | **12** | Choose another talent, or gain a flexible ability increase as described in [advancement](01_system_overview.md#ability-increases-from-class-talents). |

=== "Desert Rider"

    ## 🐪 Desert Rider

    Howling warriors thundering across the sand on wild horses, elven scouts wielding curved blades atop silvery camels, or bandits wrapped in colorful silks racing on sleek desert stallions.

    * **Weapons:** Club, dagger, javelin, longsword, pike, shortbow, scimitar, spear, whip
    * **Armor:** Leather armor, shields
    * **Hit Points:** 1d8 per level
    * **Charge:** 3/day, you can charge into combat by moving at least Near before attacking. Each time you do this, your melee attacks deal **double damage** that round.
    * **Desert Mount:** You start with a common camel or horse with a loyal demeanor. It comes when you call.
        * While riding your mount, you both gain a **bonus to AC equal to half your level (round down)**.
        * Your mount gains **additional levels equal to half your level (round down)**.
        * You can freely leap on or off your mount once per round.
        * *Replacing a Mount:* If you lose your mount, pass a DC 15 CHA check during downtime to train a new one (DC lowers by 1 step each attempt).

    ### Desert Rider Talents (2d6)
    | 2d6 | Effect (2 duplicate = +1 use per day) |
    | :---: | :--- |
    | **2** | 1/day, your mount can move double its speed and make an attack. |
    | **3–6** | **+1 to melee and ranged attacks while mounted.** |
    | **7–9** | **Gain a prime-ability increase as described in [advancement](01_system_overview.md#ability-increases-from-class-talents).** |
    | **10–11** | **+1 use per day of Charge.** |
    | **12** | Choose another talent, or gain a flexible ability increase as described in [advancement](01_system_overview.md#ability-increases-from-class-talents). |

=== "Duelist"

    ## 🤺 Duelist

    Spinning swordsmen and fast-talking fame mongers. Duelists deliver insults and injuries alike to defeat their foes in style.

    * **Weapons:** Dagger, all swords (Rapier, Scimitar, Shortsword, Longsword, Bastard Sword)
    * **Armor:** Leather armor, mithral chainmail
    * **Hit Points:** 1d8 per level
    * **Parry:** Once per day, an attack of your choice that would hit you **misses instead**.
    * **Tale Spinner:** Make a DC 15 Charisma check. On a success, strangers around you believe you are famous and important for the remainder of your interaction (a given individual cannot be fooled twice).
    * **Taunt:** When an enemy misses you with an attack, you gain **Advantage on attack rolls against that enemy next round**.

    ### Duelist Talents (2d6)
    | 2d6 | Effect (2 duplicate = +1 use per day) |

    | **7–9** | **Gain a prime-ability increase as described in [advancement](01_system_overview.md#ability-increases-from-class-talents).** |
    | **10–11** | Deal **+1d6 extra damage** when you hit with a Taunt attack. |
    | **12** | Choose another talent, or gain a flexible ability increase as described in [advancement](01_system_overview.md#ability-increases-from-class-talents). |

=== "Kyzian Archer"

    ## 🏹 Kyzian Archer

    Swift horse riders who race across the Steppes like wolf packs, trained in the deadly Kyzian composite archery style.

    * **Weapons:** Dagger, longbow, shortbow, sling, scimitar
    * **Armor:** Leather armor
    * **Hit Points:** 1d6 per level
    * **Hawk Eye:** 3/day, add **1 + half your level (round down)** to your next ranged attack roll and its damage roll.
    * **Kyzian Quiver:** One of your arrow quivers holds up to **80 arrows while only occupying 1 gear slot**.
    * **Steppe Steed:** You have a warhorse with a loyal demeanor that comes when you call.
        * Your mount gains additional levels equal to half your level (round down).
        * While riding in combat, you are **not slowed by difficult terrain**.
        * You can fire bows from horseback without penalty while moving.

=== "Pit Fighter"

    ## 🩸 Pit Fighter

    Blood-soaked arena champions, scarred desert duelists, and brash tavern brawlers who never back down from a fight.

    * **Weapons:** All weapons
    * **Armor:** Leather armor, shields
    * **Hit Points:** 1d8 per level
    * **Flourish:** 3/day, regain **1d6 Hit Points** when you hit an enemy with a melee attack.
    * **Implacable:** Advantage on Constitution checks to resist injury, poison, or endure extreme environments.
    * **Last Stand:** You rise from dying with 1 HP on a natural d20 roll of **18–20**. Once per day, you may automatically succeed on this roll.
    * **Relentless:** While dying, you may forgo d20 recovery rolls to **remain conscious and act normally**. Each time you take damage in this state, you lose 1 death timer round.

    ### Pit Fighter Talents (2d6)
    | 2d6 | Effect (2 duplicate = +1 use per day) |
    | :---: | :--- |
    | **2** | 1/day, **completely ignore all damage and effects from one attack**. |
    | **3–6** | **+1 to melee attacks and damage.** |
    | **7–9** | **Gain a prime-ability increase as described in [advancement](01_system_overview.md#ability-increases-from-class-talents).**, OR +3 max HP. |
    | **10–11** | Increase the HP regained from **Flourish by +1d6**. |
    | **12** | Choose another talent, or gain a flexible ability increase as described in [advancement](01_system_overview.md#ability-increases-from-class-talents). |

=== "Ranger"

    ## 🌲 Ranger

    Skilled trackers, stealthy wanderers, and peerless frontier scouts who call the primeval wilderness their home.

    * **Weapons:** Dagger, longbow, longsword, shortbow, shortsword, spear, staff
    * **Armor:** Leather armor, chainmail
    * **Hit Points:** 1d8 per level
    * **Wayfinder:** You have **Advantage** on checks associated with navigation, tracking, bushcraft, stealth, and handling wild animals.
    * **Herbalism:** Make an Intelligence check during exploration to prepare an herbal remedy. If you fail, you cannot brew that remedy again until you complete a rest. Unused remedies expire in 3 rounds.

    #### Herbal Remedies Table
    | DC | Remedy | Effect |
    | :---: | :--- | :--- |
    | **11** | **Salve** | Heals 1 Hit Point. |
    | **12** | **Stimulant** | Target cannot be surprised for 10 rounds. |
    | **13** | **Foebane** | Gain Advantage on attack and damage rolls against one chosen creature type for 1d6 rounds. |
    | **14** | **Restorative** | Instantly cures one poison or non-magical disease. |
    | **15** | **Curative** | Equivalent to a *Potion of Healing* (restores 1d6+Level HP). |

=== "Roustabout"

    ## 🍺 Roustabout

    Average laggards and tavern hands of no particular lineage. They wield weapons with the elegance of a hog-tied kobold, but when their back is against the wall, surprising guts carry the day.

    * **Weapons:** Club, dagger, staff
    * **Armor:** Leather armor
    * **Hit Points:** 1d4 per level
    * **Knowaguy:** Advantage on checks related to interacting with commoners, procuring shady rumors, and sourcing favors.
    * **Lucksmith:** Whenever another player uses your Luck Token, they gain **Advantage on the newly rolled check**.
    * **Surprising Guts:** When reduced to half your HP or lower, you gain **Advantage on your next roll**.

=== "The Ríastrad (Warp-Spasm Berserker)"

    ## 👹 The Ríastrad (The Warp-Spasm Berserker)

    Warriors of the primeval barrows who do not merely rage; they channel the terrifying, mythological Irish *ríastrad* (torque of the Earth Goddess). When invoked, divine earth-power violently warps through flesh and bone, turning the warrior into an agonizing, unrecognizable titan of war who tears through shield-walls.

    * **Weapons:** Greataxe, greatsword, javelin, spear, spiked club, unarmed gore
    * **Armor:** Leather armor, shields (cannot wear heavy plate; the spasm bursts the rivets)
    * **Hit Points:** 1d10 per level
    * **The Warp-Spasm (The Ríastrad):** 1/day (or by voluntarily sacrificing 1d6 maximum Hit Points until full sanctuary rest), you trigger the grotesque metamorphosis:
        * **Anatomical Distortion:** Your muscles balloon and contort grotesquely, bones snap and invert into savage leverage angles, and your skull erupts into a terrifying, monstrous shape. You grow to Large size (Reach: Near).
        * **Terrifying Presence:** All enemy warbands and beasts of lower level within Near range must immediately make a **Morale Check (DC 16)** or flee in panic.
        * **Devastating Rampage:** Your melee attacks deal **+1d8 force/bludgeoning damage**, ignore mundane shields and non-magical armor, and strike up to 2 adjacent foes with each swing.
        * **Immunity to Mortal Shock:** You are immune to fear, stun, knockdown, and pain debilities while transformed.
    * **The Agonizing Burden (Somatic Toll):** The transformation lasts for 5 rounds or until all enemies fall. When it ends, your body pays the brutal price:
        * You collapse into a feverish stupor and gain **2 levels of Fatigue**.
        * **The Three-Vat Quenching:** Your boiling blood must be cooled in cold water or ice within 10 minutes of battle, or you take 2d6 internal hemorrhage damage.
        * **Blind Frenzy:** If no enemies remain while the spasm is active, you must pass a **DC 14 Wisdom check** each round or lash out at the nearest living creature (friend or foe).

    ### Ríastrad Talents (2d6)
    | 2d6 | Effect |
    | :---: | :--- |
    | **2** | **Lon Laíth (The Hero's Halo):** A jet of boiling black blood erupts from your scalp when transformed; ranged attacks against you suffer Disadvantage. |
    | **3–6** | **+1 to melee weapon damage** in both normal and transformed states. |
    | **7–9** | **Gain a prime-ability increase as described in [advancement](01_system_overview.md#ability-increases-from-class-talents).** |
    | **10–11** | **Earth-Root Endurance:** Reduce the HP sacrifice required for additional Warp-Spasms from 1d6 to 1d4. |
    | **12** | Choose another talent, or gain a flexible ability increase as described in [advancement](01_system_overview.md#ability-increases-from-class-talents). |

=== "Dragon Rider"

    ## 🐉 Dragon Rider (The Bonded Dyad)

    Heirs to ancient draconic bloodlines (such as the Dragon Masters of Melniboné or Valyrian lords) who share an unbreakable telepathic and metaphysical bond with an apex dragon. They operate as a symbiotic dyad: the rider provides human cunning and fine diplomatic agency, while the dragon commands catastrophic aerial devastation.

    * **Weapons:** Dagger, lance, longbow, riding sword, dragon-horn
    * **Armor:** Leather armor, chainmail
    * **Hit Points:** 1d8 per level
    * **Telepathic Impression:** You and your bonded dragon share thoughts, sensory perception, and spatial awareness instantly across any distance. You cannot be surprised while your dragon is airborne.
    * **The Draconic Mount:** You command a bonded adolescent or mature dragon:
        * **Aerial Supremacy:** Flies with blinding speed (movement: Far/turn). Can carry the rider and up to 2 companions or 10 gear slots.
        * **Dragon-Breath (Recharge 5–6):** Unleash a cone of infernal flame (Near range, 3d6 fire damage, area, burns structures).
        * **Apex Claws & Jaws:** Melee attack deals **2d8 damage** (forceful, messy).
    * **Sympathetic Life-Link (Dual Vulnerability):**
        * If either dragon or rider takes damage, the other suffers 25% of that damage as psychic resonance shock.
        * If your dragon is slain, you must pass a **DC 18 Wisdom check** or immediately suffer permanent heartbreak madness (or death).
    * **Apex Upkeep & Melnibonéan Slumber:** A dragon cannot sleep in a tavern; it demands 2 cattle or massive game daily. Once per campaign arc or season, the dragon enters a deep, unshakeable torpor (draconic hibernation) that only an ancient Dragon Horn or existential threat can awaken.

    ### Dragon Rider Talents (2d6)
    | 2d6 | Effect |
    | :---: | :--- |
    | **2** | **Dragon-Horn Mastery:** You can summon your dragon from hibernation or across distant hexes in 1d4 rounds. |
    | **3–6** | **+1 to ranged attack rolls** and mounted melee attacks. |
    | **7–9** | **Gain a prime-ability increase as described in [advancement](01_system_overview.md#ability-increases-from-class-talents).** |
    | **10–11** | **Draconic Scales:** Your dragon's natural Armor Class increases by +2, and you gain +1 Armor while mounted. |
    | **12** | Choose another talent, or gain a flexible ability increase as described in [advancement](01_system_overview.md#ability-increases-from-class-talents). |
