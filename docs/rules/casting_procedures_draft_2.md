# Six ways to cast magic

*ASH playtest rules.* Give each caster one of the six procedures below. Use that procedure to cast and recover magic. **Tier** means ASH spell tier (1–5). Power-point lists have their own spell levels (1–20). A **rest** in these handouts means a completed ASH long rest. Starting quantities, advancement rates, DCs and breakpoints below are playtest choices. The first draft records the source games, including the priest's penance variant. The broader compendium compares 17 engines. [Magic systems design notes](https://github.com/kostchei/ash-rpg/blob/main/docs/rules/11_magic_system_mechanics.md) · [ASH rest rules](08_exploration_and_crawling.md)

| Tradition | What to put on the sheet | When the player commits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full Vancian | Spellbook and one named spell per prepared copy | At rest. In a fight, someone may need to protect a long casting. |
| Sorcery | Source spells, chosen spells, slots by tier and sorcery points | On casting. Pick a known spell, then decide whether to pay for metamagic. |
| Power points | Trained lists, spell levels (1–20), realm and list ranks, current and maximum points | Before each roll. The spell's list level sets its price and difficulty. The fraction of power left modifies the roll. |
| Kelseyian | Known spells by tier, with a box beside each | On each attempt. A miss can cost the one spell you need later. |
| Risk improvement | Known spells, Heat and HP | Between casts. Vent or risk injury. A caster who survives backlash can attempt the same spell again. |
| Binder | Resident vestiges, their signs, demands and gifts | At the pact. Each guest determines what you can do for the next day. |

## Full Vancian

The model is the AD&D first-edition magic-user. Each memorized copy carries one particular spell into the dungeon. The whole-round threshold below translates casting time into ASH initiative. [AD&D *Players Handbook*, “Acquisition of Magic-User Spells”](https://drduick.com/D%26D/books/ADnD_Players_Handbook.pdf)

**Prepare copies.** At the end of a long rest, study your book. Use the Wizard Spells Known by Tier table as your capacity for *prepared copies* at each character level. Write one spell of the matching tier in each space. Three tier-1 spaces could hold *Sleep, Sleep, Light*. Casting *Light* crosses out that copy. The next long rest restores every expended space. Prepare the same spells again or change the selection. [Wizard progression](05_classes_core.md)

**Cast.** Name the spell and its target, check its range, and expend the copy when you begin. Tiers 1–2 resolve on your action. Tiers 3–5 take a whole round. Spend this action casting and finish at the start of your next turn. A hit, a failed save, or loss of the ability to speak or gesture during that interval interrupts the spell and costs the copy.

**Resolve.** A completed spell works if its stated conditions still hold. *Sleep* affects eligible creatures within its rolled Hit Dice allowance. Excess Hit Dice or an immune creature can defeat it. Most spells give no save. Keep exceptions written into individual spells, such as *Fireball*'s DEX save for half. The spellbook excludes all healing magic, including researched effects.

**Grow the book.** Start with three tier-1 spells. In downtime, copy a scroll or borrowed formula with an INT check against 10 + tier. To recover a lost spell through research or invent an original one, agree on the effect, tier, limits and required evidence with the GM before beginning the work. Completing the research gives you a formula to enter in the book. The library can grow beyond what you can carry in memory.

## Sorcery

**Know a few spells.** Choose a source such as storm blood. Each time you unlock a tier, your source fixes one spell and you choose one other spell of that tier. Both count toward your repertoire. A storm sorcerer might start with *Gust* fixed by blood and choose *Spark*. Resting replenishes the power to cast them. Your source spell stays with you even if a different choice would suit tomorrow's journey.

**Spend a slot.** For the playtest, use the Wizard progression table as *open slots* by tier. At level 1 you have three tier-1 slots. At level 3 you have four tier-1 slots and one tier-2 slot. On your action, spend one slot of the spell's tier or higher on any known spell it can fuel. A higher slot improves the effect only where the spell says how. Restore all slots on a long rest. [ASH progression table](05_classes_core.md)

**Resolve the spell.** *Spark* lights one Near wick, or deals 1d4 damage to a Near creature on a spell attack (d20 + CHA modifier against AC). *Gust* pushes one Near creature or loose object one boat-length away. An unwilling creature can resist with a STR save (DC 10 + CHA modifier + spell tier). A miss or successful save still costs the slot.

**Metamagic.** Start with two sorcery points and regain them on a long rest. For this test, learn *Reach* and *Split*. Spend one point when casting to extend Near range to Far, or spend two to affect a second eligible target within Close of the first. Choose one modification per cast. Resolve attacks or saves for each target. Ordinary casts need only the slot. The slot and point economy draws on the 5e sorcerer. The fixed source share and prices above are ASH choices. [D&D sorcerer](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes#Sorcerer)

## Power points

Learn ordered lists of related spells. Each list belongs to one of three realms. Train in the realm's broad casting skill and in individual lists within it. A rank in a broad skill helps every list under it. A rank in a particular list helps only that list. Rolemaster supplies the realms and list structure. The following d20 procedure uses ASH attributes. [Iron Crown's Rolemaster comparison](https://ironcrown.com/rolemaster/rolemaster-past/)

| Realm | Casting skill | Attribute | Sample list with spells at list levels 1 and 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essence | Schools | INT | Movement — *Draw* one loose, hand-sized object within Near to you, *Still* one Near vessel up to the size of a river ferry for one round |
| Mentalism | Sciences | CHA | Perception — *Sense Attention*, *Share Sight* with a willing ally |
| Channeling | Devotions | WIS | Shelter — *Ward a Threshold*, *Shelter a Company* |

**Build the pool.** Maximum power points equal **2 × ASH character level + ancestry reserve**. The reserve is a fixed number assigned to each ancestry when the campaign begins. For the playtest, use 6 for Elf and 4 for every other ancestry. A level-5 Elf has 16 points and a level-5 Human has 14. A long rest refills the pool. Raising a casting skill improves the roll and leaves the pool size unchanged.

**Learn lists.** Start with one rank in your realm skill and one rank in a list. The first rank teaches the list. Character level determines which entries you can cast. On a native 20-level progression, a level-7 character can use entries through list level 7. For ASH's ten character levels, use a ceiling of **twice your character level**, up to list level 20. As that ceiling rises, the new entries in every trained list become available. Each level after the first grants one rank in your realm skill or a list in that realm, including a new list. Each skill and list has a five-rank cap.

**Borrow spells from other ASH classes.** Assign the spell to a list that fits its effect. Use the first number in its tier's band as its list level. List authors can use the rest of the band for distinct spells of the same tier. For a class from a 20-character-level game, use the earliest character level at which that class normally learns the spell as its list level. To use a native list spell in another ASH class, read the table in reverse and use that class's usual casting rules. These bands preserve the levels at which ASH's Wizard and Priest first gain each tier. [ASH class progressions](05_classes_core.md) · [Spell compendium](10_spells_and_magic.md)

| ASH spell tier | Equivalent list levels | Borrowed spell enters at | ASH level for the borrowed spell | Spell from an ASH class |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1–4 | 1 | 1 | *Cure Wounds* |
| 2 | 5–8 | 5 | 3 | *Hold Person* |
| 3 | 9–12 | 9 | 5 | *Fireball* |
| 4 | 13–16 | 13 | 7 | *Dimension Door* |
| 5 | 17–20 | 17 | 9 | *Raise Dead* |

**Cast from a trained list.** Choose an available entry and read the reserve band *before* paying. Spend one action and **power points equal to the spell's list level**. Roll **d20 + attribute modifier + realm skill rank + list rank + reserve modifier** against **10 + list level**. A natural 1 or a total below the DC spends the points and the spell fails. You need the full price to begin. On success, apply the spell's written effect, including any attack or target save it specifies. A borrowed *Fireball* costs 9 points and requires a DC 19 casting check. Its targets still get their DEX save for half damage.

| Fraction of maximum points before payment | Reserve modifier |
| --- | --- |
| More than two thirds | +0 |
| More than one third, up to and including two thirds | −2 |
| At most one third, with enough points to cast | −4 |

## Kelseyian

**Know a fixed repertoire.** Read the Wizard Spells Known by Tier table literally. At level 1 choose three tier-1 spells. At level 3 you know four tier-1 spells and one tier-2 spell. At an advancement that would raise a stat, you may take a feat to learn one extra spell of any tier you can cast. Record that spell in addition to the table allowance. A found scroll can supply the formula for a new choice when a place opens. [Wizard progression](05_classes_core.md)

**Roll every cast.** Choose an available spell and spend an action. Roll **d20 + INT modifier against 10 + spell tier**. On success, apply the effect and leave its box open. On failure, the spell fails and you mark its box. Only that spell is lost until you rest. On a natural 1, mark it and also roll on the Arcane Mishap Table. Rest clears the marks, subject to any lasting mishap described there. This roll-to-retain procedure comes from Shadowdark. [ASH mishap table](10_spells_and_magic.md) · [Shadowdark quickstart](https://www.thearcanelibrary.com/pages/shadowdark)

## Risk improvement

Use the Kelseyian spells-known table and the feat that trades a stat increase for an extra spell. Every known spell stays available after every attempt. Track **Heat** (or **Corruption**) from 0 to 3, starting at 0. To cast, spend an action and roll on your current row. Check the natural face of the kept die for a critical or fumble. For an ordinary result, add your INT modifier and Heat bonus to that face and compare the total with **10 + spell tier**. Use this single casting roll to land an offensive spell.

| Heat before cast | Bonus to hit or cast | Fumble | Ordinary check | Critical |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | +0 | 1 | 2–19 | 20 |
| 1 | +1 | 1–2 | 3–18 | 19–20 |
| 2 | +2 | 1–3 | 4–17 | 18–20 |
| 3 | +3, roll with Advantage | 1–10 | — | 11–20 |

A **critical** succeeds and doubles one numeric effect of your choice (damage, duration or distance). If none applies, you may affect a second eligible target in range. An **ordinary success** produces the written effect. On an **ordinary miss**, the action is spent and the spell fails. On a **fumble**, the spell fails and fire or corruption costs you **HP equal to 1 + your Heat before the cast**. Resolve that loss immediately. At 0 HP follow ASH's death and dying rules. If you can still act, you can cast any known spell on your next action, including the one that just failed. [Death and dying](09_combat_and_hazards.md)

After every attempt, raise Heat by one, up to 3. At 3 you can keep casting at 3 for as long as you can withstand the backlash. Spending an action to vent clears all Heat. A completed rest also clears it.

At maximum Heat, roll two d20s and keep the higher. The kept face alone decides the outcome. Two dice showing 10 or less fumble (25%). Every other pair crits (75%). The bonus has already reached its cap and cannot move the natural face across either boundary.

## Binder

**Make the pact.** Draw a known vestige's seal, meet its stated terms of invitation, and offer it residence in your body for 24 hours. Roll **d20 + CHA modifier + Binder level** against its pact DC once when it enters. The guest grants its powers on either result. A good pact lets you conceal the physical sign and act freely. A poor pact exposes the sign and subjects you to the guest's demand. The first time you defy that guest, take −2 on d20 checks until its pact expires. Its gifts remain available. [D&D 3.5 *Tome of Magic*, “Pact Magic,” Binder class and Table 1–1](https://dtdnd.neocities.org/books/player/Tome%20of%20Magic.pdf)

**Use the resident's gifts.** Most are supernatural abilities, usable continuously, at will or after a stated recovery. A few vestiges also grant a particular spell, with its own frequency and resolution. The pact roll settles the relationship for the day. To use a gift, follow its card. At expiry, remove the gifts, sign and demands together. A fresh pact can bring back the same guest or invite another.

| Vestige (pact DC 12) | Gifts | Sign and demand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Last Ferryman. Lay a copper coin on the seal. | Walk on water with your ordinary gear. As an action, carry one willing passenger across water to a safe spot within Near. This gift is ready again five rounds after use. | Green copper circles stain your eyelids. Give passage to anyone who tenders a coin while you can carry them. |
| The Widow of Bells. Ring a bell over the seal. | At will, speak through a bell you can see within Far. Touch metal to hear the last words spoken beside it (one action, ready again five rounds later). Once per pact, cast *Silence* on one touched bell for one minute as an action. | A bronze clapper hangs in place of your tongue. When someone calls for help within earshot, answer aloud and give your position. |

**Advance the binding.** Start with one resident and access to tier-1 vestiges. Unlock stronger vestige tiers at Binder levels 3, 5, 7 and 9. For this ten-level ASH progression, capacity rises to two simultaneous residents at level 5 and three at level 9. Each needs its own seal and pact check. Track each sign, demand and gift's recovery separately.
