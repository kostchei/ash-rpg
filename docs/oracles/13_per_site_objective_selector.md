# Per-site objective selector

Implemented for new ASH site layouts. Existing saved objectives are preserved. The catalogue merges the CoreDark and DuskUltima types into 15 types; it is separate from the longer 264-entry authoring wish list.

## Selection and placement

Each constituent small, medium, or large section receives exactly one objective, placed independently of its room-feature roll. A saved seeded coin flip chooses similar (same type, distinct targets) or different (distinct types without replacement) for the linked set. One large section has one objective regardless of this coin flip. The primary opening rescue is preserved in the terminal section; intermediate sections have separate targets.

Act I weights information, rescue, caches and passage; Act II weights materials, wards and access; Act III weights relics, cleansing, guardians and secured routes. Half of unforced primary selections use these act preferences and half use the full vocabulary. Thus every type remains possible, while path/act subjects change the reason for the operation. Ordinary regional leads use regional subjects.

## Persistence, clues, rewards and boundaries

Every objective has a stable section ID, type, target, procedure, completion condition, approaches and next action. Two different rooms contain evidence for its investigation; essential evidence is visible when those rooms are explored, without another success roll. Named contacts and routes are stored in that evidence. The final contact follow-up is a journal instruction: this selector does not automatically spawn a new tavern card, NPC dossier, or remote mapped site.

Treasure objectives place an identifiable physical target in room treasure. Existing room contents are retained and treated as one find; merely mentioning a hoard twice creates no second claim. Generated clues and ordinary targets do not receive invented additional treasure XP. The reward system still governs collection/allocation.

Resolving each constituent objective awards +1 story XP to participating active characters once. Three small sections can therefore award three distinct completion XP. Generic section completion does not automatically add path Knowledge or trigger victory. The existing authored rescue deed retains its explicit path effects. Both completion controls share the stable award identity.

The roll, related/different mode, objective locations and evidence survive return visits. Player projections conceal unreached objectives and clues and omit the mode roll. Authored completion checks remain table-adjudicated; routine investigation is not automatically gated behind a successful die roll.

Validation: all 22 profiles, three acts, six site-roll outcomes; all 15 types; deterministic seeds; both modes; distinct targets; one objective and two evidence rooms per section; treasure preservation; terminal rescue compatibility; hidden-player projection; three unique story awards with replay and alternate-handler deduplication.
