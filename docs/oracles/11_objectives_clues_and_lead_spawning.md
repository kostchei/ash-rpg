# Objectives, clues, and replenishing leads

Design specification. The starting procedural tavern currently has two Mind Below leads and one regional lead. The nine outer-power encounter packs have authored clue chains. The replenishment and per-engine spawning procedures below are not yet wired into the runtime.

**Procedural authoring:** Use the [264 objective templates grouped by path and act](12_procedural_objective_pools.md) and [campaign site, XP, loot, and replayability budget](../plans/procedural_campaign_budget.md). Named opening examples below illustrate mechanics; they are not compulsory starting quests. Bind eligible templates to the generated world instead.

## The available choices

At the start, create exactly two distinct path opportunities and one unrelated regional opportunity. Subsequently roll `N = max(2, d6)` once per meaningful replenishment event:

- Slots 1–2 are useful path opportunities at distinct sites, preferably advancing different independently sufficient solutions.
- For each slot 3–N, independently roll 50% path opportunity / 50% unrelated side quest.
- Add one guaranteed unrelated regional opportunity outside those N slots.

This produces 3–7 offered opportunities. A roll of 1 or 2 produces the minimum three. A roll of 6 guarantees two path opportunities and one unrelated opportunity, with four mixed slots. This counts offered choices, not accepted quests: accepted or partially explored quests remain in the journal and never disappear to satisfy the cap.

Keep at least two actionable path opportunities and one actionable unrelated opportunity while the central problem remains unresolved. Different sources pointing to the same obstacle do not count as two choices. A dangerous expedition can count if there is an attainable preparation route; an inaccessible destination alone cannot. If only one final operation remains, the other option can be a materially different preparation, access, rescue, or repair operation. Do not invent a second final victory if the party knowingly foreclosed it. After victory, retire this guarantee and offer aftermath or regional work.

The guaranteed unrelated slot retains the starting rule: 50% genuine, 25% fabricated claim, 25% previously real but now empty. Apply that same truth roll to additional unrelated slots. Roll and save truth at creation. Main-path essential evidence is never subject to this roll. Unrelated means no secret path prerequisite, mandatory clue, or antagonist affiliation; it may still provide ordinary money, supplies, allies, or renown.

## When to spawn

Use one campaign-wide opportunity ledger, presented through different sources. Taverns expose requests delivered by actual people; field discoveries can immediately create objectives without a tavern visit. Neither needs to be the exclusive quest giver.

Meaningful events are an objective being resolved, an expedition being concluded, an established deadline passing, a path operation changing the world, or evidence exposing a new actionable dependency. Process each event once. Visiting a screen, accepting a quest, resting repeatedly, or reconnecting is not a replenishment event.

Retain valid offered leads. Fill vacancies toward the saved rolled target; do not add a new full board on every event. If the current offers exceed a new target, keep them and stop filling. Replace completed, disproven, or explicitly abandoned offers as needed to maintain the minimum. Abandoning a lead does not reset its site, erase consequences, or create unlimited rewards: prefer another already-established opportunity before authoring a new one. Do not reroll the target merely because a slot needs replacement.

Tavern information must have a route home: a survivor, messenger, merchant, public announcement, or the party's own report. A clue recovered alone underground appears immediately in the party journal, but is not mysteriously known to the barkeep. If the party is away and the minimum is breached, make the replacement accessible through evidence, a reachable contact, or an observed event at their location.

## Spawn from needs, not quest titles

Each independent victory route has prerequisites expressed as world facts: knowledge, access, equipment, consent, protection, and a completion operation. Prerequisites within a route can require AND; complete routes are joined by OR. The generator finds the nearest unmet prerequisite that the party can act on now.

1. Read the saved world, known evidence, living contacts, accessible sites, supplies, and current antagonist operations.
2. Choose two different unmet needs, preferring different solution routes. If a remedy is unknown, choose an investigation that can reveal it without naming the hidden power in advance.
3. Choose an objective verb: recover information, verify a claim, rescue a source, obtain access, test a countermeasure, secure supplies, recruit consent, disrupt an operation, or execute a remedy.
4. Bind it to an existing plausible site and contact. Create a site only when no suitable site exists; persist its location, occupants, evidence, and rewards now.
5. Write the request, evidence of relevance, feasible approach, hazards, preparation, completion predicate, and actual consequence. A request is not proof that its issuer is right.
6. Attach evidence that resolves the current question and points to a next action. Reveal only what the source could know.
7. Add unrelated opportunities according to the slot and truth rolls. These draw from regional occupations, relationships, ruins, disputes, and ordinary hazards.

An information objective is complete when the relevant fact is learned and recorded, not when a boss dies or a quest giver receives an arbitrary token. Physical delivery, publication, or authentication is a separate objective only when somebody actually needs it. If players independently achieve a predicate, recognise it without requiring prior quest acceptance.

Example: “Find out where the night wagons take their prisoners.” The waterworks manifest, a rescued driver, or observation of the route can establish the destination. Each source reveals the same necessary fact, but carries different costs and optional detail. Once learned, the next objective concerns reaching that destination or protecting another threatened captive; it does not demand collecting the other copies.

## Clues that create play

Every essential inference has at least two independent sources at different reachable sites, ideally one testimony and one physical or observable source. Two documents locked in the same room are one source for this purpose. At least one means of finding essential evidence needs no successful check once the party reaches and investigates the right thing. Checks can determine time, noise, extra detail, bargaining advantage, or cost.

Each clue records: source; observable evidence; claim or established fact; which inference it supports; how it can be checked; site/contact it points toward; acquisition predicate; whether already revealed. Avoid naked lore paragraphs. “The invoice names three cisterns and tomorrow's departure” gives locations and a decision.

Discovery should change at least one decision: where to go, whom to trust, what to carry, when to act, what operation to interrupt, or how to win. Do not automatically equate testimony with verified truth. Essential sources can be mistaken about interpretation but must retain a recoverable route to the required fact.

If a source is destroyed, preserve the consequence and use an independently established source. If all authored sources are lost, generate a plausible recovery investigation, such as reconstructing dispatches from recipients. Do not teleport the original clue into the next chest. A missed deadline creates a changed situation with evidence, not a blank quest failure screen.

False regional leads yield a decisive discovery at the destination, allowing the journal to close them. Empty means the promised encounter or reward is gone; traces explain that outcome. Neither generates its promised treasure on entry or revisit. Travel still uses ordinary regional procedures. These leads need not secretly reward a path clue to justify their existence.

## Persistence and UI contract

Save an opportunity's stable ID, originating event, source, objective predicate, target site, supporting evidence IDs, follow-on needs, deadline, reward source, status, and hidden relevance/truth. Save the d6 result and slot rolls with the replenishment event. Use statuses offered, accepted, achieved, reported, disproven, expired, and abandoned; achieved and reported are distinct when delivery matters.

The player sees a concrete request, who supplied it, why it matters to that person, directions, known hazards, preparations, and discovered updates. Do not show “path quest,” “irrelevant,” truth percentages, unknown solution predicates, or the concealed patron. The host can inspect provenance and rolls. A player-created objective can link to an existing opportunity or evidence without generating a duplicate site.

Tavern cards and the field journal share this ledger. “Pursue” selects an expedition objective; it does not teleport, create a new reward, or reroll a site. An update can say “The ledger names a second cistern” and offer the newly evidenced expedition directly. Returning to town can supply new human requests as well as reports on existing ones.

## Implementation and acceptance checks

Implement a shared opportunity service around saved path facts and events, with per-path candidate recipes. Connect both procedural world sites and encounter-pack sites to it before claiming universal runtime support. Existing generic witness/record/preparation/finale chains are useful authored material, but are not a substitute for each engine's event triggers.

- Opening: exactly two different actionable path sites plus one unrelated site.
- Every d6 result respects the minimum; extra-slot side-quest rolls and regional truth rolls are separate.
- Resolving one lead leaves two useful path choices after replenishment, without erasing accepted work.
- Reads, retries, and restarts reproduce the same board, truth, sites, and rewards.
- Each essential inference remains obtainable through either independent source; losing one does not demand a successful check at the other.
- Field discoveries spawn the next feasible objective immediately; tavern knowledge respects its source.
- Every path can reach each authored independent victory without completing the alternative route.
- Dead NPCs, changed sites, elapsed deadlines, and completed predicates constrain later generation.
- Player projections reveal no hidden relevance, truth, future clues, or unearned solution conditions.
- False/empty destinations resolve once and remain empty; unrelated quests never become mandatory path dependencies.
