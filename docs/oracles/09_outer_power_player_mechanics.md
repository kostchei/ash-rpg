# Outer-Power Incursions: Player Mechanics

Status: authored rules revision, 2026-09-07. Implements the approved player-level redesign of engines 6A–6I. These procedures are campaign design, not registered runtime adapters or verified playable software.

An initial [connected encounter implementation](10_connected_path_encounters.md) now supplies selectable, persistent table-assisted modules for all nine methods. It records their sites, interactions, clues, and alternative outcomes. Full rules automation, regional placement, and complete campaign adapters remain distinct work; see that document's explicit automation boundary.

Use with [Adventure Path Engines](07_adventure_path_engines.md) and [Population Procedures](08_adventure_path_population.md). The engine describes what advances; this document specifies what players do, carry, risk, learn, and accomplish. The nine methods can serve different patrons, but an alternate antagonist must preserve the method's executable choices and receive appropriate dependencies and endings.

## Shared Authoring Contract

Each incursion changes a recurring player decision by the third expedition. Each has at least two **independently sufficient** success conditions. The conditions are alternatives, not components of a mandatory final boss kill. Record an actual completion event: possessing a key or learning a rite enables an attempt but does not win by itself.

Use the existing three-act, level 1–10 content envelope. Act transitions follow evidence and access. Earned early victory remains valid; later content concerns its inheritance. The normal branches must each have feasible routes, useful rewards, and enough authored material under the [XP and zone plan](../plans/path_sites_xp_and_zones.md). Do not count post-victory XP as preparation for an earlier confrontation.

- **Facts before consequences:** commit dependencies, enemy assets, timing, costs, interruption rules, and success predicates before they affect a decision. Preserve history when an active capability is removed.
- **Two clue routes:** every indispensable conclusion and remedy has independent evidence sources, including a class-independent means of access and execution. Ordinary clues name an actionable next step.
- **Prices before consent:** state a supernatural benefit, exact cost, due event, refusal option, and means of release before accepting it. No generic corruption meter applies across these paths. An NPC's coercion or an involuntary injury is not a player's consent to a bargain.
- **Useful tactics:** rescuing a person, stopping a raid, or banishing a body has its actual benefit even when it does not resolve the campaign. Do not force tactical success or erase its benefit to demonstrate a theme.
- **Fair opposition:** monsters pursue named objectives and retain actual locations, casualties, supplies, and expended abilities. No retroactive omniscience, free replacements, or arbitrary escalation to invalidate a solution.
- **Knowledge earns control:** successful investigation reduces uncertainty. Hidden state is generated and saved, not adjusted after the players act.
- **Party agency:** no accepted role, gift, or reputation automatically takes control of a player character. Explain the consequence and let the player choose the action. Solo parties can use timed mechanisms, recruited operators, and sequential tasks instead of compulsory split-party operations.
- **Aftermath:** successful endings preserve the Toll. Later attacks on a won settlement or working remedy are new, telegraphed situations, not retroactive cancellation of victory. Award each source once; alternative successes never duplicate campaign completion XP.

| Engine | Main player record | Recurring decision |

| Cthulhu | Dream connections and warded refuges | Accept useful contact or preserve safe sleep? |
| Nyarlathotep | Endorsements, evidence, and actual authority | Whose request deserves our name? |

## 6A. Cthulhu — Dream Contact and Safe Recovery

**Retain:** preparations make repeated arrival easier; completed discoveries remain in cult history. Banishment does not erase that knowledge. **Change:** a banishment earns a committed interval of safety; it is useful even when not a final victory.

**Generate.** Name three communities, every available arrival site, the submerged source, a dreamer refuge, and the next possible conjunction. For each manifestation, commit the earliest re-entry condition and the resources required after banishment. Generate two source-binding evidence routes as well as anchor-exclusion evidence: a rescued dreamer knows the binding cadence; an independent submerged archive records its geometry. Each essential fact remains recoverable without accepting a dream.

**Player procedure.** Before rest, a contacted character may accept a shared dream or decline it. An accepted dream reveals one specific true operational fact, such as an arrival site, route, or weakness. Record a connection to that dream's named servant. On a later unwarded sleep, that servant can locate the camp and must then travel there; it does not teleport or automatically interrupt recovery. Declining new contact does not erase an existing connection.

Warded sleep blocks location through a connection for that rest. The baseline ward consumes one bundle of prepared salts per camp rest, occupying one gear slot until used, or uses a functioning fixed sanctuary. Wards and ordinary recovery are separate: the ward prevents dream tracking; it does not guarantee safety from other threats. A cleansing rite at a named refuge permanently severs one connection. Its materials and class-independent officiant or instructions are discoverable from two sources. Ordinary uncontacted sleepers gain no connection merely by resting in the region.

**Monster jobs.** Deep Ones abduct or recruit dreamers and secure coastal approaches. Shoggoths consume a declared body or material cache to gain a stated encounter capability; material is removed once. Starspawn protect a specific arrival operation. Warn through tracks, dreams, and survey evidence before placing the only refuge under threat.

**Three acts:** discover contact and first-arrival conditions → exploit a banishment interval and test protection → execute either permanent remedy. Source binding remains possible before a manifestation; arrival is never mandatory.

**Success A — Coastal exclusion:** complete permanent exclusion works at every committed arrival site and discharge the active intersection. **Success B — Source binding:** reach the submerged source and complete its binding through one declared conjunction interval, preventing projection through otherwise intact arrival sites. Neither requires the other. Both end this incursion; killing the manifestation alone does not.

**Failure/neglect:** feasible preparations and conjunctions proceed. Enemy victory requires an actually sustained permanent intersection; a completed source binding or exclusion prevents it even if every preparation is in history. A banished body cannot reappear before the saved re-entry condition. Early success leaves calling knowledge, saved dreamers, and coastal recovery. Lost communities remain in the Toll.

## 6B. Nyarlathotep — Endorsements and Divided Interests

**Replace:** the predetermined six-step script and assumption that unmasking itself advances the plan. Keep fallible masks, conflicting patrons, and a committed objective.

**Generate.** Create three masks, two genuinely opposed institutions, one incarnation site, and three indispensable local mandates: custody of a relic, authority over the site, and an invitation from its recognised keeper. Name the issuer, scope, beneficiaries, evidence, expiry or revocation procedure, and means of impersonation for each. The incarnation requires all three active mandates and a voluntary acceptance at its site. The masks must actually acquire them.

**Player procedure.** A public endorsement or signed warrant creates only the authority stated. Record who heard or received it and what those people can permit. Renown is contextual: a community that knows and trusts the party may act on its endorsement, while an unknown community need not. Existing renown never grants universal authority or automatic corruption. Offers must give a concrete benefit, requested endorsement, known consequence, and refusal option. Concealed identity can be investigated; a request's stated scope cannot secretly authorise everything.

For example, rescuing a companion in exchange for a public accusation can both save that person and empower a patron to seize a suspect's office. Forged evidence has provenance and discoverable contradictions. The party can decline, negotiate narrower terms, seek independent rescue, or later revoke an endorsement through the issuer's actual procedure.

**Monster jobs.** Nightgaunts abduct witnesses; flying polyps isolate people or conceal a withdrawal; hunting horrors intercept couriers and escort the prize. Track captured people and papers, not only monster casualties. Monsters do not know testimony that never reached a mask.

**Three acts:** useful commissions and discrepancies → trace who authorised what → revoke authority or trap the embodied mask. At least two witnesses/documents independently reveal each essential mandate; a maker's record and former host independently reveal the trapping condition.

**Success A — Revoke the incarnation:** expose the common beneficiary to the actual issuers, revoke the three mandates, and complete a public severance at the site; its old endorsements can no longer sustain this incarnation. **Success B — Bind the mask:** use the discovered true designation and prepared vessel to trap and banish the local incarnation during its acceptance, even if the institutions still believe its public identities. Collecting the designation alone is insufficient.

**Failure/neglect:** masks can employ other willing agents with access; they cannot treat every refusal as consent. Enemy victory occurs when the three mandates and voluntary acceptance realise the committed seizure of authority before either remedy. Early exposure or banishment leaves betrayed clients, genuine accusations, and offices requiring legitimate successors.

## 6C. Shub-Niggurath — Gifts with Appetites

**Retain:** births, carrying capacity, food routes, and reproduction sources. **Add:** individually chosen gifts whose benefits genuinely work and whose maintenance affects expedition preparation.

**Generate.** Map three nurseries, their shared food network, the Mother Gate, and two independent sources for both severance and stabilisation. A captive breeder's records and root inscriptions reveal the source; an independent herbalist and an older stable grove demonstrate how bounded reproduction can work. Fix births, consumption, gift feeding intervals, and available substitutes. Count capacity once per connected component.

**Player procedure.** Record each gift's benefit, diet, maintenance interval, missed-payment consequence, release method, and consent. Baseline **Rooted Flesh** restores one additional HP after an otherwise valid rest in living soil. Its bearer substitutes one slot-sized mineral ration for ordinary food each adventuring day. Missing it applies ordinary hunger rules; it neither commands the character nor instantly transforms companions. A disclosed graft-removal procedure ends both benefit and appetite. Generate purchasable supplies and a noncult preparation recipe so accepting the gift is not a hidden compulsory allegiance.

Community gifts use the same principle: reliable crops or a guardian require a stated input. Separate voluntary bounded dependence from compulsory propagation. Transformed inhabitants retain their loyalties and agency; appearance does not establish allegiance.

**Monster jobs.** Fungi establish a nursery only after reaching and seeding viable ground. Ghouls retrieve actual battlefield remains. Dark Young maintain named reproductive nodes. Killing raiders stops that raid; survivors, births, and supply limits determine replacement. Destroying food may endanger civilians and increase raid pressure rather than automatically solve the network.

**Three acts:** save communities and discover useful gifts → provision, cure, or isolate dependencies → sever or stabilise reproduction.

**Success A — Sever propagation:** disable the Mother Gate and every independent forced-propagation source so no compulsory expansion remains possible. **Success B — Bound the ecology:** replace each divine reproductive dependency with the demonstrated regulator, supply its bounded population, and complete one full birth/feeding cycle without tribute or compelled propagation. The sources can remain alive under B. Neither requires killing transformed civilians or curing every voluntary gift.

**Failure/neglect:** advance births and consumption on their declared schedule. The enemy prevails when every food refuge is in its sustained feeding territory and forced replacement remains operative; a stabilised ecology cannot satisfy that predicate. Preserve rescued people, accepted transformations, famine losses, and damaged land in the aftermath.

## 6D. Hastur — Roles, Recognition, and Audiences

**Retain:** voluntary participation, required cast, and desecrated venues. **Add:** accepted roles grant privileges the player can choose to invoke.

**Generate.** Name the work, three venues, six indispensable roles, and the actual audience groups required at the final performance. Each role has a benefit, an invocation deed, a disclosed obligation, and a withdrawal/restoration procedure. Give two independent sources for withdrawal and counter-performance: former performers' records and an unaltered community rite. Passive exposure or accidental attendance does not accept a role.

**Player procedure.** Record role, accepting person, witnesses, privilege used, promises made, and recognition displaced. Baseline **The Returning Hero** lets the character request shelter and a willing local guide from an audience that recognises the role. Invoking it requires a public promise to return for a named performance on a stated date. Refusal or breaking the promise loses the privilege and changes those witnesses' trust; it never forces the player to attend or transfers control of the character.

Before invoking a role, explain any identity consequence: witnesses may begin using the role's name instead of the person's, affecting access to a specific relationship or office. Restoration requires the published rite and willing participation, not a universal reputation roll. Renown spreads accounts only through actual audiences and communication; ordinary fame alone does not corrupt anyone. Rival art and ordinary festivals provide real belonging without the patron's claim.

**Monster jobs.** Undead reenact a scene and hold people in its physical circumstances; byakhee transport performers or invitations; the King prepares venues. Killing an actor can interrupt this performance but cannot by itself erase a role that another person could accept.

**Three acts:** discover desirable privileges → recover personal identities and role terms → dissolve participation or perform restoration.

**Success A — Dissolve the cast:** enable an indispensable participant to withdraw and complete that role's dissolution rite at its originating venue, so a replacement audition cannot restore it. **Success B — Counter-performance:** recover the original names and rite and complete a willing counter-performance that restores all linked venues and cancels the work's claims, even with its cast still assembled. Neither is merely burning a book or murdering a performer.

**Failure/neglect:** real invitations and bookings spread the work. The enemy wins only when the intact required cast, consenting audience, and prepared venue complete the finale before dissolution/restoration. Lost identities and damaged relationships remain in the Toll; early victory leaves a cultural and civic inheritance.

## 6E. Yog-Sothoth — The Map as Equipment

**Retain:** a fixed, discoverable connection graph and legal activation order. **Add:** carried keys, player-directed rerouting, and declared enemy transformations.

**Generate.** Draw six thresholds with destinations, activation keys, return conditions, redirection rules, and ordinary approaches to at least the first two. Commit the invocation circuit and an uninhabited containment destination. Give independent evidence for cut/seal and redirection through a courier's route book and an engineer's archive. The containment site's emptiness and capacity can be verified before commitment; do not reveal surprise inhabitants after the choice.

**Player procedure.** Each attuned threshold key occupies one gear slot. Carrying the appropriate key permits its stated route or rerouting action; it does not unlock every gate. Record which end remains accessible after transit. Keys left with allies work only where those allies can operate them. A timed mechanism or sequential activation must allow a solo party to complete each solution. Lost keys have a committed replacement or recovery method.

People and cargo use the graph too. Closing an edge can strand a grain shipment or prevent pursuit. Show the known consequence before a deliberate closure. Each paid rerouting changes the saved graph and remains true until someone performs a lawful counter-operation; it is not a one-scene illusion.

**Monster jobs.** Mutants scout thresholds; abominations guard junctions; spawn can transport or anchor a gate where their defined ability permits it. A surviving enemy may promote only through a declared post-encounter condition, using a fixed form and capability chosen before combat. Retreat preserves injuries and triggers only the earned transformation, not a full reset or automatic level scaling.

**Three acts:** prove impossible routes → exploit and carry connections → cut or redirect the circuit.

**Success A — Cut and seal:** isolate an indispensable junction, safely discharge its stored power, and permanently seal its circuit function. **Success B — Complete into containment:** key the verified uninhabited destination, redirect the required edges, complete the conjunction there, and close the return connection. B can succeed with all original junctions intact. Both leave unrelated routes available according to the resulting graph.

**Failure/neglect:** only reachable, keyed nodes can activate in legal order. The enemy wins through its committed circuit and destination; a completed containment route does not count as hostile success. Stranded travellers, disrupted trade, and erased passages remain real aftermath.

## 6F. Tsathoggua — Rest, Deferred Prices, and Preserved Strength

**Replace:** the sealed strength multiplier, compulsory silence, and unrewarded investigation. Hidden strength consists of actual saved assets; discoveries narrow uncertainty. Retain disappearances, caches, and a coordinated emergence.

**Generate.** Create three caches, their provisioned broods, tunnels, target strongpoints, and three tribute agreements sustaining the local incursion. Record every stored creature and load, capacity and upkeep, and the emergence requirements. Each discovered cache gives its actual contents and useful evidence about another asset. A receipt and a rescued worker independently reveal each essential supply dependency; a former signatory and a sanctuary archive reveal the severance rite.

**Player procedure.** Keep the Absence Ledger alongside discovered reserves and accepted bargains. A hospice, borrowed servant, or safe tunnel offers a useful benefit at a stated price. Baseline hospice rest allows ordinary recovery in a guarded refuge plus one additional HP; its price is one sealed parcel carried to a named surface depot within seven days. Disclose the parcel's known purpose and inspection/refusal options. No bargain may conceal an unlimited claim inside the delivery promise. Failure loses access and permits the stated collection demand; it does not automatically enslave the character. Independently stocked shelter provides the ordinary alternative.

Rest advances only the actual time used. Dormant forces retain unspent resources; resting does not generate an arbitrary extra enemy action. Each wizard-servant can have one prepared response per encounter, named in hidden state and discoverable: open an escape, move one captive through an existing conduit, or remove a specific cache. A response consumes its actual charge and cannot be replenished until its declared supply arrives. Telegraph the mechanism; baiting it expends it for real.

**Monster jobs.** Formless spawn occupy rest sites and conduits. Serpent servants copy one witnessed technique only after returning to a named archive and completing a declared study; they do not gain the entire party's abilities. Wizard-servants interrupt through their prepared response rather than unlimited referee exceptions. Rescuing captives and recovering stores earn ordinary objective and treasure rewards.

**Three acts:** investigate absences and convenient offers → map reserves and build independent rest → preempt emergence or sever its tribute.

**Success A — Exhaust the emergence:** destroy or recover the indispensable stored assets and collapse the dedicated conduits, leaving no legal operation capable of supplying the committed occupation. **Success B — End the claim:** release the pledged servants, cancel all three sustaining tribute agreements, and complete the severance rite, expelling the incursion even with physical caches intact. The agreements cannot be renewed by simply appointing another collector after severance.

**Failure/neglect:** actual deliveries and tunnel work proceed; shortages limit the broods. Enemy victory requires taking and supplying the named strongpoints, not merely revealing hidden numbers. Independent shelters, rescued people, unpaid mundane debts, and lost property persist after early victory.

## 6G. Ithaqua — Warmth, Hauling, and Exceptional Access

**Retain:** nine-, six-, and three-week thaws, scheduled reserve growth, and an offensive that spends its stored reserve once. **Replace:** the absolute prohibition on winter attacks. Prepared exceptional access makes a specific winter expedition possible.

**Generate.** Name settlements, four anchors, their replenishment source, shelters, round-trip durations, hauling options, and a prepared sink capable of accepting a full discharge. Record which winter routes can be opened by a guide, equipment, shelter work, or a discovered glacier passage. Establish independent evidence for anchor destruction and sink/severance through an almanac keeper and a former winter-rite operator.

**Player procedure.** Cold protection, expedition fuel, and hauled supplies use actual gear/transport capacity under the equipment rules. Before departure, show outward/work/return time and food/fuel needed for the chosen route. Count a rescued traveller's transport and provisions; do not automatically kill them for joining the expedition. Maintaining a refuge changes reachable destinations and safe return duration on the map.

Baseline exceptional access requires insulated equipment, an identified winter guide or proven route, and a maintained shelter at each declared overnight stopping point. Provision the journey at twice its normal food/fuel requirement. Commit route-specific hazards before departure; meeting the requirements makes travel possible, not automatically safe. The calendar still advances normally and no thaw weeks are manufactured.

A wind bargain may permit one otherwise closed crossing. Its disclosed price is a hunter's mark: until removed at a named fire shrine, the marked traveller's position becomes known to a specified hunter at dusk. That hunter must travel normally. Refusing the bargain leaves equipment, guides, and refuge-building as alternatives.

**Monster jobs.** Wendigos separate travellers and threaten rescue routes; Gnoph-keh hold passes and shelters. Capturing a shelter changes logistics. A provoked offensive empties the saved reserve once, even into an empty valley; hibernation does not create invulnerability.

**Three acts:** provision and map the first winter → spend the short thaw or earn winter access → destroy transmission or exploit a full discharge.

**Success A — End transmission:** destroy all functioning banking/transmission anchors and sever their connection so the system cannot suppress the returning thaw. **Success B — Drain and sever:** lure the whole reserve into the prepared sink and permanently sever the replenishment source during its declared exposed interval. B may leave the anchors intact but inert. Neither simple endurance nor a discharge without permanent severance wins.

**Failure/neglect:** reserve grows only at committed dates from functioning sources. Permanent winter requires sustained closure of every thaw route and an operative source; a fulfilled remedy prevents it. Food debts, lost homes, and people left outside shelters remain in the Toll. Early success changes remaining expeditions into recovery work.

## 6H. Tcho-Tcho Compact — The Material Ability to Refuse

**Retain:** essential services and viable alternatives. **Add:** named priestly intermediaries, collection rights, and specific legitimacy rather than automatic recruitment after violence. Membership is affiliation; ancestry establishes neither allegiance nor guilt.

**Generate.** For medicine, justice, protection, trade, and travel in three communities, record provider, beneficiaries, capacity, inputs, cost, current alternatives, and route. Create three chapters, each with a named high priest, authorised collection date, tribute, supernatural subsidy, successor procedure, and patron contract. Each priest has expertise and motives as well as authority. Account books and an independent former intermediary reveal collection conditions; a dissident and the original contract reveal collective severance.

**Player procedure.** A service request or refusal changes real capacity and obligations. Record who can deliver the promised replacement and for how long. Renown here is reliability witnessed by a particular community: a delivered shipment or functioning clinic can earn trust and access; a boast or unrelated monster kill cannot substitute for medicine. Repeated claims about one delivery do not earn repeated trust or rewards.

Capture, negotiation, protection, or replacement of a priest affects exactly the collection rights attached to that office. Where martyrdom empowers succession, commit a specific condition—for example, a publicly witnessed killing during collection followed by a named successor completing the funerary rite. Preventing the rite, preserving the priest, or refusing public endorsement can matter. No death automatically recruits an entire population. Essential care can continue under other qualified providers.

**Monster jobs.** Proto-shoggoths guard tribute stores and transport. Human agents maintain clinics, adjudicate claims, or pressure signatories. Encounters can require protecting a useful physician while stopping a particular collection, rather than clearing every member from the map.

**Three acts:** establish real benefits and costs → deliver alternatives or prepare chapter defection → terminate collection through either route below.

**Success A — Independent provision:** replace all five services with operational independent providers, survive the next scheduled collection without losing essential care, and execute the contract's termination procedure so collective tribute is unenforceable. **Success B — Collective defection:** obtain all chapters' valid withdrawal, transfer necessary tools and mundane expertise to their communities, and perform the shared severance rite. B keeps the former chapter providers rather than requiring competing institutions; patron-only benefits have known substitutes before withdrawal. Killing the patron's local avatar is not required by either route.

**Failure/neglect:** the Compact prevails only as sole viable provider across the baseline region with enforceable collection. A successfully severed cooperative does not count as Compact dominance. A closed alternative changes control only when someone actually replaces it. Past tribute, service debts, and institutions to maintain form the inheritance.

## 6I. Azathoth — Useful Power and Its External Cost

**Retain:** specific capture boundaries, harmonics, linked collectors, and a stabilising counter-pattern. **Broaden:** the inputs include devices available to all characters and communities, not an indiscriminate penalty on spellcasters.

**Generate.** Place four collectors, three required harmonic categories, physical links, a committed conjunction window, and the counter-pattern. List exactly which resolved spell effects or device activations feed each category and what one captured event contributes. Include a healing installation, furnace, or defensive relic with real immediate value. A tuning sheet and an independent device test reveal capture; an engineer's notes and an intact stabiliser reveal both shutdown and counter-pattern procedures.

**Player procedure.** Before a known qualifying activation, state its benefit, recipient collector, harmonic contribution, and any sequence it completes. Baseline furnace: one full-night operation keeps a named shelter warm and contributes one heat harmonic if it remains inside a functioning capture boundary. Moving outside the boundary, grounding the device, or shutting down that collector prevents capture while preserving the device's stated ordinary use where feasible.

Count one resolved casting or activation, not each target or damage die. Ordinary magic outside capture remains ordinary magic. Unknown capture must first provide an observable harmless tell; the first unannounced use cannot complete catastrophe. Enemy cultists and public utilities obey the same conditions, so a party without casters still faces a real incursion.

Provide a discoverable grounding kit occupying one gear slot. A prepared kit safely shunts one qualifying activation, then requires one exploration turn to reset outside an active collector boundary. Retuning and physical disconnection offer further counterplay. Fixed-schedule utility demands make shutting off a device a rescue/provision problem, not an arbitrary class tax.

**Monster jobs.** Each anomaly has one committed dependency: a creature protected only while a specific sound reaches it, a thing recurring at matching spell emissions, or an intruder displaced when local gravity is restored. Evidence exposes that dependency before an unavoidable confrontation. Repair or isolation can defeat the encounter without HP depletion; ordinary attacks still have their declared effects.

**Three acts:** correlate useful power with emissions → choose, ground, or replace hazardous utilities → disconnect or stabilise through the conjunction.

**Success A — Disconnect and discharge:** isolate every collector from the invocation network and safely discharge its stored harmonics, leaving the local invocation circuit inoperable. **Success B — Restore separation:** complete the counter-pattern and sustain it throughout the committed conjunction, permanently excluding this resonance from the region even with collectors intact. Neither requires defeating Azathoth in combat. The finale can be a repair/defence operation under changing physical conditions.

**Failure/neglect:** enemy castings and utility activations supply only their declared harmonics. Hostile success requires all four linked collectors sounding the required pattern during conjunction with separation incomplete. No arbitrary spell total substitutes for that predicate. Early success leaves useful machinery, stewardship choices, and the Toll of people lost to failed physical laws.

## Review and Future Runtime Acceptance

For each of the nine engines, run a paper walkthrough of: opening expedition, recurring player choice, monster objective, failed intervention, both independent victories, early success, and aftermath. Check each alternate patron separately. Prices and numerical defaults above are authored ASH proposals requiring playtesting, not rules attributed to Cthulhu Wars or Shadowdark.

Future adapters must persist their own records, evidence provenance, accepted prices, elapsed time, monster assets, and individual success predicates. Shared infrastructure may store facts, but must not convert them into one corruption track or a universal Doom clock. Player projections conceal unknown actors and future locations while exposing the effects and choices the party has actually learned.

Minimum verification cases:

- Remedy A wins with remedy B false, and B wins with A false, for every patron supported by an adapter.
- Declining a gift, dream, role, service, or bargain leaves a feasible class-independent route forward.
- Tactical victories preserve saved people, spent enemy resources, and earned reprieves.
- Discoveries reduce uncertainty; reloads cannot redraw reserves, change a mandate, or move a threshold without an actual event.
- Winter access works when its stated preparation is met; a spent reserve cannot discharge twice.
- Completed service delivery, role invocation, harmonic capture, gift consumption, and victory rewards cannot duplicate on retries.
- All ordinary parties can use the important counterplay; no required solution presumes a wizard, a compulsory PC sacrifice, or simultaneous split-party control.

The faction economies, movement, and combat exceptions informing this revision were compared against the [Cthulhu Wars Omega rulebook](https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/6b/8b/42-cthulhu-wars-rulebook.pdf) and [Petersen Games rules FAQ](https://petersengames.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/48000952254-cthulhu-wars-rules-faq).
