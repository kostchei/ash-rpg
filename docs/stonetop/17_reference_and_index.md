**Mediography**


**Immediate game influences**
The following works directly inspired _Stonetop_ ’s mechanics, techniques, setting, themes, and approach
to information design.

_Dungeon World_, Adam Koebel & Sage LaTorra, Sage Kobold Productions, 2012

the Coast, 2008
