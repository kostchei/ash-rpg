**The Fox**

_The elders tell a story about Fox, who knows lots of tricks, and Hedgehog, who knows one: how to_
_curl up into a ball when there’s danger. Fox can’t eat Hedgehog when he’s all curled up, so in the story_
_Fox goes hungry. But you’re not that Fox, and this is no story. You want that Hedgehog? Go get a knife._



**Background** <u>(Choose 1)</u>

**THE NATURAL**
You grew up around here, and always picked things
up quickly. Reading and numbers, sure, but more.
Hide and seek. Throwing stones. Climbing. Fighting. Whatever you tried, you were good at it. As
good as anyone else, if not better.

Sure, you’ve got a reputation for bending the rules.
Playing dirty. But why play if you don’t play to win,
right? And who do they come to when there’s a
problem needs solving? You, that’s who.

When you **_Seek Insight_**, you may roll +INT
instead of +WIS and add “What opportunity does
<u>no one else see?” to the list of possible questions.</u>

**A LIFE OF CRIME**
You’re new to Stonetop, having left behind a...
_colorful_ past. How did you get into that life? Why
and how did you get out? Who and what did you
leave behind?

Regardless, these people have taken you in. Time to
lead an honest life, right?

You start with either Burgle or Light Fingers (your
choice) as an extra move, and either burglar tools or
a hidden stash (your choice) as an additional special
<u>possession. Go mark them now.</u>

**THE PRODIGAL RETURNED**
You left long ago, travelling far and living by your
wits. Why did you leave? What deeds do you boast
of, and which do you regret?

You always longed to return to Stonetop, and return
you have. You’re a bit of a celebrity now, and you’ve
got friends (or close enough) strewn about the
known world.

When you **_declare that you know someone outside of_**
**_Stonetop_**, someone who can help, name them and
roll +CHA: **on a 10+**, yeah, they can help (tell us
why they’re willing); **on a 7-9**, they can help but
pick 1 from the list below; **on a 6-**, the GM chooses
1 and then some.

They still hold a grudge
They’re going to need something from you first
They swore off this sort of thing long ago
You can’t exactly, y’know, trust them

**<u>Moves</u>** <u>You start with Ambush OR Skill at Arms; Danger Sense OR Perceptive; and 1 of your choice.</u>



**ALL IN THE WRIST** _a few left_ _out_
Any knife or dagger gets the _thrown_ tag in your
hands. Also, you keep a few iron throwing blades
( _near_ ) on you; they don’t take up space in your
<u>inventory. Reset your ammo whenever you Outfit.</u>

**AMBUSH**
When you **_get the drop on a nearby foe_**, you can deal
your damage or opt to roll +DEX: **on a 10+**, deal your
damage and pick 2; **on a 7-9**, deal damage and pick 1:

Deal +1d4 damage
Stop them from making noise/raising an alarm
Slip away before they can react
Create an opportunity; you or an ally gains
<u>advantage on the next move to act on it</u>

**BURGLE**
When you **_sneak off on your own into a dangerous_**
**_place_**, roll +INT: **on a 7+**, you make it back, and the
GM says where you got to and what you learned.
Then, **on a 10+**, also pick 2; **on a 7-9**, also pick 1:

You got away clean, rousing no suspicion
You swiped something valuable (GM’s choice)
You set something up to exploit on your return
Ask a Seek Insight question about what you saw

**On a 6-**, you either make it back but with trouble in
<u>tow, or you’re missing in action (your call).</u>

**CATLIKE**
When you **_carry a light load and act with care_**, you
move silently. When you **_hide in shadows or dark-_**
**_ness_**, you remain unseen until you draw attention to
<u>yourself, move positions, or attack.</u>

**DABBLER**
(Requires level 2+ and the Fox)
Each time you take this move, choose a move from
the Heavy, Marshal, Ranger, or Seeker playbooks
for which you otherwise qualify. (You can’t take
Improved Stat or Superior Stat.)

**DANGER SENSE**
You can always ask the GM, “Is there an ambush or
trap here?” If they say “yes,” roll +INT: **on a 10+**, ask
the GM both of the questions below; **on a 7-9**, ask
1; **either way**, gain advantage on your next roll to
act on the answer(s).

What will trigger the ambush or trap?
What will happen once it’s triggered?

**On a 6-**, don’t mark XP; you know there’s a trap or
<u>ambush, but nothing bad happens just yet.</u>

**FREE RUNNING**
When you **_carry a light load and move with speed_**
**_and grace_**, gain advantage on any move to surmount
<u>or bypass a physical obstacle.</u>

**IMPROVED STAT**
Each time you take this move, increase one of your
<u>stats by 1 (to a max of +2).</u>

**IRRESISTIBLE**
When you **_interact with someone_**, you can ask their
player if they find you attractive and get an honest
answer (usually “yes”).

When you **_Persuade by using your considerable_**
**_<u>charms as leverage</u>_** <u>, you have advantage.</u>

**LAUGH AT DANGER**
When you **_are about to roll +CON and you make a_**
**_joke about the adversity you face_**, you can roll
<u>+CHA instead.</u>

**LIGHT FINGERS**
When you **_perform sleight of hand on an unwary_**
**_mark_**, you succeed and no one’s the wiser. If you’re
being watched, roll +DEX: **on a 10+**, you succeed
and no one’s the wiser; **on a 7-9**, you succeed OR
no one’s the wiser (your choice).

**PERCEPTIVE**
When you **_Seek Insight_**, you may ask 1 additional
question. Even on a 6-, you can ask 1 question
<u>(though you might not like how you learn the answer).</u>

**RAPIER WIT**
When you **_pierce an NPC’s pride with a well-placed_**
**_quip_**, they must do 1 (their choice):

Attack, doing +1d4 damage if they hit but giving
you advantage on your next roll against them

Stoop to your level and respond in kind
Spend a few moments fuming, sputtering, or
<u>controlling their temper</u>

**SKILL AT ARMS**
When you **_wield a weapon with speed and grace_**, roll
<u>+DEX to Clash (instead of +STR).</u>

**PARRY & RIPOSTE**
(Requires Skill at Arms)
When you **_Defend with a weapon that you can_**
**_wield quickly_**, you can spend 1 Readiness to both
halve an attack’s effects/damage and strike back
at the attacker (deal your damage with disadvan<u>tage), instead of spending 1 Readiness for each.</u>

**SILVER TONGUED**
When you **_use words to avoid suspicion or trouble_**,
roll +CHA: **on a 10+**, hold 3 Nerve; **on a 7-9**, hold
1 Nerve. You may spend Nerve, 1-for-1, to:

Move about or maneuver unchallenged
Withstand direct scrutiny or questioning
<u>Direct suspicion or attention elsewhere</u>

**UNDER YOUR SKIN**
When you **_engage an NPC in conversation_**, you can
ask the GM 1 of these and get an honest answer:

What are they expecting me to do?
What, in general, are they trying to hide?
What do they want to happen?

your damage, avoid your enemy’s attack, and im<u>press/embarrass/overawe your foes.</u>

**CHEAP SHOT**
(Requires level 6+ and Ambush)
When you **_Ambush with a_** **hand** **_weapon_**, you have
<u>advantage on your damage roll.</u>

**EYE ON THE DOOR**
(Requires level 6+)
When **_you and your allies need to get out of here_**,
name your escape route and roll +INT: **on a 10+**,
you’re gone; **on a 7-9**, you can stay or go, but if you
go, it costs you—the GM will tell you what (or

**PANTS ON FIRE**
(Requires level 6+)
When you **_Defy Danger, Persuade, or Interfere by_**
**_being deceitful_**, you have advantage.

When another move (like Seek Insight) allows a
player to ask you a question, you can opt not to
<u>answer.</u>

**SECOND INTENT**
(Requires level 6+, Parry & Riposte, and Ambush)
When you **_Defend and spend 1 Readiness to Parry_**
**_<u>& Riposte</u>_** <u>, also pick 1 option from the Ambush list.</u>

**SLIPPERY**
(Requires level 6+)
When you **_roll to escape being caught or controlled_**,
treat a 6- as a 7-9. On a 12+, say how you turn the
<u>tables or use the circumstances to your advantage.</u>

**SUPERIOR STAT**
(Requires level 6+)
Increase one of your stats by +1 (to a max of +3).

**Introductions**
Wait here for everyone else. When everyone’s ready,
take turns introducing your characters. When
**_someone reveals something and you want to know_**
**_more_**, ask them about it. When **_someone asks you a_**
**_<u>question</u>_** <u>, answer it truthfully.</u>

**<u>1</u>** On your first turn, **introduce yourself** by name,
<u>pronouns, background, origin, and appearance.</u>

**<u>2</u>** On your second turn, **describe your special**
**possessions** and how you contribute to the
<u>village (beyond working the fields).</u>

**<u>3</u>** On your third turn, **tell us your tall tales** . Feel
free to embellish and exaggerate to the other
<u>players, but always answer the GM truthfully.</u>

**<u>4</u>** On your next turn, **answer one of the following**,
naming one or more NPCs who live in Stonetop.

Who is your closest kin?


Who holds the reins to your heart?


Whose respect means the world to you?


To whom do you owe a debt that cannot
be repaid?


**<u>5</u>** Go around again. Answer another question
<u>from 4, or pass. When everyone has passed, go on.</u>

**<u>6</u>** On your next turn, **ask your fellow PCs one of**
**these** . When others ask you, answer as you like.

Which one of you joined me in my latest hijinx?


Which one of you brings your problems to me?


Which one of you saved my bacon, mor’n once?


Which one of you trusts me not one bit?


**<u>7</u>** Go around again. Ask another question from
<u>6, or pass. When everyone has passed, go on.</u>

**<u>8</u>** Add your home to the steading playbook.
When everyone is done, let spring burst forth!
