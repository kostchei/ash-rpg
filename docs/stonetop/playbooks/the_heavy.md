**The Heavy**

_These are good people. Hard-working,_ **_honest_** _. They look out for each other. But sometimes,_
_looking out for each other ain’t enough. Sometimes, good people need someone to stick up for_
_them. Someone who’s not afraid to get a little bloody. To get heavy. Yeah, someone like you._



**Background** <u>(Choose 1)</u>

**SHERIFF**
You keep order in Stonetop and protect it from
outside threats. It might not be anything official,
but everyone knows you’ve got a cool head and the
weight to back up your words.

When you **_bark an order or warning_**, roll +CHA:
**on a 7+**, they must choose 1:

Do what you say
Dig in/take cover/flee
Attack you

**On a 10+**, you can sense which one they’re about to
<u>do and act first if you like; gain advantage if you do.</u>

**BLOOD-SOAKED PAST**
You left behind a life of violence and a name
mothers used to scare their children. For whatever
reason, the people of Stonetop took you (back?) in
and treat you like one of their own.

When you **_Persuade using violence or threats against_**
**_someone who knows your black reputation_**, you can
roll +STR instead of +CHA. Also, if you take the
Formidable move, you can choose to roll +CON
instead of +CHA.

When you **_fight to kill without mercy or hesitation_**,
<u>you deal +1d4 damage.</u>

**STORM-MARKED**
You’ve been touched by Tor (Rain-maker, Thunderhead, Slayer-of-Beasts!) and bear runic markings
similar to those etched into the Stone. When did
the marks manifest? Are they a symbol of your
strength, speed, and courage? Or their source?

You start with the Storm Markings major arcanum.
Mark one of the boxes on the front of the Storm
Markings sheet, and describe here the time you
were struck by lightning and walked away unharmed:

**ARMORED**
When you **_carry a shield_**, mark only <sup>**4**</sup> (instead of

**4** **4** ). Also, you can ignore the _cumbersome_ tag on
any armor you wear.



If you take this move at the start of play, add an

iron hauberk, bronze cuirass, or
scale coat to your inventory (all are 2 armor,
_<u>warm</u>_ <u>,</u> _<u>cumbersome</u>_ <u>).</u>

**BATTLE JOY**
When you **_spill blood—yours or another’s—and_**
**_lose yourself in battle_**, you ignore fear, pain, mindcontrol, and the effects of debilities as long as you
keep fighting.

When **_the action stops_**, roll +CON: **on a 10+**, that
was a rush, regain 1d4 HP; **on a 7-9**, you’re winded
and out of it, but you’ll be fine with a few minutes’
<u>rest;</u> **<u>on a 6-</u>** <u>, mark a debility but don’t mark XP.</u>

**BERSERKER**
(Requires Battle Joy)
While **_in your Battle Joy_**, add the _area_ tag to your
melee attacks, lashing out at anyone nearby
(friend and foe alike). Roll damage separately for
<u>each target.</u>

**CARVED OUT OF WOOD**
<u>Increase your max HP by 4.</u>

**4** **DANGEROUS**
(Requires the Heavy)
<u>When you</u> **_<u>deal your damage</u>_** <u>, you have advantage.</u>



**FORMIDABLE**
When you **_wade into battle_**, you can choose to roll
+CHA: **on a 10+**, both; **on a 7-9**, pick 1:



Lesser foes will quail, hesitate, or flee before you.
Doughty foes will focus on you, seeing you as
the greatest threat.
**On a 6-**, pick 1 but ask the GM what you’ve missed.



**FROSTY**
When you **_Defy Danger by keeping calm and carry-_**
**_ing on_**, **on a 10+** you can also ask the GM a question
that you could ask when Seeking Insight. You have
<u>advantage on your next move to act on the answer.</u>

**GUARDIAN**
When you **_Defend_**, hold 1 extra Readiness. Even on
<u>a 6-, hold 1 Readiness (plus whatever the GM says).</u>

**IMPROVED STAT**
Each time you take this move, increase one of your
<u>stats by 1 (to a max of +2).</u>

**INTIMIDATING**
When you **_Persuade using violence or threats_**, you
<u>have advantage.</u>

**4** **HARD TO KILL**
When you **_are at Death’s Door,_** you can roll +CON
or +nothing (your choice). **On a 7-9**, you can mark
<u>a debility of your choice to regain 1 HP.</u>

**UNSTOPPABLE**
(Requires Hard to Kill)
When you **_are reduced to 0 HP in battle_**, you can
keep fighting. Each time you take damage while
at 0 HP, mark 1. If you would regain HP while
fighting, clear one mark instead.

When you **_stop fighting_**, roll for Death’s Door
with a -1 penalty for each circle marked. If you
<u>survive, clear all your circles.</u>

**MUSCLEBOUND**
(Requires Strength +2 or higher)
When you **_make a hand-to-hand or thrown attack_**,
it’s _forceful_ and _messy_ . If it would already be _forceful_
and/or _messy_, it’s even more so.

**PAYBACK**
When you **_deal damage to a foe that has harmed you_**
**_<u>or one of your allies</u>_** <u>, deal +1d4 damage.</u>

**RELENTLESS**
When you **_Clash and your foe survives_**, you gain
<u>advantage the next time you Clash with them.</u>

**SEASONED WARRIOR**
(Requires level 2+ and the Heavy)
Take a move from the Fox, Marshal, Ranger, or
Seeker playbooks, for which you otherwise qualify.
You can pick from a different playbook each time.
<u>(You can’t pick Improved Stat or Superior Stat.)</u>

**SITUATIONAL AWARENESS**
When you **_Seek Insight_**, add the following to the
list of questions you can ask:

Who or what here is the biggest threat?
What is my enemy’s true position?
What here can I use as a weapon?

When **_a fight breaks out_**, ask the GM 1 question
<u>that you could ask when Seeking Insight.</u>

**UNCANNY REFLEXES**
When you **_are unarmored and carrying a normal or_**
**_light load_**, you impose disadvantage on any damage
<u>you take that you could dodge or roll with.</u>

**UNFETTERED**
When you **_are subject to physical or mental restraint_**,
you may mark a debility to immediately break free
<u>of that restraint.</u>

**TERROR ON THE FIELD**
When you **_reduce a foe to 0 HP_**, describe how you
take them out. If you fell them in a particularly
brutal or impressive manner, their allies are
impressed, dismayed, or frightened and respond
accordingly.

**CUT FROM GRANITE**
(Requires level 6+ and Carved Out of Wood)
Gain +1 armor (stacks with other sources) and

**MIGHTY THEWS**
(Requires level 6+ and Musclebound)

**NEMESIS**
(Requires level 6+ and Relentless)
When you **_Clash and your foe survives_**, all of your
<u>future attacks against them do +1d6 damage.</u>

**STEADFAST GUARDIAN**
(Requires level 6+ and Guardian)
While you **_hold Readiness (from Defend)_**, you
can always suffer the damage/effects of an attack
instead of your ward; no need to spend Readiness,
<u>you can just do it.</u>

**STONE COLD**
(Requires level 6+ and Frosty)

**_<u>keeping calm and carrying on</u>_** <u>, treat a 6- as a 7-9.</u>

Just about everyone here talks about the time you…
(pick 1 or 2)



… drove off a thunder drake that got too close
to town.

… killed that hagr in the Foothills.
… slew a dozen crinwin in one battle.
… tossed those adventurers out of town.
… bested Ivan, the scariest bandit in Brennan’s
gang, the Claws.

… dragged yourself (and another?) into town,
bleeding from a dozen wounds.



… were struck by lightning and woke up
covered in these marks.



But folks are less keen to discuss… (pick 1 or 2)

that blood.

for you.



… the shouting matches between you and
your love.

have it coming.

those weird marks in the dirt.



What keeps you up at night? (pick 1 or 2)

Wondering what Brennan’s up to, now that he’s

**Introductions**
Wait here for everyone else. When everyone’s ready,
take turns introducing your characters. When
**_someone reveals something and you want to know_**
**_more_**, ask them about it. When **_someone asks you a_**
**_<u>question</u>_** <u>, answer it truthfully.</u>

**<u>1</u>** On your first turn, **introduce yourself** by name,
<u>pronouns, background, origin, and appearance.</u>

**<u>2</u>** On your second turn, **describe your special**
**possessions** and how you contribute to the
<u>village (beyond working the fields).</u>

**<u>3</u>** On your third turn, **tell us about your history**
**<u>of violence</u>** <u>, and what keeps you up at night.</u>

**<u>4</u>** On your next turn, **answer one of the following**,
naming one or more NPCs who live in Stonetop.

Who is your closest kin?


Who is your lover/spouse/betrothed?


Who most needs/deserves your protection?


Whose forgiveness do you strive to earn?


**<u>5</u>** Go around again. Answer another question
<u>from 4, or pass. When everyone has passed, go on.</u>

**<u>6</u>** On your next turn, **ask your fellow PCs one of**
**these** . When others ask you, answer as you like.

Which one of you once dragged me home,
bleeding and unconscious?


Which one of you can I trust to always have
my back?


Which one of you has stayed my hand?


Which one of you has traded blows with me?


**<u>7</u>** Go around again. Ask another question from 6,
<u>or pass. When everyone has passed, go on.</u>

**<u>8</u>** Add your home to the steading playbook.
When everyone is done, let spring burst forth!



Dark visions of things moving in the earth,
restless, whispering, and hungry.



The question of who’ll look after your family
when you get yourself killed.



The worry that they’ll all learn the truth about
you, sooner or later.
