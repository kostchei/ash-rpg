**The Lightbearer**

_Imagine yourself and your kin in a cave lit by a single torch, entranced by shadow puppet_
_stories. Imagine realizing there is a greater truth, and stepping out of the cave into the true_
_Light of day. Would you not bring that Light back into the darkness, to set your people free?_



**Background** <u>(Choose 1)</u>

**AUSPICIOUS BIRTH**
You were born in Stonetop, and that birth was
marked by the God of Light. You were born during
an eclipse, perhaps, or under the light of a bright
new star? Maybe you bear a sun-shaped birthmark?

Whatever the sign, your connection to Helior was
clear early on. You’ve a place of honor in Stonetop,
though it’d be a lie to say you don’t make some uneasy.

When **_one of your moves has you mark a debility_**,
you may mark this background’s circle instead, to
no ill effect. Clear it when you Make Camp
<u>or Convalesce.</u>

**ITINERANT MYSTIC**
They think of you as a self-important kook who
comes through now and again, speaking in riddles
and playing tricks with the light. Sure, they know
there’s something holy about you, but it’s not like
you’re a priest or anything. Priests talk sense.

When you **_go off a-wandering_**, hold 1 Enigma if
you’re gone for days, 2 if you’re gone for weeks, or 3
if you’re gone for months. At the very start of play,
hold 3 Enigma. Spend Enigma 1-for-1 to:

where you are needed, fully Outfitted

**SOUL ON FIRE**
You once led a worldly life, full of fear and doubt,
base pleasures and petty grudges. But something
happened. Injury, illness, a brush with death. Or just
a moment of such profound misery and selfloathing that you thought you could fall no further.

There, in the dark, Helior’s light shined upon you,
igniting in your soul, lifting you and filling you with
a profound sense of purpose.

When you **_Persuade a group by preaching charity,_**
**_mercy, and hope and roll a 7+_**, aside from the usual
effect, choose 1:

Your name and your message spread

**<u>Moves</u>** <u>You start with Consecrated Flame and Invoke the Sun God, plus 1 more of your choice.</u>



**A CANDLE AGAINST THE DARK**
When you **_wield a holy light but go otherwise un-_**
**_<u>armed</u>_** <u>, you have 2 Armor.</u>

**LUMINOUS SHIELD**
(Requires A Candle Against the Dark)
When you **_brandish a holy light to turn aside an_**
**_attack against body, mind, or soul_**, roll +CHA: **on**
**a 10+**, the attack is deflected and, if the attacker
is in range of your light, they are briefly blinded;
**on a 7-9**, the attack is deflected but your holy
light flickers and dims, threatening to go out;
**on a 6-**, your light snuffs out and the attack is
<u>unimpeded.</u>

**ALL IS ILLUMINATED**
When you **_look closely on another and see their soul_**
**_laid bare_**, roll +WIS: **on a 10+**, ask their player 1
question from the list below, plus “And what would
make them feel loved, beautiful, or worthy?”; **on a**
**7-9**, ask them 1 question from the list. In any case,
they must answer truthfully.

Of what are they most ashamed?
What do they most desire or covet?
What hope have they abandoned?
<u>Who or what is most precious to them?</u>

**AND BEHOLD A PALE HORSE**
When you **_spend the night gazing into a flame_**, ask
the GM to reveal an impending doom or grim
portent that will come to pass unless you intervene,
<u>and how you might yet do so.</u>

**4** **CONSECRATED FLAME**
When you **_whisper words of consecration to a flame_**,
the flame casts a holy light. Holy light is uncomfortable for creatures of darkness to look upon,
but does no true harm. The holy light lasts until
the flame goes out or until you consecrate another
flame, whichever comes first.

**122**



**FIRE WITHIN**
When you **_are in darkness_**, you are able to see by the
light of your inner fire. When you **_take damage from_**
**_<u>cold or fire</u>_** <u>, reduce that damage by 2.</u>

**GUIDING LIGHT**
When you **_lead one or more NPCs through danger_**,
roll +CHA: **on a 10+**, you all make it through
(Helior be praised); **on a 7-9**, the GM will tell you
<u>what’s required to get everyone through safely.</u>

**HELIOR’S UNBLINKING EYE**
When you **_stare into the sun long enough to lose your_**
**_vision_**, name a person or place that you know and
roll +WIS: **on a 10+**, you briefly glimpse your subject as if from a great height, and choose 2 from the
list below; **on a 7-9**, you briefly glimpse your subject
as if from a great height, and choose 1:

The glimpse lasts as long as you wish
Your point of view shifts to very close range
<u>You recover your vision quickly</u>

**IMPROVED STAT**
Each time you take this move, increase one of your
<u>stats by 1 (to a max of +2).</u>

**4** **INVOKE THE SUN GOD**
When you **_imbue a holy light with Helior’s power_**,
choose an Invocation you know and roll +WIS: **on**
**a 10+**, it works as described but you must choose 1
consequence from the list below; **on a 7-9**, it works
as described, but you and the GM each choose 1.

The Invocation has its _reduced_ effect
The effort taxes you; mark a debility
The light is snuffed out when the Invocation is
complete, its fuel consumed

You must bask in sunlight for an hour or so
before using that Invocation again

See the **Invocations** insert for details.

**KEEP THE HOME-FIRES BURNING**
When you **_build a camp fire and sprinkle it with ash_**
**_from your own hearth,_** anyone who Makes Camp
with you is free from nightmares or bad dreams and
<u>recovers (extra) HP equal to your CHA.</u>

**LAMPLIGHTER**
When you **_whisper to a flammable object_** (a torch, a
<u>wick, kindling, etc.), it ignites in moments.</u>

**PIETY**
When you **_spend at least an hour in proper worship_**
**_to Helior,_** hold 1 Blessing. Other faithful PCs who
partake in this worship also hold 1 Blessing. At any
time, you can spend 1 Blessing to add +1 to a roll
<u>you just made in pursuit of a righteous cause.</u>

**PURIFYING FLAMES**
When you **_wield a holy light against a creature of_**
**_darkness_**, it counts as a weapon (d10 damage, _hand_,
_close_, _area_, 2 piercing) and you can choose to roll
<u>+WIS to Clash.</u>

**RADIANT COUNTENANCE**
When you **_give someone your fond attention_**, you
can then Persuade them with advantage. If they are
a follower, you can instead choose to Strengthen
<u>Your Bond (as if you paid their cost).</u>

**RISE LIKE THE SUN**
When you **_draw attention to yourself by word or deed_**,
roll +CHA: **on a 10+**, everyone turns and looks, and
you hold their gaze as long as you keep giving them
<u>reason to look;</u> **<u>on a 7-9</u>** <u>, everyone turns and looks.</u>

**SPRING’S FIRST THAW**
When you **_spend time (an hour at least, maybe more)_**
**_seeking to stir hope, kindness, or mercy in an NPC_**,
roll +CHA: **on a 10+**, you light a fire deep within
them and affect a lasting change; **on a 7-9**, you
kindle goodness in their heart for now, but they will
eventually return to their old ways; **on a 6-**, their
heart hardens and, whatever else the GM says, you
can’t use this move on them again.

**BURN TWICE AS BRIGHT**
(Requires level 6+ and Invoke the Sun God)
When you **_Invoke the Sun God_**, you may mark a
debility to use 2 Invocations at once. Roll once, and
<u>apply any consequences to both Invocations.</u>

**EMPOWERED INVOCATIONS**
(Requires level 6+ and Invoke the Sun God)
When you Invoke the Sun God, you can choose
an extra consequence before you roll. If you do, the
<u>Invocation has its</u> _<u>empowered</u>_ <u>effect.</u>

**GLORIOUS SERVANT**
(Requires level 6+ and Invoke the Sun God)
When you **_Invoke the Sun God and roll a 10+_**, you
need not choose a consequence; **on a 7-9**, you
<u>choose a consequence but the GM does not.</u>

**HUNGRY FLAMES**
(Requires level 6+ and Purifying Flames)
When you **_deal damage with a holy light_**, you deal
+1d6 damage and your target is engulfed in holy
<u>light and flames.</u>

**LIGHT, MORE LIGHT**
(Requires level 6+ and Consecrated Flame)
When you **_consecrate a flame_**, it burns brighter than
normal. A rushlight or candle illuminates to _reach_
range, an oil lamp, lantern, or torch out to _near_
<u>range, and a bullseye lantern out to</u> _<u>far</u>_ <u>range.</u>

**SUPERIOR STAT**
(Requires level 6+)
<u>Increase one of your stats by +1 (to a max of +3).</u>

**WIELDER OF THE WHITE FLAME**
(Requires level 6+ and Invoke the Sun God)
When you **_channel Helior’s essence into an object you_**
**_carry_**, roll +WIS: **on a 10+**, it ignites with a white
flame that casts a holy light ( _reach_, _area_ ) and burns
neither you nor the object, and you may Invoke the
Sun God right now as if you rolled a 10+; **on a 7-9**,
it ignites with a white flame that casts a holy light
( _reach_, _area_ ) and burns neither you nor the object.
