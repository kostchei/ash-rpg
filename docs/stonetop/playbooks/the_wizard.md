# The Wizard

*The common folk believe that words are merely breath on the wind, soon cooled and forgotten. They tell their children that iron reaps what plowshares sow, and that all men bleed the same red mud.*

*They are fools. You have held crumbling vellum in trembling hands and deciphered the living geometry of the ancient world. You know that true spells are not passive thoughts—they are alien, squirming equations of star-fire and cosmic order, crammed kicking and screaming into the dark corners of your skull. Holding a formula in your head makes your temples throb, your sleep uneasy, and your mortal frame grow cold.*

*When you speak the final syllable and open the valve of your mind, the magic tears free with a rush of ozone and winter wind, leaving your brain ringing and hollow until you can consult your grimoire again. Stonetop has barley and spears, but when things that laugh at spears crawl out of the deep barrows, they come knocking at your door, praying you prepared the right miracle before the sun came up.*

## Page 1: Origins, Instincts & Identity

### Background (Choose 1)

#### THE BATTLE-HARDENED (Tenser's Path)
You got tired of cowering in the rear rank with four hit points while your companions bled in the mud. You learned your formulas in the stench of siege trenches, strapping boiled leather over your woolen robes and picking up a notched arming blade. When the shield-wall breaks, you don't run—you step into the breach, teeth bared, holding the line with muscle, steel, and raw force.

* **Iron Constitution:** Your base HP is **16 + CON** (instead of 12 + CON), and your Base Damage is **1d8** (instead of 1d4).
* **Armored Casting:** You can wear light and medium armor (up to Armor 1) and wield martial weapons without hindering your casting or mental focus.
* **Wade into the Fray:** When you rush into melee armed with steel and an active spell of warding or force, you deal **+1d4 damage** and take **+1 Armor forward**.

#### THE GREY SAGE (Elminster's Folk)
You wander the dirt tracks of the frontier wrapped in patched, travel-stained wool, smelling of woodsmoke and dried sweetleaf. You sit on horse-troughs, trade gossip with the washerwomen, tell riddles to wide-eyed children, and share dry barley biscuits with beggars. To the village, you are just an eccentric, harmless old scholar. Only you know what horrors sleep under the hills, and what ancient, uncorrupted fire smolders behind your tired eyes.

* **Folk Wisdom & Friendly Counsel:** When you **_Persuade_** by sitting down, lighting your clay pipe, and offering patient counsel, old folklore, or quiet common sense, roll **+INT** instead of +CHA.
* **Hearth Cantrips:** You command the small, comforting arts of the world at will without preparation or rolling: spark a hearth fire with a snap of your fingers, mend a torn cloak, clean muddy boots, illuminate a dark pipe, or whisper a secret message on the breeze across a crowded mead-hall.
* **The Ancient Ember:** Once per session, when an ally at your hearth or camp is struck down, you may invoke an elder word of grace to grant them **+2 Armor forward** or stabilize their dying breath.

#### THE HIGH MAGISTER (Mordenkainen's Circle)
You were bred in high collegiate towers where magic is treated not as folk superstition, but as an exact science and a high game of statecraft. You carry yourself with aristocratic grace and an icy, calculating intellect. You came to the frontier not out of curiosity, but to establish a redoubt, monitor ancient thresholds, and ensure that no petty warlord or ravening beast upsets the delicate equilibrium of the world.

* **The Unseen Retinue:** You are attended at all times by an *Unseen Servant*—a silent, invisible telekinetic force that carries your traveling chests (up to 3 weight), keeps your robes spotless, serves your tea in fine porcelain, and maintains a vigilant psychic watch over your study while you sleep.
* **Architect of Balance:** When you **_Seek Insight_** regarding rulers, political rivalries, ancient bloodlines, or the balance of power, roll with **advantage**. On a 10+, you may also ask the GM: *"Who holds the true balance of power here, and what would it take to tip it?"*
* **Aura of Authority:** Common folk and mercenaries recognize the bearing of a High Magister; when you speak with cold arcane certainty, they hesitate before daring to defy you.

### Starting Moves

#### THE LIVING FORMULAE (Vancian Inscription)
Your mind is an iron lattice holding living, dangerous geometric equations. You do not cast spells from an endless well of mana; you **cram specific workings into your memory** through grueling study, and release them into the world when needed.

When you spend an hour in quiet, uninterrupted study with your grimoire by the campfire or hearth (usually at dawn or nightfall), you may inscribe formulas into your mind. You can hold a number of **Spell Circles** in your head equal to your **INT modifier + your Level** (minimum 2).
* A 1st Circle working occupies 1 slot of mental space.
* A 2nd Circle working occupies 2 slots of mental space.
* A 3rd Circle working occupies 3 slots (and so on, up to the legendary 9th Circle at Master tier).

#### UNLEASH THE FORMULA (Cast a Spell)
When you speak the harsh elder syllables, trace the geometric angles in the air, and release a prepared formula from the cage of your mind, state your target and roll **+INT**:

* **On a 10+:** The formula bursts forth into reality with terrifying precision. Choose one:
  * *Retain the Pattern:* You maintain an iron grip on the magic; the formula remains lodged in your mind to cast again.
  * *Burn the Pattern:* The formula tears out of your mind and vanishes until you study again, but the release is magnificent: **double the spell’s range, duration, or area of effect**, or add **+1d6 damage** to its impact.
* **On a 7–9:** The spell manifests and achieves its effect, but the strain of holding cosmic order in a mortal brain takes its toll. Choose one:
  * *Memory Purged:* The formula burns away; your mind feels hollow and cold, and the spell cannot be cast again until you re-study your grimoire.
  * *Mortal Strain:* You force the pattern to stay, but your body reels from the backlash: take **1d4 damage** (ignoring armor) or mark the *Confused* or *Shaken* debility.
  * *Arcane Flare:* The magic bleeds violently into the surroundings: a deafening thunderclap, a blinding flash, a foul stench of sulfur and burning ozone, or alerting nearby predators and spirits to your location.
* **On a 6-:** Mark XP. The formula collapses inside your skull or violently twists as it escapes. The spell fails or strikes wildly, and the GM makes as hard a move as they like.

#### THE PRICELESS GRIMOIRE
You carry your life’s work: a massive, heavy (2 weight), brass-cornered, locked grimoire bound in treated wyrm-hide or thick bull-leather. Inside its vellum pages are written the complex astronomical tables, alchemical inks, and elder runes of your craft.

* **Paranoid Custody:** If your grimoire is lost, soaked in water, burned, or stolen, your power is crippled. You retain only the formulas currently inscribed in your skull. You cannot prepare any new spells until you recover your book or spend seasons of labor and fortunes in gold reconstructing it in a distant collegiate library.
* **Repertoire:** You begin play with **three 1st Circle spells** and **one 2nd Circle spell** inscribed in your grimoire.

#### ARCANE SCRUTINY
When you take time to inspect an unknown rune, an ancient maker-monolith, a magical barrier, or an enchanted curio, roll **+INT**:
* **On a 10+:** The GM reveals its true nature: what power animates it, what danger it poses, and the exact condition, keyword, or counter-rite needed to safely trigger, bypass, or dispel it.
* **On a 7–9:** You glean its basic purpose and the general nature of its trigger, but unraveling its full mystery requires a rare component, dangerous experimentation, or significant time.
* **On a 6-:** You misinterpret the arcane geometry, or accidentally trigger its defensive wards.

### The Starting Grimoire

#### 1st Circle Workings (Subtle & Decisive)
* **The Kinetic Dart (Magic Missile):** Three glowing bolts of blue force snap from your fingertips (*near, 1d4+1 damage each, ignores armor, strikes unerringly*).
* **The Slumber-Mist (Sleep):** A heavy, silver-grey haze rolls across an area (*near, area*). Living creatures of weak constitution or minor strength sink into a deep, supernatural slumber for minutes or hours.
* **The Aegis of Force (Shield):** An invisible, curved pane of telekinetic force hovers before you. You gain **+2 Armor** against attacks from the front and absolute immunity to force darts. Lasts for the encounter.
* **The Gentle Cadence (Charm Person):** Speak soothing mathematical cadences while making eye contact. A humanoid who hears your voice must roll with disadvantage or regard you as a dear, trusted friend and counselor until you or your allies do them harm.
* **The Floating Plane (Tenser’s Disk):** Conjure a circular horizontal plane of force 3 feet wide that hovers at waist height. It faithfully follows 6 feet behind you, carrying up to 10 weight of supplies, ore, or an injured companion for 1 hour.
* **The Witch-Light:** Cause an object you touch to radiate warm, clean daylight out to near range for several hours.

#### 2nd Circle Workings (Tactical Mastery)
* **The Gossamer Snare (Web):** Spray thick, sticky, fibrous strands of adhesive webbing across an opening or passage (*near, area*). Traps and restrains anyone caught within; highly flammable (burns for 2d4 fire damage if ignited).
* **The Veil of Unseeing (Invisibility):** Touch a creature or object. It disappears completely from sight until it attacks, casts a spell, or you dismiss the ward.
* **The Shifting Phantasm (Mirror Image):** Conjure 1d3 illusory duplicates of yourself that mimic your every movement. When an enemy attacks you, roll a die: on an even roll, the attack strikes a duplicate instead, causing it to pop like a soap bubble.
* **The Pyre-Lance (Scorching Ray):** Hurl two lances of blinding, white-hot alchemical flame (*near, 2d6 damage, fiery, messy*).

### Advanced Moves (Levels 2–5)

#### EXPANDED MIND-PALACE
Your mental lattice grows deeper and more resilient. You can hold **+2 additional Spell Circles** in your mind. *(You may take this move twice).*

#### SCRIBE SCROLL
When you spend a full day in quiet labor and expend 1 use of fine inks from your scriptorium, you can transfer a formula from your grimoire onto a vellum scroll (1 weight). Anyone holding the scroll can read it aloud to unleash the spell once without preparation or rolling.

#### COUNTER-MAGIC
When an enemy spellcaster, witch, or supernatural horror attempts to weave magic within your sight, you may immediately expend a prepared spell of equal or greater circle to unravel their working. Roll **+INT**:
* **On a 10+:** The enemy's spell unravels harmlessly into wisps of cold smoke.
* **On a 7–9:** The enemy's spell is broken, but the violently dispersed energy strikes you or an ally for 1d6 damage.

#### CIRCLES OF FIRE AND WIND (Circles 3 & 4)
You decipher and inscribe grander, more destructive workings into your grimoire. Choose two spells:
* **The Detonating Sphere (Fireball - 3rd Circle):** A bead of ruby light streaks from your finger and erupts in a roaring concussive explosion (*far, area, 2d8 damage, fiery, destructive*).
* **The Stroke of Thunder (Lightning Bolt - 3rd Circle):** A blinding arc of blue electricity tears through a line (*near/far, 2d8 damage, forceful, piercing 2*).
* **The Falcon’s Mantle (Fly - 3rd Circle):** The target rises into the air, soaring swift as a hawk for up to 1 hour.
* **The Winter Gale (Ice Storm - 4th Circle):** Driving hailstones and razor winds batter a huge area (*near/far, area, 2d6 damage, difficult terrain, deafening*).
* **The Beast-Shape (Polymorph - 4th Circle):** Reshape the flesh of a willing or helpless creature into the form of a beast for 1 hour.

#### TENSER’S TRANSFORMATION
*Requires: The Battle-Hardened origin*  
You can cast a special 3rd Circle working: your eyes glow with red fury, your muscles swell with unnatural vigor, and your skin hardens like boiled leather. For the remainder of the battle, you gain **+2 Armor**, your melee attacks deal **+1d8 damage**, and you cannot be frightened or knocked down. While transformed, you cannot cast any other spells.

#### MORDENKAINEN’S FAITHFUL HOUND
*Requires: The High Magister origin*  
You can cast a special 3rd Circle working: summon an invisible, phantom mastiff to stand vigil over an area (*near*). Only you can see it. It barks a mental alarm that wakes you instantly if any creature approaches within 30 feet, and it leaps upon intruders with spectral fangs (*1d8 damage, piercing 2*).

#### ELMINSTER’S SILVER FIRE
*Requires: The Grey Sage origin*  
Once per session, when your companions face despair or annihilation, you can unleash the ancient silver fire that slumbers in your blood. Unleash a beam of pure celestial/hearth flame (*near*): choose to either **restore 2d8 HP** to an ally and purge all curses/poisons, or strike an unnatural horror for **3d8 damage** (ignoring all armor and magical defenses).

### Master Moves (Levels 6–10)

#### MASTER OF THE NINTH CIRCLE (Mythic Workings)
You reach the terrifying summit of 1st Edition wizardry. Inscribing an 8th or 9th Circle working into your grimoire takes months of research; preparing one consumes your entire mind-palace for the day:
* **Mordenkainen’s Disjunction (9th Circle):** All magical wards, ongoing spells, barriers, and supernatural effects within near range are instantly obliterated and erased from existence. Magic items and arcana in the area must make a saving roll or be permanently drained of power, reduced to mundane brass and glass.
* **Meteor Swarm (9th Circle):** You reach into the heavens and pull down four incandescent falling stars (*far, colossal area, 4d10 damage, catastrophic, ignores armor*). The impact obliterates castles, levels forests, and permanently alters the regional map into a glowing wasteland of craters.
* **The Severing of the Hour (Time Stop - 9th Circle):** The flow of time freezes solid for everything and everyone in the world except yourself for 1d4 rounds. Falling arrows stop in mid-air, flames freeze like glass, and enemies stand paralyzed while you move, prepare defenses, and place yourself freely.
* **The Dread Wish (9th Circle):** You utter a single sentence that forcefully bends reality to your mortal will. The GM will grant your wish, but the price in your own vitality, mortal years, or catastrophic unforeseen consequence is absolute.

#### SPELL SUPREMACY
Choose one 1st Circle working in your grimoire. That formula is permanently burned into your mind's core. You may cast it at will without preparing it and without expending mental space.

#### SANCTUM SANCTORUM
You raise a warded stone tower or subterranean redoubt in or near Stonetop. The sanctum is perpetually guarded by unseen servants, alarm wards, and protective circles. Within your tower, you roll with advantage on all casting and research rolls.

## Page 4: Worldbuilding & Introductions

### Left Column: Worldbuilding Prompts

#### THE SHATTERED WORKING (Round 3 Worldbuilding)
*Everyone in the region remembers the day you unleashed a spell of terrifying magnitude...*

* **When you were younger, you cast an epic formula to... (choose 1):**
  * Break a bloody siege at the border river and drown the invaders in mud.
  * Seal an abyssal rift in the heart of the Great Barrows that was vomiting forth ghouls.
  * Slay a drake that was incinerating the summer barley fields.
  * Drag someone you loved back from the very threshold of Death.

* **...and the unleashed magic tore through the land, leaving behind... (choose 1):**
  * A glassified crater where the earth still hums and glows faintly at twilight.
  * A withered, dead grove where no bird sings and snow never melts in winter.
  * An unbound, nameless spirit that still whispers your true name on the north wind.
  * A rival cabal of cloaked sorcerers who swore a blood-debt to hunt down your grimoire.

* **...and to this day, you still carry with you... (choose 1):**
  * A heavy shard of green glass from the crater that is ice-cold even when thrown in a hearth.
  * A shock of pure white hair and nightmares of what you glimpsed between the stars.
  * The heavy brass key to a buried vault you swore to the gods you would never reopen.

### Right Column: The 8-Round Introduction Guide

#### Round 1: Introduce Yourself & Background
State your name, show the table your appearance, and answer your **Origin Questions**:

* **If you are The Battle-Hardened (Tenser):**
  1. What bloody siege or broken shield-wall did you survive where all your fellow apprentices died?
  2. Which warrior or militia guard in Stonetop owes their life to your blade when your magic ran dry?
  3. What deep physical wound aches in your chest whenever unnatural creatures approach?

* **If you are The Grey Sage (Elminster):**
  1. Which family hearth in Stonetop always keeps an earthen mug and a stool by the fire ready for you?
  2. What ancient, dangerous creature living in the Great Wood greets you as an old friend?
  3. What terrible disaster did you quietly steer away from the village three winters ago without anyone noticing?

* **If you are The High Magister (Mordenkainen):**
  1. What distant lord, collegiate council, or cabal still sends secret raven-letters to your window?
  2. Which elder in Stonetop bristles at your imperious manner but dares not cross you?
  3. What brewing war or supernatural incursion are you secretly preparing Stonetop to survive?

#### Round 2: Possessions & Village Livelihood
Show the table your **Priceless Grimoire** and your **Special Possessions**. Explain how you earn your keep during the three frozen months of winter (transcribing land deeds, surveying, distilling fever medicines, or tutoring youths).

#### Round 3: The Worldbuilding Tale
Answer the prompts from **The Shattered Working** (left column) and place the mark of your past magic on the regional map.

#### Rounds 4 & 5: NPC Connections (Choose 1 or 2)
1. **The Supplier:** Who in Stonetop collects tallow, goose feathers, and odd botanical roots for your studies in exchange for protective wards over their granary?
2. **The Skeptic:** Which elder in Stonetop remembers when you were just an awkward child reading by rushlight, and flatly refuses to be awed by your magic?
3. **The Midnight Visitor:** Who in the village came to your study at the dead of night begging for a forbidden charm or lethal draught—and what did you tell them?
4. **The Aspiring Apprentice:** Which youth in Stonetop follows you around, begging to learn the secrets of the quill and star-ink, despite their parents' terror?

#### Rounds 6 & 7: PC Connections (Ask the Table)
1. *Which one of you carries my spare satchel of components and swore a solemn oath never to look inside?*
2. *Which one of you stood over me with shield raised and fought like a demon while I finished chanting a formula under arrow fire?*
3. *Which one of you believes my magic is an abomination against the Danu, and watches my every move with an uneasy hand on your hilt?*
4. *Which one of you did I once drag back from the brink of supernatural ruin, leaving a faint arcane sigil on your flesh?*

#### Round 8: Establish Your Home
Place your study, cottage, ruined tower, or garret on the Stonetop village steading sheet.
