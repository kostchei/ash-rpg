**How to Awaken Cthulhu:**

&nbsp;

1. There must be a gate in Great Cthulhu's starting Area (can be abandoned or enemy-controlled).  
2. If this is first Awakening, pay 10 Power. Otherwise, pay 4 Power.  
3. Cthulhu appears in its starting Area. (Remember to gain 1 Elder Sign).

**Devour (Pre Battle):** The enemy player chooses and Eliminates one of his Monsters or Cultists in the Battle.

## **3\. Spellbook Effects (Image 3\)**

* **Y'ha Nthlei (Ongoing):** During the Gather Power Phase, if Cthulhu is in play, gain 1 Power for each enemy-controlled Gate in an ocean or sea Area.  
* **Dreams (Action: Cost 2):** Choose an Area containing an enemy's Acolyte. Your enemy must Eliminate one of his Acolytes from that Area and replace it with one from your Pool.  
* **Absorb (Pre-Battle):** If a Shoggoth is present, Eliminate one or more of your Monsters or Cultists in the Battle. For each Unit so removed, add 3 dice to the Shoggoth's Combat for that Battle.  
* **Regenerate (Post-Battle):** Assign up to 2 Kill or Pain Battle results to the same Starspawn. If 2 Kills are applied, the Starspawn is Killed. On any other combination of Kill or Pain results, the Starspawn is only Pained.  
* **Submerge (Action: Cost 1):** If Cthulhu is in an ocean or sea Area, remove him from the Map and place him on your Faction Card, along with any or all of your Units in the Area. Later, as a 0-cost Action, you may place Cthulhu, plus all accompanying Units, into any Area. This can be a land Area.  
* **Devolve (Ongoing):** After any player's Action, you may replace any number of your Acolyte Cultists on the Map with the same number of Deep Ones from your Pool.

# **OSR Campaign Strategy: Stopping Cthulhu**

To thwart the Great Cthulhu faction in an OSR campaign, players must fundamentally shift their strategy from traditional heroic warfare to asymmetric denial, containment, and surgical strikes. Because Cthulhu thrives on direct conflict and maritime dominance, conventional armies will only feed his power.

## **1\. Denying the Slaughter**&nbsp;

Cthulhu unlocks his core power by killing or devouring groups of  enemy units in a single battle.

&nbsp;

* **The Action:** Avoid pitched battles at all costs. Kings and generals must be convinced to disband massive standing armies that act as easy meat.  
* **Precognitive Evacuation:** Using divine augury or precognition, players must predict where Cthulhu intends to use the *Submerge* spell to suddenly appear in any land or sea area. By evacuating towns and abandoning forts before the tide arrives, they deny Cthulhu the combat engagements he needs to unlock his spellbooks.

## **2\. Shattering the Sunken Network**&nbsp;

Cthulhu’s territorial goal is to control 3 gates in ocean/sea areas, or simply ensure 4 ocean gates exist.

&nbsp;

* **The Action:** Aquatic dungeon crawls and magical demolition. The players must venture into submerged coastal ruins or deep-sea trenches to physically destroy these gates using supreme magical force or titanic strength (like a Storm Giant's maul).  
* **Acolyte Assassination:** If the gates are too deep to reach, players must hunt the Acolytes. Without Acolytes to control the gates and gather power, Cthulhu's economy collapses.

## **3\. Culling the Dreamers (Countering Dreams and Devolve)**

Cthulhu infiltrates via the *Dreams* spell, replacing enemy followers with his own Acolytes, and uses *Devolve* to instantly transform those Acolytes into monstrous Deep Ones.

&nbsp;

* **The Action:** Divine quarantine. Clerics and paladins must cast protective wards, *Mind Blank*, or mass blessings over the populace to block the psychic broadcast of the *Dreams* spell.  
* **Inquisition:** Players must root out and eliminate Acolyte sleeper cells in landlocked cities before a localized cult can be mutated into an ambush force of Deep Ones.

## **4\. Focused Eradication (Countering Shoggoths and Starspawn)**

* **Isolating Shoggoths:** A Shoggoth uses *Absorb* before a battle to consume acolytes and deepones, massively increasing its combat lethality. Players must use battlefield control magic (walls of stone, banishment, or forced teleportation) to separate the Shoggoths from their Acolyte and Deep One fodder, starving them of their power source.  
* **Overwhelming Starspawn:** The *Regenerate* spell allows Starspawn to downgrade lethal hits unless they take two simultaneous kill results. Plinking them with arrows or weak militia is useless. Players must use coordinated, massive burst damage—such as concentrated siege weapon fire or high-level destruction magic—to obliterate them instantly.

## **5\. Breaking the Anchor (Countering the Immortal Awakening)**

Awakening Cthulhu the first time requires a gate in his starting area and 10 Power. Once awakened, his Immortal ability allows him to return for a mere 4 Power.

&nbsp;

* **The Action:** Do not fight a war of attrition against the god himself. Killing Cthulhu is a temporary setback that only costs his cult 4 Power to reverse. Players must bypass the god and destroy the gates that act as his summoning anchors. Without a gate on the board to emerge from, the cheap resummoning cost is useless.
