## 🐉 Featured Monsters

=== "Giant Trapdoor Spider"

    <div class="stat-block" markdown>
    ### Giant Trapdoor Spider
    *Large beast, unaligned*

    * **Armor Class:** 13 (Natural Armor)
    * **Hit Points:** 18 (3d8 + 6)
    * **Speed:** 30 ft., climb 30 ft.
    * **Morale:** 7 (2d6) | **XP:** 65
    * **Senses:** Blindsight 30 ft. (vibrations), Infravision 60 ft.
    * **Attacks:**
        * **Bite:** Melee (Close), +4 to hit, 1d8 + 2 piercing damage + DC 12 CON save or poisoned (paralyzed for 1d4 rounds).
    * **Special Traits:**
        * **Ambush Hunter:** Advantage on stealth checks when concealed beneath its camouflaged earthen trapdoor lid.
        * **Web Tether:** Can drag a paralyzed medium or small victim into its burrow as a free action.
    </div>

    #### 📚 Monsternomicon Lore DCs
    * **Common (DC 9):** Digs deep vertical burrow pits lined with silk; strikes suddenly when prey steps on sensory web strands.
    * **Field (DC 12):** Sensitive to vibrations; throwing heavy stones on ground can trigger a false strike.
    * **Obscure (DC 15):** Immune to non-magical web entanglements; susceptible to fire damage (double damage).
    * **Arcane (DC 18):** Its venom sac can be distilled into non-lethal knockout draughts or permanent paralyzing blade poisons.

=== "Barrow Wight"

    <div class="stat-block" markdown>
    ### Barrow Wight
    *Medium undead, lawful evil*

    * **Armor Class:** 14 (Ancient Bronze Mail)
    * **Hit Points:** 26 (4d8 + 8)
    * **Speed:** 30 ft.
    * **Morale:** 10 (2d6) | **XP:** 120
    * **Senses:** Infravision 90 ft.
    * **Damage Resistances:** Cold, Lightning; Non-magical weapons (half damage unless silvered).
    * **Damage Immunities:** Poison, Necrotic.
    * **Attacks:**
        * **Ancient Bronze Longsword:** Melee (Close), +4 to hit, 1d8 + 2 slashing damage.
        * **Life Drain:** Melee (Close), +4 to hit, 1d6 necrotic damage. Target must pass a **DC 13 CON check** or have their maximum HP reduced by the damage dealt until completing a full sanctuary rest. A target reduced to 0 maximum HP dies and rises as a wight minion at the next sunset.
    </div>

    #### 📚 Monsternomicon Lore DCs
    * **Common (DC 9):** Cursed warriors entombed in ancient barrows who hunger for the living warmth of mortals.
    * **Field (DC 12):** Non-magical and non-silver weapons glance off their dried skin; silver blades bite deeply.
    * **Obscure (DC 15):** Holy water burns their flesh like boiling acid (2d6 extra radiant damage); vulnerable to turn undead.
    * **Arcane (DC 18):** The wight's dried heart contains preserved tomb-dust capable of enchanting weapons with life-stealing necrotic energy.

=== "Owlbear"

    <div class="stat-block" markdown>
    ### Owlbear
    *Large monstrosity, unaligned*

    * **Armor Class:** 13 (Thick Hide & Feathers)
    * **Hit Points:** 42 (5d10 + 15)
    * **Speed:** 40 ft.
    * **Morale:** 9 (2d6) | **XP:** 200
    * **Senses:** Infravision 60 ft., Keen Scent & Sight (Advantage on Perception).
    * **Attacks (Multiattack - 2 Attacks):**
        * **Beak:** Melee (Close), +6 to hit, 1d10 + 4 piercing damage.
        * **Claws:** Melee (Close), +6 to hit, 2d6 + 4 slashing damage. If both attacks hit the same target, the Owlbear executes a **Crushing Hug** dealing an additional 1d8 bludgeoning damage.
    </div>

    #### 📚 Monsternomicon Lore DCs
    * **Common (DC 9):** A terrifying hybrid predator combining the ferocity of a grizzly bear with the nocturnal vision and razor beak of a giant owl.
    * **Field (DC 12):** Extremely territorial; will fight to the death to protect its nest or young.
    * **Obscure (DC 15):** Loud booming noises or bright flashes of sudden fire force a Morale check at Disadvantage.
    * **Arcane (DC 18):** Its owl-feather quills and optical lenses can be synthesized into lenses of true seeing or enchanted bows of silent flight.
