## 📜 Expedition Log Archive

=== "Session 01: The Barrow in the Fen"

    * **In-Game Date:** 14th of Highsun, Year 1042
    * **Expedition Route:** Hex 00 (Oakhaven) $\rightarrow$ Hex 01 (North Pass) $\rightarrow$ Hex 04 (The Mist Fen)
    * **Roster Present:** Thokk (Fighter 1), Vesper (Thief 1), Sister Morwen (Priest 1), Elowen (Magic-User 1)
    * **Torches Burned:** 3 Torches (3 hours real-time)

    ### Expedition Narrative & Turns
    1. **Turn 1–2 (Wilderness Travel):** Journeyed North through pine foothills. Alik the guide helped bypass a goblin ambush on a DC 14 navigation roll.
    2. **Turn 3–4 (Entering Hex 04):** Discovered a sunken moss-covered barrow mound flanked by standing serpent stones.
    3. **Turn 5 (Dungeon Incursion):** Vesper detected a needle trap in the ancient stone doorway (*Passive Trap Sense* succeeded DC 13). Disarmed cleanly.
    4. **Turn 6–7 (Chamber 1 - Sarcophagus Room):** 3 Skeletal Wardens burst from clay urns. Thokk held the choke point while Sister Morwen channeled *Turn Undead*, repelling 2 skeletons. Thokk crushed the remaining one with his warhammer.
    5. **Turn 8 (Loot & Clue Discovery):** Sarcophagus opened. Found **85 silver coins**, a **silver torc (value 40 gp)**, and a **black iron coin stamped with the Crowned Lich Skull** (*Clue 2 for Vector C!*).
    6. **Turn 9–10 (Safe Return):** Party retreated before midnight; returned to Oakhaven to divide loot and rest.

    ### Treasure & XP Summary
    * **Gold Acquired:** 48 gp equivalent value per party member.
    * **XP / Milestone:** 1 Lore Shard recorded; all characters advance toward Level 2.
