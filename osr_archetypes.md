# Classic Adventuring & Sword-and-Sorcery Archetypes

> *"Gold in the tombs, salt spray on the shield, steel against sorcery, and a leather-bound grimoire held close against the dungeon damp."*

These archetypes adapt iconic figures from classic 1st Edition OSR fantasy, the *Vikings* saga, and golden-age Sword & Sorcery into the Stonetop / ASH question engine.

---

## Part 1: The 1st Edition Wizard (The Magic-User)

*Not an otherworldly warlock bound to a patron, but the classic Gygaxian scholar of ink, vellum, component pouches, and dangerous curiosity. Fragile in hit dice, terrifying in preparation.*

### The Delver-Scribe *(The "Tenser" Tradition)*
*You find quiet towers suffocating. You believe true arcana is tested down in torch-lit flagstones, stepping right behind the fighters with a stout staff or blade in hand and spells ready to break an ambush.*
* **What signature utility spell or dungeoneering device** (a floating disc of force, an arcane lantern, an iron-ward) did you personally devise to haul treasure and survive underground scrapes?
* **What martial weapon** (a broadsword, short sword, or balanced cudgel) did an old mercenary teach you to swing, and why did the academic mages scoff at your "brawling"?
* **Which frontline fighter in the company** knows you won’t hesitate to drop a *Sleep* spell into a chaotic melee, trusting them to duck in time?
* **What monster skull or dungeon relic** hangs from your pack as proof that you do your research with your boots in the mud?
* **What physical scar or singed eyebrow** reminds you of a misfired scroll read in the panic of close quarters?

### The Recluse Scholar *(The "Sage" Tradition)*
*You are the settlement's indispensable man of letters, dwelling in an old stone mill or neglected tower on the outskirts. You translate barrow-runes, brew fever-draughts, and ponder the stars while everyone else works the fields.*
* **What ancient ruin, stone circle, or forgotten tomb** at the edge of the wilderness drew you to settle in this quiet backwater?
* **What rare, moldering grimoire** did you salvage from a lost vault, and what dead language is it penned in?
* **Who in the village knocks on your door at night** with a jar of fresh honey or eggs, asking you to identify a strange coin, ward off cattle-blight, or read a sealed missive?
* **What eccentric, absent-minded habit** (smoking strong pipe-weed, muttering to your owl familiar, cataloging mosses) makes townsfolk think you are harmlessly eccentric?
* **What coming planetary alignment, monster migration, or ominous sign** are you quietly tracking while the rest of the village goes about their harvest?

### The Schismatic *(The "Rary" Tradition)*
*You broke away from the High Guild or Archmage’s Conclave. You aren't a slavering cultist—you are an aloof, razor-sharp pragmatist who realized your former masters were arrogant, corrupt, or dangerously blind. You trust only what you can prove and bind.*
* **What valuable spellbook, brass astrolabe, or secret formula** did you carry off when you broke with your former order?
* **What bitter disagreement over ancient lore, magical relics, or political meddling** caused you to pack your satchel and walk out into the frontier?
* **Why do simple townsfolk lower their voices** and make warding signs against the evil eye when you walk down the village lane?
* **Which local ruler, captain, or village elder** secretly seeks your cold, unfiltered counsel, even while publicly keeping their distance?
* **Which rival scholar or bounty-hunter from your old guild** has been seen sniffing around the local taverns, asking quiet questions about your whereabouts?

## Part 2: The Warlock (The Pact-Bound)

*If you want the eerie, patron-bound occultist, this is the distinct class where power is borrowed on credit from entities that dwell beyond mortal law.*

### The Fairy-Bartered
*As a youth, you stumbled into a hollow hill or twilight glade and danced with the Fair Folk. You returned with unearthly beauty, silver in your hair, and a pact with the Court.*
* **What bizarre, whimsical taboo** (never turning right thrice, never touching cold iron with bare hands, always leaving milk on the doorstep) must you obey to keep your patron pleased?
* **What token or charm of briar, silver, or gossamer** did your otherworldly lover or lord give you as an arcane focus?
* **Who in the village lost time or was tricked by the Good Neighbors,** and why do they blame you for it?
* **What supernatural gift of glamor or charm** did you use to escape an otherwise fatal confrontation?

### The Deep-Drowned
*You should have drowned in a sunken sea-cave, bottomless tarn, or subterranean karst. Something ancient and colossal that swims beneath the dark waters kept you breathing.*
* **What does the water-spirit or leviathan demand** that you drop into deep water on each new moon?
* **What cold, brine-scented phenomenon** (drops of seawater dripping from your fingertips, salt crusting on stone) manifests whenever you call upon your patron’s power?
* **Which fisher, boatman, or riverside peasant** recognizes the signs of the deep gods upon you and treats you with terrified reverence?
* **What drowned secret or sunken wreck** has your patron commanded you to seek out on the frontier?

### The Fiend-Debtor
*You didn't stumble into power; you signed the parchment in good faith. You needed power to save your skin, avenge a blood debt, or rescue someone you loved, and you paid with your signature.*
* **What did you desperately need when you struck the bargain,** and did the fiend honor their half of the deal?
* **What burning mark or infernal brand** is hidden beneath your clothes, aching whenever holy rites are sung?
* **What minor task or errand of mischief** does your patron’s imp or familiar periodically demand of you?
* **Who in town suspect your soul is forfeit,** and how do you keep them from running you out with pitchforks?

## Part 3: The Norse & Sea-Raider Archetypes *(Inspired by "Vikings")*

---

### The Visionary Raider *(Ragnar Lothbrok)*
*The charismatic, restless freeholder who looks at the grey western waves and sees new worlds to conquer.*

#### 1. The Sunstone Navigator
* What secret tool of navigation (an Icelandic spar sunstone, an unfamiliar sea-chart, a magnetic needle) did you acquire from a foreign wanderer?
* Why do you refuse to keep raiding the same poor eastern shores like your chieftain commands?
* What bold boast did you make at the *Thing* that earned you the chieftain’s cold glare and the youth’s eager grins?
* Which loyal shipmate or shield-brother would row through a gale of spears on your word alone?
* Who in the village reports your private meetings and shipbuilding plans to the chieftain?

#### 2. Raven-Marked
* What waking dream or omen of the Allfather convinced you that your bloodline is destined for legendary renown?
* What ravens, wolves, or eagles seem to shadow your ship wherever you make landfall?
* Which village elder or skald warned you that great renown always demands the blood of those you love most?
* What foreign kingdom or city of stone towers have you seen in your visions, though you have never set foot upon its soil?

#### 3. The Chieftain-Challenger
* What insult, stolen land, or murdered kin made you swear to take the seat of power for yourself?
* Who among the older veteran warriors quietly backs your ambition, supplying you with axes and mail?
* What foreign plunder or holy relic do you display in the longhouse to prove your voyages bear gold?
* What dangerous alliance did you forge with a rival clan to secure enough oars for your voyage?

---

### The Sacred Shipwright *(Floki)*
*The eccentric artisan, wood-whisperer, and zealous guardian of the old ways.*

#### 1. The Wave-Carver
* What revolutionary innovation in keel, rib, or rudder makes your longships dance across open seas where others founder?
* How do the living trees of the sacred grove speak to you when you select the timber for a ship's dragon-prow?
* Which warrior’s life was miraculously saved in a storm because your woodwork held against the sea?
* What eccentric ritual or nervous laughter accompanies your work at the adze and chisel?

#### 2. The True Believer
* Why does the mere mention of the southern "Cross-God" and his monks fill you with visceral fury?
* What offering of your own blood did you paint upon the ship's mast to earn the favor of Njord and Thor?
* Which foreign traveler or captive did you openly threaten when they mocked the pagan stones?
* Who in the settlement is the only one you truly love and trust to soothe your wild, unquiet mind?

#### 3. The Marsh-Hermit
* Why do you live out in the damp woods or marsh away from the chatter of the village feast-hall?
* What rare craft (carving bone runes, distilling wolf-poisons, balancing throwing axes) do warriors travel miles to buy from you?
* Who in the village thinks you are half-mad or touched by the Trickster God, yet fears your curses too much to speak against you?

---

### The Shield-Breaker *(Rollo)*
*The towering berserker who carves a red path through shield-walls, forever struggling to escape the shadow of his kin.*

#### 1. The Shadowed Kinsman
* In what legendary raid did you perform the greatest feats of arms, only for your brother or companion to receive the songs and glory?
* What simmering resentment eats at you during the mead-hall feasts as you watch others take the high seat?
* Which proud warrior or shieldmaiden looks past your kin's shadow to recognize your true worth?
* What reckless wager or drunken brawl did you start to prove you bow to no man?

#### 2. The Bear-Shirt
* What battle-fury takes you when the shields lock, making you cast aside your helm and armor to fight half-naked?
* What terrible wound should have slain you, leaving only a white scar across your chest?
* What trusted friend had to drag you off a dying foe before you butchered your own comrades in the frenzy?
* Who in town stays out of your path when they see the wild light return to your eyes?

#### 3. The Foreign-Bound
* What foreign luxury (Frankish wine, Saxon mail, silver brooches) do you prize above northern horn and wool?
* What promise of lands, titles, or a noble bride across the water tempts you to turn your back on your homeland?
* Which staunch traditionalist in the warband accused you of forgetting the blood of your ancestors?

---

### The Spámaðr / The Seer
*The blind, smoke-shrouded oracle dwelling between the world of men and the hall of the gods.*

#### 1. The Blind Hearth-Oracle
* How did you lose your earthly vision, and what terrifying inner sight did the gods grant you in trade?
* What gift (a lick of the palm, a measure of mead, a silver arm-ring) must a petitioner bring before you speak their fate?
* What dark doom did you foretell for the ruler of this land that they are desperately trying to avoid?
* Who among the villagers visits your smoke-filled turf hut in secret when darkness falls?

#### 2. The Staff-Singer *(Völva)*
* What carved staff of ash or bone do you carry as the anchor of your trance-songs?
* What protective ward or binding rune did you weave into the sail of the warband’s flagship?
* Which chieftains or jarls from distant fjords send messengers to consult your visions?
* What price do the spirits extract from your body each time you call them down into the hall?

## Part 4: Sword & Sorcery Legends

---

### The Primal Reaver *(Young Conan)*
*A panther-muscled barbarian youth in the corrupt cities of civilization, laughing at doom, mocking soft priests, and trusting keen steel and iron nerve.*

#### 1. The Roof-Runner of the City of Thieves
* What impossible prize (a star-gem, an ancient tiara, a sorcerer’s golden idol) did you steal from a walled tower under the moonlight?
* What tavern wench, fence, or fencing master hid you in their cellar when the City Guard cordoned the quarter?
* What decadent luxury of the civilized south do you secretly enjoy, despite your barbarian pride?
* What master thief betrayed you to save their own skin, and how did you repay them?
* Why do civilized sorcery and priestcraft turn your stomach with revulsion?

#### 2. The Frontier Mercenary
* What fortress or siege-wall did you storm as a howling youth in the front rank of a mercenary band?
* Which mercenary captain did you serve with, and why did you part company over a matter of loot or honor?
* What foreign language, swear words, and weapons did you pick up while marching under southern banners?
* What prince or warlord learned the hard way that you cannot be bought once your blood is up?

#### 3. The Highland Exile
* What clan blood-feud or ancestral vengeance drove you down out of the misty northern crags into the southlands?
* What grim superstition or warrior-code of your people do you uphold without question?
* Who in this civilized town did you beat bloody for insulting your gods or your barbarian heritage?
* What creature of the wild crags can you stalk and slay with only a knife and cord?

---

### The Horned-Helm Dreadnought *(Gath of Baal / Death Dealer)*
*A primitive nomad warrior bound to an ancient, terrifying relic of war—a towering champion fighting against corrupt sorcerous empires.*

#### 1. The Relic-Bound
* How did the horned, impenetrable black helmet of the ancient Death God come into your possession?
* What cold, bloodthirsty instinct does the helmet awaken in your mind when enemies surround you?
* What sorcerer-king or priest of an evil empire senses your relic from across the wilderness and hunts you for it?
* Who among your tribe sees the helmet as a divine blessing, and who fears it will devour your humanity?
* What mortal vow or beloved companion keeps you anchored to your mortal soul when the slaughter begins?

#### 2. The Horse-Lord of the Wastes
* What wild stallion did you break on the plains that will allow no other rider to touch its reins?
* What invading imperial legion did your nomadic warband ambush and wipe out in the mountain passes?
* What simple, unwritten law of the wilderness clans do you enforce with your two-handed battleaxe?
* Why do the merchant caravans pay you tribute in grain and steel rather than risk your wrath?

#### 3. The Slayer of Sorcerers
* What decadent witch or serpent-priest slaughtered your kin with dark arts while you were left for dead?
* What ancient amulet, bone-talisman, or herb gives you resistance against the illusions and fire of wizards?
* How many corrupt cities have you burned to the ground in pursuit of your vengeance?
* What prevents you from ever laying down your axe and living in peace?

---

### The Bonded Scoundrels of Lankhmar *(Fafhrd and The Gray Mouser)*
*The quintessential swashbuckling duo: a giant, romantic northern skald and a diminutive, cynical ex-magician's apprentice, bound by loyalty and a knack for glorious misadventures.*

#### 1. The Skald-Swordsman *(Fafhrd)*
* Why did you abandon the icy peaks of the Cold Waste and your clan for the smog, alleys, and wine of southern metropolises?
* Where did you forge or win *Graywand*, your long two-handed blade, and what makes it your pride?
* What hopelessly romantic dream, poetic quest, or philosophical curiosity repeatedly drags you and your comrade into danger?
* What eccentric, many-eyed patron or mysterious sage (like Ningauble) periodically sends you on bizarre errands?
* Why would you cross swords with an entire city watch to defend the small rogue at your side?

#### 2. The Hedge-Mountebank *(The Gray Mouser)*
* Under what circumstances did you abandon your formal magic apprenticeship to take up rapier and lockpicks in the city alleys?
* Where did you acquire your twin blades—the slender rapier *Scalpel* and the curved dirk *Cat’s Claw*?
* What minor sliver of real street-magic or cantrip (a spark of fire, a distraction of dust, a whispered distraction) do you still remember?
* What eyeless or hooded patron (like Sheelba) demands strange tasks from you in dark alleys?
* What sharp insult or witty jest of yours turned a simple barroom argument into a three-quarter rooftop chase?

#### 3. Defiers of the Guild
* What sacred rule of the city’s powerful Thieves' Guild did you two brazenly violate?
* Which Guildmaster or assassin has placed a price on both your heads across all civilized ports?
* What secret rooftop escape route, cellar cache, or tavern hideout belongs exclusively to you two?
* What fortune in pearls, silk, or ancient gold slipped through your fingers at the end of your last escapade?
