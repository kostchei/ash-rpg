# The Gloaming (Cursed Scroll 1: Diablerie)

Gothic Mistwood, Witchcraft & Barrow Mounds (Cursed Scroll 1)
Source reference: JSON pp. 39-44 (sourced)

## Landform & Settlement
### Woodcutter Steading
- **Water Source:** Clear forest brook and timber-shored spring
- **Food Support:** Small turnip plots, trapped hare, and smoked boar
- **Purpose:** Timber harvesting and charcoal burning for nearby settlements
- **Description:** A ring of peat-thatched timber cabins surrounded by heavy sharpened palisades.

### Fen Stilt Village
- **Water Source:** Boiled rainwater cisterns and slow moss-filtered bayous
- **Food Support:** Catfish traps, wild watercress, and duck eggs
- **Purpose:** Access to rich peat beds, medicinal leeches, and rare bog moss
- **Description:** Wattle-and-daub huts elevated on cedar poles above black marsh water.

### Priory Waystation
- **Water Source:** Blessed deep limestone well
- **Food Support:** Walled herb garden, beehives, and tithe grain
- **Purpose:** Spiritual sanctuary and pilgrim hospice along the old road
- **Description:** A fortified stone chapel and guest hospice surrounded by sacred iron lanterns.

## Historical Layers
1. **Barrow Kings' Sacred Henges**: Ancient chieftains raised megalithic alignments and buried their dead in deep stone mounds.
2. **Holy Lantern Pilgrimages**: The Knights of St. Ydris blazed stone causeways and erected warding lantern posts through the mire.
3. **The Bitter Spore Rot**: A fungal blight seeped from Bittermold Keep, drowning several hamlets and corrupting livestock.
4. **Encroaching Covens and Wary Woodcutters**: The Coven of Bittermold seeks ancient stones while local herders fortify their clearings.
