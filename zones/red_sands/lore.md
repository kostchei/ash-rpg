# The Red Sands (Djurum) (Cursed Scroll 2: Red Sands)

Sun-Baked Desert, Fighting Pits & Burning Tombs (Cursed Scroll 2)
Source reference: JSON pp. 31-36 (sourced)

## Landform & Settlement
### Oasis Caravanserai
- **Water Source:** Perennial artesian spring fed by deep sandstone aquifers
- **Food Support:** Date palms, goat milk, roasted barley, and imported figs
- **Purpose:** Vital caravan water stop and camel market along the southern trace
- **Description:** Thick adobe walls surrounding deep date-palm groves and a guarded crystalline spring.

### Canyon Hermitage & Wells
- **Water Source:** Subsurface seep collected in shaded sandstone pools
- **Food Support:** Terraced squash, drought beans, and dried mutton
- **Purpose:** Shelter from blistering noon heat and defensible vantage over canyon routes
- **Description:** Adobe dwellings carved into sheer sandstone cliffs with gravity-fed rock-cut cisterns.

### Nomad Camp
- **Water Source:** Seasonal gravel-well dug into the dry wadi floor
- **Food Support:** Camel milk, dried cheese, and caravan trade goods
- **Purpose:** Seasonal pasture for grazing herds following desert rains
- **Description:** Woven goat-hair pavilions clustered around a seasonal wadi water hole.

## Historical Layers
1. **Ancient Qanat Engineers**: An older empire channeled mountain groundwater dozens of leagues through underground aqueducts.
2. **The Great Incense & Spice Boom**: Rich trade leagues built fortified way-castles and fighting pit arenas to secure trade routes.
3. **The Aqueduct Fracture**: A seismic tremor cracked the primary subterranean qanat, desiccating three settlements and drying up downstream wells.
4. **War of the Water Stewards**: Nomad tribes and city guild masters clash over the remaining active springs.
