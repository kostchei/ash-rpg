# The City of Masks (Meridia) (Cursed Scroll 6: City of Masks)

Canal Metropolis, Masked Aristocracy & Duelist Guilds (Cursed Scroll 6)
Source reference: JSON pp. 40-45, 50-65 (sourced)

## Landform & Settlement
### Meridia Canal Core
- **Water Source:** Trematora River aqueducts and deep artesian city cisterns
- **Food Support:** River barge grain imports, coastal fishing fleets, and hinterland market gardens
- **Purpose:** Imperial trading port, seat of the Duke, and cultural heart of mask-wearing society
- **Description:** A labyrinthine metropolis of marble palazzos, arched bridges, and bustling water bazaars.

### River Barge Landing
- **Water Source:** Freshwater river channel
- **Food Support:** River fish, bread from local watermills, and tavern stew
- **Purpose:** Upstream freight transfer and customs inspection before entering the city proper
- **Description:** A rustic riverside hamlet where heavy cargo barges transfer grain and wine onto canal gondolas.

### Hinterland Vineyard Steading
- **Water Source:** Hillside spring fountains and terraced stone irrigation channels
- **Food Support:** Grapes, olives, goat cheese, and estate wheat
- **Purpose:** Wine production and agricultural provisioning for the grand balls of Meridia
- **Description:** Walled stone villas surrounded by sunlit hillside vineyards and olive groves.

## Historical Layers
1. **The Fishermen's Sanctuary**: Early settlers built wooden stilt docks at the river mouth and worshipped ancient sea saints.
2. **The Grand Canal Dredging**: The first Duke engineered stone embankments, dredged eight major canals, and divided the city into eight districts.
3. **The Mask Plague and Conspiracy**: A virulent contagion led the nobility to mandate decorative persona masks, which soon hid rampant political murder.
4. **Duelists in the Shadows**: The Shroud, the Bardic College, and the Duke's Guard clash in clandestine street warfare.
