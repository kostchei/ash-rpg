# Morzomotha & Karst Deeps (Cursed Scroll 5: Dwellers in the Deep)

Subterranean Karst, Sunless Libraries & Delver Pits (Cursed Scroll 5)
Source reference: JSON pp. 26-31 (sourced)

## Landform & Settlement
### Delver Expedition Camp
- **Water Source:** Pure mineral seepage collected in clean stone basins
- **Food Support:** Imported smoked meats, iron rations, and cultivated glow-lichen paste
- **Purpose:** Base camp for charting uncharted descent shafts and mining silver veins
- **Description:** A ring of sturdy canvas shelters and iron barricades anchored in a dry limestone chamber.

### Fungus Grower Grotto
- **Water Source:** Underground river overflow channel
- **Food Support:** Edible cap mushrooms, blind cave crayfish, and fermented fungus beer
- **Purpose:** Critical food production sustaining deep spelunkers and subterranean enclaves
- **Description:** Stone terraced beds nourished by bat guano and subterranean river silt.

### Chasm Tollpost
- **Water Source:** Dripping limestone flowstone flume
- **Food Support:** Toll fees paid in dried surface food and lamp oil
- **Purpose:** Control of the sole safe crossing between the upper sinkhole and the Library of Leng
- **Description:** A fortified stone redoubt guarding a rope-and-chain suspension bridge spanning a bottomless chasm.

## Historical Layers
1. **The Primordial Karst Architects**: Eons ago, blind subterranean artisans carved cyclopean arches and deep drainage siphons into the living rock.
2. **The Silver Delver Boom**: Surface mining syndicates sank deep shafts and anchored heavy bronze chains into the chasm walls.
3. **The Great Cavern Collapse**: A subterranean earthquake crushed the main descent gallery, drowning lower tunnels in black mineral water.
4. **Incursion of the Leng Scholars**: Alien librarians arrived in the deep vaults, collecting eldritch scrolls and barring access to lower archives.
