# The Black River (Cursed Scroll 4: River of Night)

Primeval Jungle, Ziggurats & Basilisk Cults (Cursed Scroll 4)
Source reference: JSON pp. 24-37 (sourced)

## Landform & Settlement
### Itzalca River Village
- **Water Source:** Rushing tributary spring filtered through volcanic basalt
- **Food Support:** River catfish, maize terraces, wild cassava, and sweet papayas
- **Purpose:** Control of prime canoe landings, fishing shallows, and obsidian blade trade
- **Description:** Thatched pole-houses perched atop ancient stone river-terraces above seasonal floods.

### Relic Expedition Base
- **Water Source:** Gravity-fed river water cleared with charcoal filters
- **Food Support:** Smoked monkey meat, traded maize, and dried rations
- **Purpose:** Staging ground for exploring overgrown stepped ziggurats deeper inland
- **Description:** A fortified palisaded camp on a raised bluff overlooking the main river confluence.

### Basalt Terrace Landing
- **Water Source:** Fast-moving clean mountain runoff
- **Food Support:** Traded dried fish, plantains, and bush meat
- **Purpose:** Mandatory portage station around roaring falls and treacherous river rapids
- **Description:** A stone quay with carved serpent bollards where river craft portage around boiling rapids.

## Historical Layers
1. **Primeval Itzalca Dynasty**: Centuries ago, a grand stone civilization built stepped pyramids, canal networks, and obsidian temples.
2. **Rise of the Basilisk Priesthood**: A serpent-worshipping priesthood seized the river temples, demanding tribute and breeding venomous beasts.
3. **Volcanic Rupture & Silt Floods**: A volcanic eruption upstream poisoned a river branch with ash and swallowed several temple complexes in mudslides.
4. **Expedition Incursions and Tribal Resistance**: Foreign adventurers enter the basin seeking lost gold while resident tribes defend their ancestral territories.
