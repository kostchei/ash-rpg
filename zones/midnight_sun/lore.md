# The Isles of Andrik (Cursed Scroll 3: Midnight Sun)

Glacial Fjords, Northern Gods & Sea Wolf Raiders (Cursed Scroll 3)
Source reference: JSON pp. 37-41 (sourced)

## Landform & Settlement
### Fjord Farmsteading
- **Water Source:** Rushing glacial melt stream and freshwater fjord springs
- **Food Support:** Hardy barley, dairy cattle, salted cod, and foraged lingonberries
- **Purpose:** Fertile volcanic soil in the valley bottom and sheltered bay for small fishing boats
- **Description:** Turf-roofed longhouses with drystone cattle byres in a sheltered coastal valley.

### Longship Haven
- **Water Source:** Cascading waterfall routed through hollowed cedar flumes
- **Food Support:** Whale meat, dried seal, imported malt, and harbor catch
- **Purpose:** Deep, wind-sheltered harbor with easy access to open sea raiding and trade lanes
- **Description:** Timber boathouses, slipways, and an earthen-rampart fort protecting sea wolf longboats.

### Sacred Thing-Mound
- **Water Source:** Holy hillside spring boiling from volcanic earth
- **Food Support:** Sacrificial mutton, mead gifts, and assembly tithes
- **Purpose:** Religious heart of the archipelago; judicial assemblies and seasonal god-feasts
- **Description:** A consecrated stone assembly circle and sanctuary hall where regional oaths are sworn.

## Historical Layers
1. **Sea-Giant Cairns and Runestones**: Primordial giant kin built cyclopean stone sea-walls and inscribed elder runes upon the cliffs.
2. **Jarl Alliances and Longship Fleets**: Rival northern jarls established fishing villages, built timber longships, and cleared lower valleys.
3. **The Winter of the Weeping Draugr**: A sunken burial ship broke open during a storm, releasing undead sea-corpses along several island bays.
4. **Blood Feud and Runic Omen**: Two rival clan heads contest harbor dues while the seers warn of an eternal auroral winter.
