# Oakhaven Borderlands (Shadowdark RPG Core / ASH Baseline)

Classic Frontier Sanctuary & Hex Wilderness (Legacy Baseline)
Source reference: ASH dc229a4 baseline (adapted)

## Landform & Settlement
### Frontier Basin Town
- **Water Source:** River Mor freshwater flow
- **Food Support:** Barley wealds and cattle enclosures
- **Purpose:** Crossroads between the coast, dwarven crags, and imperial highroad
- **Description:** Walled market settlement with stone watchtowers and river landing.

## Historical Layers
1. **Ancient Imperial Outposts**: The old Empire constructed paved highroads, stone wayposts, and border watchtowers.
2. **Frontier Settlement Boom**: Settlers and miners established Oakhaven and prospector quarries in the hills.
3. **The Karst Collapse**: A massive karst sink opened in the north, severing the deep dwarf tunnels and opening underworld incursions.
4. **Frontier Defense**: The Oakhaven Guard fortifies the basin while hiring crawlers to scout the wild hexes.
