# Diablo base item names

The CSV lists ordinary equipment base names usable for white, grey or blue items, without random affix names or bonus statistics. One row per game, item type and base name; the same name can occur in multiple games or weapon types.

Coverage:
- Diablo I: original ordinary equipment bases, using Jarulf's Guide. No mod-added equipment. Diablo I has no equippable gloves or belts.
- Diablo II: Arreat Summit's Normal, Exceptional and Elite bases, including Lord of Destruction class-specific equipment. These tiers are independent of item rarity. Socketed and ethereal variants do not add base names. This is the classic/LoD catalogue, not an audit of later Resurrected additions.
- Diablo III: Blizzard's equipment guide, including expansion and class-specific bases. The guide sometimes represents a base through its crafting recipe; only the base name is retained. Mystery gambling placeholders, anniversary named items, transmogs, sets and legendaries are excluded.
- Diablo IV: Common/Magic world-drop entries from Chuck0709's public item catalogue, whose Notes tab states 27 July 2026. Multiple-rarity world drops are included where Common or Magic is among their rarities. Named quest rewards, unavailable entries, shop cosmetics and Rare-only bases are excluded. This is a dated community-source snapshot, not a claim to exhaustive current-patch coverage. Source spelling errors for quarterstaves and inconsistent sword capitalization/spacing have been normalized. Diablo IV has no equippable belts.

Armour includes body armour, shields and other protective slots such as boots, pants, shoulders and bracers where present. Gloves, helms and belts have separate categories. Weapons include magical and ranged off-hands. Jewellery, consumables and follower tokens are excluded.

Sources appear in each CSV row. The CSV is UTF-8 with BOM and standard quoted fields for Excel/Google Sheets compatibility. Research date: 30 September 2026.
