VERSION 1 · 20 RECORDS

Canal water laps against the landing. Above it, shuttered galleries overlook the crowded quay.

Giresa brings the work to the party through Meridia City Watch. Giresa is looking for an expedition to join as a paid retainer.

Begin at Mill of the Abandoned Ritual. Overcome the Bear Brown holding the disputed sealed door of the trade route. Each section has its own holder and marked threshold. The other listed sites are additional jobs in the same region.

The local faction wants the work completed before rivals can claim its outcome. Failure leaves the claim unsettled; success gives the party a result they can bring back and defend.

The associated encounters use the listed reaction rolls. Treasure is allocated to the listed sites, but its discovery and access still need to be resolved in play. Tarot meanings are prompts; they do not add an unlisted power or compel an outcome.
