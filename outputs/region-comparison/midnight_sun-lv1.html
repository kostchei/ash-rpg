Hrafnfjord · Seeded story templates, encounters, people and treasure. Oracle meanings remain prompts. Unfilled details remain unresolved.

VERSION 1 · 20 RECORDS

Meltwater runs between the standing stones. Beyond the sheltered farms, snow still fills the mountain cuts.

Maras brings the work to the party through Sea Wolf Clans. Maras needs two fresh venom glands or rare herbs.

Begin at Caverns of the Unfinished Cult. Rescue Willow, Halfling Bard, held at the salvaged holding pen. Rescue Ruhiel, High Elf Monk, held at the unworked shuttered cell. Each section has its own holder and marked threshold. The other listed sites are additional jobs in the same region.

The local faction wants the work completed before rivals can claim its outcome. Failure leaves the claim unsettled; success gives the party a result they can bring back and defend.

The associated encounters use the listed reaction rolls. Treasure is allocated to the listed sites, but its discovery and access still need to be resolved in play. Tarot meanings are prompts; they do not add an unlisted power or compel an outcome.
