VERSION 1 · 20 RECORDS

Sea spray freezes along the landing ropes. Turf roofs and carved prows shelter beneath the basalt cliffs.

Gendry brings the work to the party through Sea Wolf Clans. Gendry secretly gathers information for a faction or local ruler.

Begin at Caverns of the Buried Beast. Examine the unworked ledger recording the trade route. Examine the rain-spoiled survey rolls recording the abandoned store. Examine the salvaged correspondence recording the district's safe passage. Each section has its own holder and marked threshold. The other listed sites are additional jobs in the same region.

The local faction wants the work completed before rivals can claim its outcome. Failure leaves the claim unsettled; success gives the party a result they can bring back and defend.

The associated encounters use the listed reaction rolls. Treasure is allocated to the listed sites, but its discovery and access still need to be resolved in play. Tarot meanings are prompts; they do not add an unlisted power or compel an outcome.
