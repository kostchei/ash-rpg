VERSION 1 · 20 RECORDS

Water falls somewhere beyond the lamplight. The stone passage carries a cold draught from the deeper galleries.

Barvin brings the work to the party through Deep Delver Guild. Barvin secretly gathers information for a faction or local ruler.

Begin at Wreck of the Quiet Trade Road. Break the overgrown lock array on the family holding, answering only to a spoken roster of names. Collect three sealed jars of witch-iron, from the salvaged reagent cells. Overcome the Elephant holding the disputed threshold of the abandoned store. Each section has its own holder and marked threshold. The other listed sites are additional jobs in the same region.

The local faction wants the work completed before rivals can claim its outcome. Failure leaves the claim unsettled; success gives the party a result they can bring back and defend.

The associated encounters use the listed reaction rolls. Treasure is allocated to the listed sites, but its discovery and access still need to be resolved in play. Tarot meanings are prompts; they do not add an unlisted power or compel an outcome.
