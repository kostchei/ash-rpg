VERSION 1 · 20 RECORDS

Rain darkens the piazza stones. Messengers cross the bridges while watchmen check the gates.

Elten brings the work to the party through Meridia City Watch. Elten is tracking an outlaw, monster or rival who has fled into the wilds.

Begin at Archive of the Quiet Beast. Rescue Bram, Human Monk, held at the disputed flooded hold. Secure Radiant, polished pearl (40 gp), in the salvaged sealed coffer of the old claim. Each section has its own holder and marked threshold. The other listed sites are additional jobs in the same region.

The job offers a way to settle a dispute over access. Taking the reward without completing the objectives leaves the route and its claim unresolved.

The associated encounters use the listed reaction rolls. Treasure is allocated to the listed sites, but its discovery and access still need to be resolved in play. Tarot meanings are prompts; they do not add an unlisted power or compel an outcome.
