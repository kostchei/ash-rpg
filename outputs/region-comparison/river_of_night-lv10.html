VERSION 1 · 20 RECORDS

Rain ticks against the broad leaves. The river carries dark silt past the landing steps.

Martor brings the work to the party through Itzalca Free Communities. Martor is searching the nearby wilderness for a family relic.

Begin at Fortress of the Barren Family. Lift the curse affecting the unworked memorial, on which wounds taken here do not close until it is lifted. Each section has its own holder and marked threshold. The other listed sites are additional jobs in the same region.

The job offers a way to settle a dispute over access. Taking the reward without completing the objectives leaves the route and its claim unresolved.

The associated encounters use the listed reaction rolls. Treasure is allocated to the listed sites, but its discovery and access still need to be resolved in play. Tarot meanings are prompts; they do not add an unlisted power or compel an outcome.
