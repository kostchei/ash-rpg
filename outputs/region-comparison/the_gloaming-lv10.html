VERSION 1 · 20 RECORDS

Mist gathers between the trees and the old boundary stones. The nearest hearth is already hidden behind the branches.

Eltor brings the work to the party through Knights of St. Ydris. Eltor is hiding from a collector or bounty hunter.

Begin at Farmstead of the Quiet Beast. Lift the curse affecting the disputed bell, on which whoever takes from it is followed home. Lift the curse affecting the abandoned family vault, on which the same hour repeats for anyone who stays past dark. Each section has its own holder and marked threshold. The other listed sites are additional jobs in the same region.

The local faction wants the work completed before rivals can claim its outcome. Failure leaves the claim unsettled; success gives the party a result they can bring back and defend.

The associated encounters use the listed reaction rolls. Treasure is allocated to the listed sites, but its discovery and access still need to be resolved in play. Tarot meanings are prompts; they do not add an unlisted power or compel an outcome.
