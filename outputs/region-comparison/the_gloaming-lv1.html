VERSION 1 · 20 RECORDS

Reeds scrape the causeway. Beyond the last worked field, a line of ancient trees hides the road.

Elga brings the work to the party through Gloaming Fen-Herders. Elga is tracking an outlaw, monster or rival who has fled into the wilds.

Begin at Caverns of the Hollow Beast. Secure Silver-and-gold circlet (40 gp), in the rain-spoiled wall niche of the lost herd. Collect three wrapped bundles of quicksalt, from the salvaged ritual stores. Secure the rain-spoiled passage, blocked by a rockfall nobody has cleared. Each section has its own holder and marked threshold. The other listed sites are additional jobs in the same region.

The job offers a way to settle a dispute over access. Taking the reward without completing the objectives leaves the route and its claim unresolved.

The associated encounters use the listed reaction rolls. Treasure is allocated to the listed sites, but its discovery and access still need to be resolved in play. Tarot meanings are prompts; they do not add an unlisted power or compel an outcome.
