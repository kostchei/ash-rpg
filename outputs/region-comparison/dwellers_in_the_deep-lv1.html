VERSION 1 · 20 RECORDS

The cavern roof disappears above the lamps. A rope crossing leads toward abandoned stone chambers.

Tirolas brings the work to the party through Librarians of Leng. Tirolas is guiding a trade caravan toward the next frontier settlement.

Begin at Caverns of the Sundered Trade Road. Secure Magic wand, 2nd-tier spell (100 gp), in the rain-spoiled pay chest of the old claim. Secure Silver-and-gold circlet (40 gp), in the unworked toll box of the district's safe passage. Each section has its own holder and marked threshold. The other listed sites are additional jobs in the same region.

The job offers a way to settle a dispute over access. Taking the reward without completing the objectives leaves the route and its claim unresolved.

The associated encounters use the listed reaction rolls. Treasure is allocated to the listed sites, but its discovery and access still need to be resolved in play. Tarot meanings are prompts; they do not add an unlisted power or compel an outcome.
