VERSION 1 · 20 RECORDS

Roots grip the old stonework. A narrow trail leaves the cultivated bank and vanishes beneath the canopy.

Ilfen brings the work to the party through Black River Guides. Ilfen secretly gathers information for a faction or local ruler.

Begin at Barrow of the Hollow Guild. Secure the abandoned stair head, blocked by a collapsed winch and its cut cable. Each section has its own holder and marked threshold. The other listed sites are additional jobs in the same region.

The local faction wants the work completed before rivals can claim its outcome. Failure leaves the claim unsettled; success gives the party a result they can bring back and defend.

The associated encounters use the listed reaction rolls. Treasure is allocated to the listed sites, but its discovery and access still need to be resolved in play. Tarot meanings are prompts; they do not add an unlisted power or compel an outcome.
