VERSION 1 · 20 RECORDS

Heat shimmers above the stone flats. Caravans gather where the last shade reaches the well.

Nevilo brings the work to the party through Burning Brothers. Nevilo is tracking an outlaw, monster or rival who has fled into the wilds.

Begin at Hideout of the Barren Trade Road. Examine the unworked records recording the lost herd. Rescue Martor, Halfling Priest, held at the disputed collapsed cut. Recover an intact Manta Ray Giant clutch in the unworked silt bank. Each section has its own holder and marked threshold. The other listed sites are additional jobs in the same region.

The job offers a way to settle a dispute over access. Taking the reward without completing the objectives leaves the route and its claim unresolved.

The associated encounters use the listed reaction rolls. Treasure is allocated to the listed sites, but its discovery and access still need to be resolved in play. Tarot meanings are prompts; they do not add an unlisted power or compel an outcome.
