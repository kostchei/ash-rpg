ZONE midnight_sun · MINIMUM ENCOUNTER CREATURE LV 10
VERSION 1 · 20 RECORDS

Meltwater runs between the standing stones. Beyond the sheltered farms, snow still fills the mountain cuts.

Alric brings the work to the party through Seers of the Northern Gods. Alric is the only survivor of a destroyed adventuring party.

Begin at Ruins of the Quiet Betrayer. Recover Ruby-eyed, gold idol, 3 Demonskin blessings (840 gp), in the disputed casket of the old claim. Recover Potion of Extirpation (360 gp), in the salvaged standard of the trade route. Each section has its own holder and marked threshold. The other listed sites are additional jobs in the same region.

The request has become a public test of the faction’s promises. Completing the objectives supplies a concrete result; abandoning them sends the dispute back to the assembly.

The associated encounters use the listed reaction rolls. Treasure is allocated to the listed sites, but its discovery and access still need to be resolved in play. Tarot meanings are prompts; they do not add an unlisted power or compel an outcome.
